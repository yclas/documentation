The AI page builder designs your site's pages for you. Describe what you want in your own words, such as *"make the
home page look more premium, with a darker hero and rounder cards"* or *"create an About page with our story and a
FAQ"*, and the AI designer builds it with your own listings, categories and sellers in it. You then edit every
section yourself, preview it on a computer, tablet and phone, and publish it when you like it.

The page builder is included in the **Starter**, **Professional** and **Marketplace** plans. It isn't part of the
free trial. It works with the **Nova** theme. Copying a design from a website link and custom code sections come with
the **Professional** and **Marketplace** plans.
{: .note}

## Open the page builder

Click **AI page builder** in the admin panel sidebar, at the top of the **AI** section. It opens the Studio, a
full-screen editor with your site in the middle. **Back to admin** returns to the panel.

If your plan doesn't include it, the same link opens the **AI** page, where the page builder card has a **See plans**
link. See [Compare plans](/compare-plans/#ai-included-every-month).

## Build with the AI designer

1. In **AI designer**, type what you'd like to change, or click one of the ideas.
2. Press Enter. The AI works on a **draft** and shows the result in the preview.
3. Ask for more changes in the same way, or change things yourself (below).
{: .steps}

To talk about one part of the page, click it first. Each section also has **Ask the AI about this section** and a
button that asks for three different versions of it, so you can pick the one you like.

Nothing you or the AI change is live until you publish. Your visitors keep seeing your current site.
{: .important}

## Change things yourself

| Tab | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Sections** | Click any section of the page to change it. Type straight into texts, drag a section to move it, or use **+** on its edge to add one. **Add section** offers ready-made sections, and blocks you saved. |
| **Style** | Colours, fonts, corners and spacing for the whole site, or **Start from a look**: a ready-made style with a whole home page laid out for you (see them all on the [Templates page](https://yclas.com/templates.html) at yclas.com). If the Yclas team has made a design just for your site, it's at the top, under **Made for your site** (see below). |
| **Pages** | Your site pages: add a **New page**, change page texts, search titles and the social share image. You can also add, rename or remove categories and locations; their addresses stay the same. |
| **History** | Earlier versions of your design. Bring any of them back. |

At the top, **Undo** and **Redo** step through your changes, and **Desktop**, **Tablet** and **Phone** switch the
preview. Press Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac) to search for any action, page or section.

Changes you make by hand don't use your AI allowance. Only requests to the AI do.
{: .tip}

## Sections worth knowing

**Add section** lists every section with a small preview. A few that are easy to miss:

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Blog posts** | Your newest [blog](/how-to-create-a-blog/) posts, with picture, title, excerpt and date, 3 to 12 of them. It shows nothing while the blog is off or has no posts. |
| **Image banner** | A wide picture shown as it is, such as a printed banner or a masthead, with its own picture for phones and an optional link. Fill in **Heading** with the words on the picture so screen readers and search engines can read them. |
| **Ribbon** | A heading on a notched ribbon in your second colour, with a band in your accent colour below it for one or two lines, such as *Business directory* over *Find local businesses*. |
| **Brand band** | A closing band in your second colour: a picture, your logo and a tagline, and a short statement on the right. |
| **Categories** | Your categories as **Tiles**, a **List**, **Chips** or **Icons**. **Icons** is a directory-style list: each category's round icon beside its name and description, in 1 to 4 columns, with an optional last item linking to every category. Switch on **Subcategory links** to show each category's subcategories as links in place of its description. The icons are your [category icons](/how-to-add-icons-to-categories/). |

## Made for your site

When the Yclas team designs a page just for your site (for example from your flyer or your old website), it appears
in **Style** under **Made for your site**. Nobody else can use it.

1. In **Style**, click the design under **Made for your site**.
2. Read what it changes and click **Use this design**.
3. Look through the draft, change anything you like, and publish.
{: .steps}

It sets your look, the sections of your pages, the page-wide code and the header and footer it was made with. It
replaces those in your draft: **Undo** brings your draft back, and you can use the design again at any time.

If the design comes with categories, they are set up when you publish: categories are renamed, added or removed to
match, with their picture icons, and your listings stay where they are. Their addresses don't change. Check them
under **Categories** in the Studio before you publish. Unpublishing the design puts your earlier categories and icons
back, except where you've changed them by hand since.
{: .note}

## Publish

1. Click **Publish**. The builder checks your draft first and lists anything to look at, such as placeholder text
   left in or a link with no address.
2. Fix what it finds, or confirm with **I checked it: publish**.
{: .steps}

Your design goes live straight away. You can also **Schedule** it for a later date, or **Share a preview**: a link
that shows your draft to anyone who has it, for 7 days, without logging in.

To go back to the standard Nova design, open **History** and click **Unpublish** on the live version. Your versions
are kept, so you can publish one again later.

Once a design is published, the theme options and settings it controls (such as the home page texts, or your site
name) show *Managed in the page builder: change it there, or publishing the design again replaces it.* Change them in
the Studio. See [Theme options](/theme-options/).
{: .note}

## Get help

Stuck on a change? **Get help** in the Studio sends your question to our team with a link that shows them your draft
and the section you have selected, for 7 days.

## Good to know

- The page builder designs Nova. If your site uses another theme, the Studio says so, and visitors see your design
  only once you [switch to Nova](/how-to-change-theme/).
- Right-to-left languages such as Arabic work: sections lay out from right to left. See
  [Right-to-left languages](/activate-right-left/).
- Some sections fill themselves with your own data: your newest or featured listings, categories, places and sellers.
- The [vehicles, property and jobs packs](/property-and-jobs-packs/) and [directory mode](/directory-mode/) add their
  own sections, such as a mortgage calculator or a vehicle search, when they're on.
