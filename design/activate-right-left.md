In Arabic, Hebrew, Persian, Urdu and other right-to-left languages, text runs from right to left and the whole
page reads that way too: the logo sits on the right, menus open to the left and the sidebar swaps sides. Turning on
right-to-left mode mirrors your theme so it feels natural to your visitors.

## Turn on right-to-left

1. Set your site's language first: in **Settings › Translations**, click **Use** next to the language (see
   [Language and translations](/how-to-change-language/)). Changing the language doesn't switch the direction by itself.
2. Go to **Design › Theme Options** and open **General**.
3. Set **Content shift right to left** to **Yes**.
4. Click **Save changes**.
5. Open your site and check the header, a category page, a listing and the post-a-listing form.
{: .steps}

To go back, set **Content shift right to left** to **No** and save.

## Which themes support it

| Theme | Right-to-left |
| --- | --- |
| **Nova** | Yes. The page direction switches and the layout mirrors, including cards, the header and breadcrumbs. |
| **Simple White**, **Auction**, **Craigslist Sale**, **Orange Impact**, **Modern Deluxe**, **Newspaper**, **Turquoise Impact**, **Pinterest Classifieds**, **reClassifieds**, **Modern Splash**, **Listings Board** | Yes, with a right-to-left version of the theme's styles. |
| **Marketplace** | The option is there, but the page layout currently stays left-to-right. Use Nova for a right-to-left site. |
| **Home Camp**, **Job Drop**, **Responsive Listings**, **Homepage Hero** | No option. |

The setting is a theme option, so it's saved with each theme. After a [theme switch](/how-to-change-theme/), turn
it on again in the new theme.
{: .note}

## Things to know

- **One direction for the whole site.** On a [multilingual site](/how-to-activate-multilingual-mode/) the direction
  doesn't change with the visitor's language: it's right-to-left for everyone or for no one. Choose it for the
  language most of your visitors use.
- **Mixed text is fine.** Listing titles with English brand names, numbers or prices display correctly inside
  right-to-left text; the browser handles the direction of each word.
- **Your own CSS and HTML.** Rules in [Custom CSS](/how-to-use-custom-css/) that use `left` and `right`, and banners
  you designed for left-to-right, may need adjusting.
- **Translations.** Right-to-left mode only changes the layout. To change or complete the words on the site, use
  **Settings › Translations**. See [Language and translations](/how-to-change-language/).
