The **Tools** page collects the housekeeping jobs of your marketplace on one screen. Open it from the sidebar of the
admin panel: **Tools**. You don't need it every day, but it is where you go when you want to bring in data from
another site or a spreadsheet, download a copy of everything, tell Google about your pages, or make a change show up
straight away.

The page has four cards and a table.

{% include figure.html src="panel/tools.webp" alt="The Tools page, with the sitemap, cache, import, export and scheduled jobs" %}

## Sitemap

The address of your sitemap, the file search engines read to find your pages, with the date it was last generated.
Copy the address into Google Search Console, and click **Generate now** if you have just added a lot of listings and
don't want to wait for the daily refresh.

Read more in [Sitemap](/sitemap-classifieds-website/).

## Cache

Your site keeps ready-made copies of things it shows often, such as the category tree and your settings, so pages
load fast. If a change doesn't show on the site yet, click **Clear cache**.

Read more in [Cache](/modify-cache-time/) and [My changes don't show up](/changes-not-showing/).

## Move your site to Yclas

Brings listings and members in from a CSV file, Osclass, Flynax, LaraClassifier, a WordPress classifieds plugin or
another Yclas site. It can also turn pasted text (posts from a Facebook group or WhatsApp chat) into listings, add
businesses from a Google Places search, and, with the property pack, import a property feed. Click **Start**, choose where you're moving from, upload the file, check a preview and run the
import. The card shows your last import, and the page lets you undo it for a week.

The **Import a country's locations** link under the card opens the import page for categories and locations; to load
a whole country's locations, use **Listings › Locations** instead. See
[Import categories and locations](/use-import-tool-categories-locations/).

Read more in [Move your site to Yclas](/how-to-migrate-osclass-to-yclas/), [Import listings from a CSV file](/how-to-import-ads/)
and [Import users from a CSV file](/how-to-import-users/), [Import businesses from Google](/import-businesses-from-google/)
and [Import from a property feed](/property-pack/#import-from-a-property-feed).

## Export everything

**Build the export** makes one ZIP of your listings, users, categories, locations, custom fields, pages and blog
posts, plus photos, to download. It never includes passwords or secrets.

Read more in [Back up, export or close your site](/export-site/#export-everything).

## Scheduled jobs

The table at the bottom lists the jobs your site runs on its own: refreshing the sitemap, emailing sellers whose
listings are about to expire, sending the email digest, renewing memberships and so on. For each job you see its
schedule, when it last ran and when it runs next, and you can **Disable** or **Activate** it.

Read more in [Scheduled jobs](/how-to-set-crons/).

## What isn't on this page

A few things people look for under Tools live elsewhere:

| Export your newsletter subscribers | **Subscribers › Export CSV**. |
| See your traffic | **Analytics** — see [Analytics](/useful-statistics-about-your-advertisements/). |
