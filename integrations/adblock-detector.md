If your marketplace earns money from [banner ads](/how-to-add-banner/), such as Google AdSense, visitors with an ad
blocker don't see them, and you don't earn from their visits. The ad-block detector notices when a visitor's browser
blocks ads and shows them a message asking them to switch the blocker off for your site.

## Turn it on

1. Go to **Addons** and open **Adblock detector**. The page is called **AdBlock detector**.
2. Click **Enable**.
{: .steps}

To switch it off, open the same page and click **Disable**.

## What visitors see

Visitors with an ad blocker see a dark pop-up on your public pages:

> **Adblock detected!**
> We noticed that you may have an Ad Blocker turned on. Please be aware that our site is best experienced with Ad
> Blockers turned off.

They can close it and carry on using your site: the detector asks, it doesn't block. Visitors without an ad blocker
see nothing.

## Change the message

The title and text are part of your site's texts. To reword them, go to **Settings › Translations**, click
**Edit texts**, search for `Adblock detected` and `We noticed that you may have`, and type your own wording. See
[Language and translations](/how-to-change-language/).

A friendly, honest message works better than a stern one. For example: *Our marketplace is free thanks to a few ads.
Please consider allowing ads on this site.*
{: .tip}

## Should you use it?

Use it only if ads are a real part of your income. The message is one more thing between a visitor and the listings,
and on a marketplace that makes money from [featured listings](/how-to-create-featured-plan/) or
[memberships](/membership-plans/), it's usually better to let everyone browse undisturbed.

Some ad blockers also hide the detector itself, so not every visitor with a blocker sees the message.
