You can look after your site from your phone in two ways:

- **The admin app**: your site's own admin panel, installed on your phone like an app, with alerts when something
  needs you.
- **The Yclas owner app** at [yclas.com/app](https://yclas.com/app): one app for every site you own, to approve
  listings, handle reports, see orders and sign-ups and answer messages.

Both are on every plan and need nothing from an app store.

## Install the admin app

Open your admin panel on your phone. On the dashboard, the **Run your site from your phone** card shows the steps
for your phone. The card is there for administrators and moderators.

1. Under **1. Install the admin app**:
   - **Android, and Chrome or Edge on a computer:** tap **Install app**.
   - **iPhone or iPad:** open the panel in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
   - **Another browser:** choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen** in the browser menu.
2. Open the app from your home screen. It is called after your site, followed by *Admin*, and opens your admin
   panel full screen.
{: .steps}

Press and hold the app's icon for shortcuts to **Moderation**, **Orders** and **Messages** (on phones that show
shortcuts). The app uses your site's touch icon; see [Add to home screen](/add-to-home-screen/#before-you-start-upload-an-icon).

Installing the admin app doesn't change what visitors see. Your public site is installable only if you switch on
[Add to home screen](/add-to-home-screen/).
{: .note}

## Turn on alerts

Alerts use your site's [web push notifications](/push-notifications/), so those must be on (**Settings › General**,
**Notifications**). If they're off, the card says *Web push notifications are off for this site.*, with a
**Turn them on** link for administrators.

1. In the card, under **2. Turn on alerts on this device**, tap **Enable alerts**.
2. Allow notifications when your phone or browser asks.
{: .steps}

Alerts are turned on for each device separately: do it on every phone or computer where you want them. **Turn off
on this device** stops them there.

On an iPhone or iPad, alerts need iOS 16.4 or later, and they work only from the installed app, not from Safari.
{: .important}

The card hides itself once the app is installed and alerts are on. You can also close it with **×**.

## Choose which alerts you get

Open **Choose which alerts you get** in the card, tick the ones you want and click **Save alerts**. The same choices
are in your profile, under **Notifications** › **Site alerts**.

| Alert | When | On at first | Who can get it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Listings to approve** | A new listing waits for your approval. | Yes | Administrators and moderators |
| **Reported listings** | A visitor reports a listing. | Yes | Administrators and moderators |
| **Paid orders** | Someone pays for an order on your site. | Yes | Administrators |
| **New sign-ups** | A new user creates an account. | No | Administrators |
| **Contact form** | Someone writes to you through the contact page. | No | Administrators |

Each person on your team chooses their own alerts. You don't get an alert for something you did yourself, and a new
sign-up you add in **Users** or by importing doesn't send one.

## All your sites: the Yclas owner app

If you own several sites, or prefer one app for everything, use the owner app:

1. On your phone, go to [yclas.com/app](https://yclas.com/app).
2. Sign in with your yclas.com account, the one you use for [My sites](/my-sites/). Two-step sign-in works as usual.
3. Pick a site. Only your active sites are listed.
{: .steps}

To install it, choose **Add to Home Screen** (iPhone and iPad, in Safari) or **Install app** in your browser menu.

For each site you get:

| Tab | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Today** | New listings, sign-ups, paid orders and revenue for the last 7 days, a 14-day chart of new listings, and **Needs you**: what is waiting for you. |
| **Moderate** | New listings waiting for approval: **Approve**, or **Send back** with a reason and a note. The seller gets an email with both and can fix the listing. |
| **Reports** | Listings visitors reported: **Dismiss** the report, **Hide** the listing (off the site until you publish it again) or **Remove** it. |
| **Orders** | Your latest 30 orders, **Paid**, **Pending** or **All**. |
| **More** | **Sign-ups** (new users of the last 30 days), **Messages** (read and reply to your inbox on the site), **Alerts**, **Open full panel** and **Switch site**. |

**Open full panel** signs you into the site's admin panel for everything else.

What you do in the owner app is done as your site's administrator account with the same email address as your
yclas.com account (or, if there is none, the site's first active administrator), and each change is recorded in the
site's [Audit log](/admin-support-tools/#audit-log).

### Alerts in the owner app

1. In the owner app, open **More › Alerts**.
2. Tap **Turn on alerts on this phone** and allow notifications.
3. Under **Alerts for** your site, choose what you want: **Listings to review**, **Listing reports**, **Orders**,
   **Messages** (someone wrote through your contact page) and **New sign-ups**.
{: .steps}

**Messages** and **New sign-ups** are off at first. Choose them for each site. Each alert starts with the site's
name. At most 30 alerts an hour are sent for one site.

Once the owner app sends you a site's alerts, that site's admin app stops sending the same alerts to you, so you
don't get each one twice. Your admin app card then says *Your alerts for this site go to the Yclas owner app, so
this admin app doesn't send them again.* This only changes your own account: other administrators and moderators keep
getting their alerts from the admin app. If you mute an alert in the owner app, or have no phone with alerts on
there, the admin app sends it to you as before.
{: .important}

## Good to know

- The owner app is in English. The admin app follows your panel's language.
- Both work in the browser too. Installing them only adds the icon and opens them full screen.
