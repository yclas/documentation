With Leads on, every seller on your site gets a **Leads** page: one list of every buyer who contacted them, whatever
way they did it. The seller moves each lead along (**New**, **Contacted**, **Meeting or viewing**, **Won**, **Lost**),
keeps notes, sets a follow-up date and gets an email on the morning it is due.

Leads are part of the **Professional** and **Marketplace** plans, and of the free trial. Older plans keep them too.
{: .note}

## Switch Leads on or off

Leads are on for every seller as soon as your plan includes them. To check or change it:

1. In the admin panel sidebar, click **Leads**.
2. Tick or untick **Leads for every seller**. The page saves straight away.
{: .steps}

Unticked, nothing new is recorded and the page disappears for your sellers. Leads already recorded are kept and come
back when you tick it again.

If the box is greyed out with *Set by Yclas support for this site.*, our support team has set it for you: write to us
to change it. If your plan doesn't include Leads, the page shows **Leads is included in the Professional plan and up**
with a **See plans** link.

The admin's **Leads** page lists the admin's own leads as a seller. There is no list of every seller's leads: each
seller's leads are private to them. You see counts per member on their [timeline](/member-timeline/).
{: .tip}

## Where to find it

- **Nova theme:** **Leads** in the account menu.
- **Other themes:** a **Leads** button on the seller's **My Advertisements** page (next to the heading of **My
  listings** in Atlantic Lite).

## What becomes a lead

| Source (badge) | When |
| --- | --- |
| **Message** | A buyer starts a conversation about a listing. |
| **Profile message** | Someone writes to the seller from their profile, or sends them a first direct message. |
| **Contact form** | A buyer uses a listing's contact form on a site with messaging off. |
| **Booking request** | A buyer asks to book a listing (see [Bookings](/bookings/)). |
| **Viewing request** | A buyer asks for a property viewing (see [Property pack](/property-pack/)). |
| **Test drive request** | A buyer asks for a test drive (see [Vehicles pack](/vehicles-pack/)). |
| **Quote request** | A visitor sends a business a quote request (see [Directory mode](/directory-mode/#quote-requests)). |
| **Trade-in offer** | A buyer offers a trade-in on a vehicle's contact form. |
| **Phone tap**, **WhatsApp tap** | A visitor taps a listing's phone or WhatsApp button, if you switched this on (below). |
| **Valuation request** | A homeowner asks *What's my home worth?* on a property site, and the request goes to this seller. |

Some things are not new leads:

- **The same buyer asking again.** If the same buyer (same account or same email address) writes again about the same
  listing within 60 days, while the lead is still open, the lead's history says *Asked again* instead. A trade-in
  offered in an open lead's conversation adds *Trade-in offered* to its history and a **Trade-in** badge.
- **A seller writing to their own listing**, for example to test it.
- **Messages in a dispute** about an order.

### Phone and WhatsApp taps

On the admin's **Leads** page, tick **Phone and WhatsApp taps as leads**. A tap then shows as a lead with the
listing and the time, once a day per visitor. Visitors stay anonymous, signed in or not, so a tap lead has no name or
contact details. It is off until you tick it.

### Paid quote requests

If you charge businesses for each quote request, a lead that isn't paid for yet shows **Contact details locked** and
*Pay for this quote request to see who sent it: the link is in the email you got.* Once the business pays, the name,
contact details and message appear, and the history says *Contact details unlocked*.

## The Leads page

At the top, four numbers: **Open**, **Follow up today**, **Won** and **Win rate** (won leads out of won and lost). A
**Won value** card per currency adds up what the won leads were worth.

Below them, a tab for each list: **Open** (the default: new, contacted and meeting), **Follow up today**, one per
status, and **All**. Leads with a follow-up date of today or earlier have a yellow edge and a **Follow up today**
badge. A lead whose listing was marked sold has a **Listing sold** badge, and its history says so, so the seller can
mark one buyer **Won**.

Each lead shows the buyer's name, email and phone, the listing, the date it arrived and the buyer's message, with
these buttons:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Reply** | Opens the conversation in the seller's messages (message leads only). |
| **Email** | Opens an email to the buyer, with the listing in the subject. |
| **Call** | Calls the buyer's phone. |
| **WhatsApp** | Opens WhatsApp. Only for a number written with its country code (starting with + or 00). |
| **Write a reply with AI** | Drafts a reply for the seller to edit (see [AI reply drafts](#ai-reply-drafts)). |

Clicking **Reply**, **Email**, **Call** or **WhatsApp** on a new lead moves it to **Contacted**, and the history says
how. Replying in the conversation from the seller's messages does the same.

### Move a lead along

Pick the status in the list next to the lead. It saves straight away.

| Status | Follow-up date set for you |
| --- | --- |
| **New** | None. |
| **Contacted** | In 3 days. |
| **Meeting or viewing** | In 2 days. |
| **Won**, **Lost** | None. |

### Notes, follow-up and history

Click **Notes, follow-up and history** under a lead:

- **Add a note**: up to 500 characters, kept in the history.
- **Follow up on**: pick any date, or empty it for no reminder.
- **What it was worth**: a plain number, in the listing's currency. Text such as *about 14k* is refused.
- The history: the last 20 things that happened, with dates.
- **Delete lead**: removes the lead and its history for good, for example when it was spam or the buyer asked to be
  forgotten.

### Download your leads

**Download as CSV** at the top downloads up to 5,000 leads with the date, name, email, phone, listing, source,
status, follow-up date, value and message.

## Reminder emails

Once each morning, a seller gets one email if:

- a lead's follow-up date has come (*Leads to follow up today*), or
- a lead is still **New** 2 days after it arrived, with no follow-up date (*New leads waiting for a reply*). Each new
  lead is in one such email only, and only while it is less than 10 days old. Phone and WhatsApp taps aren't included.

Both kinds come together in one email, with an **Open your leads** button. A new lead never has a follow-up date of
its own, because the seller already got the enquiry's own email; reminders start with the seller's first step.

The reminder follows the **Messages** email preference: a member who switched it off doesn't get it. On a busy site,
a follow-up date that couldn't be emailed within 3 days is skipped; the lead still shows under **Follow up today**.
{: .note}

## AI reply drafts

When **Suggested replies** is on (see [AI features](/ai-features/#suggested-replies)), each lead with a message has a
**Write a reply with AI** button. AI drafts a short answer from the conversation (for a message lead) or from the
listing and what the buyer wrote. The draft opens in a box: the seller edits it, then clicks **Copy**, **Open the
conversation** or **Send by email**. Nothing is sent automatically, and the lead doesn't move until the seller replies.

The **Suggested replies** switch is offered only while messaging is on. The drafts share its monthly allowance, and
each person can ask for up to 30 a day. A lead with
no message (a phone tap, or a quote request not paid for yet) has no button.

## Privacy

- A seller sees only their own leads, and the members of their store's team (see [Team leads](/team-leads/)).
- When a member deletes their account, their name, contact details and messages are removed from other sellers'
  leads, and the leads they received as a seller are deleted.
- A member's own data download lists the leads they received and the enquiries they made.

## Good to know

- New leads are recorded only while Leads is on. Switching it on doesn't bring in older enquiries.
- Moving a lead back to **New**, or to **Won** or **Lost**, removes its follow-up date.
- **Won value** counts only leads marked **Won** that have a value.
- Every new lead can also be sent to another app with the **New lead** [webhook](/webhooks/), or to Salesforce, Zoho
  CRM or ActiveCampaign (see [CRM integrations](/crm-email-marketing/)).
