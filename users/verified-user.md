A verified badge is a small tick shown next to a member's name. It tells visitors that you, the marketplace owner,
have checked this person, for example a dealer who sent you their business registration, or a seller you've traded
with. Badges build trust, and trusted sellers get more replies.

Only you decide who gets the badge. Members can't add it to their own account.

## Switch on verified badges

The badge is a [user custom field](/users-custom-fields/) with a special name. You create it once:

1. Go to **Users** and click **User fields**.
2. Click **New field**.
3. Enter `verifiedbadge` as the **Name** (exactly like that) and choose **Checkbox** as the **Type**.
4. Give it a **Label**, for example "Verified seller". The label is what you'll see when you edit a member.
5. Switch on **Admin Privileged**, so the box only shows up for administrators.
6. Switch on **Searchable** if you want visitors to be able to list only verified members in the members directory.
7. Click **Create field**.
{: .steps}

## Verify a member

1. Go to **Users** and click **Edit** next to the member.
2. Tick the box with your badge's label.
3. Click **Submit**.
{: .steps}

To take the badge away, untick the box and click **Submit**.

## Where the badge appears

The tick, with the tooltip "Verified!", is shown next to the member's name:

- on their [public profile](/member-profiles/),
- on their listings, where the seller's name is shown,
- on blog posts they wrote,
- in the members directory.

Some older themes show it in fewer places.

## Tips

- Decide what "verified" means on your site and say so on a page, such as your FAQ: for example "we've seen an ID
  and a utility bill" or "registered business". Then apply it consistently.
- Verification is a trust signal, not a guarantee. Keep your [scam advice](/5-facts-get-scammed/) visible too.
- If a verified member's circumstances change (they close their business, or you get complaints), remove the badge
  straight away.
