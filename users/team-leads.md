A dealer or an agency rarely works alone. With store teams, the people in a store's team also work the store's
[Leads inbox](/leads/): they see its buyers, move leads along, add notes and assign each lead to one person. Each
reminder goes to the person the lead is assigned to.

Team leads need [Leads](/leads/) (Professional and Marketplace plans) and
[store teams](/storefronts/#store-teams), which come with storefronts (Marketplace plan, Nova theme).
{: .note}

## Set it up

1. Make sure **Leads for every seller** is ticked on the admin's **Leads** page (see [Leads inbox](/leads/)).
2. Switch on **Store teams** in **Settings › General › Business sellers**.
3. The store owner invites helpers from their **My store** page, under **Team** (see
   [Store teams](/storefronts/#store-teams)).
{: .steps}

Both roles, **Editor** and **Manager**, work the store's leads. While Leads is on, the role descriptions on the
invitation form say so (*works your leads*), so the owner knows the team will see buyers' contact details.

On a property site, an agency's agents are its store team members, so the same applies. Agents an agency typed in by
hand have no account and get no inbox.

## What team members see

A team member opens **Leads** as usual. Above the list, **Inbox:** shows **My leads** (their own) and the name of
each store or agency whose team they are in. Clicking the store opens its leads, titled *Leads of* and the store's
name.

| | Store owner | Team member |
| --- | --- | --- |
| See the store's leads and buyers' contact details | Yes | Yes |
| Change status, add notes, set follow-up dates and values | Yes | Yes |
| Assign leads | Yes | Yes |
| **Email**, **Call**, **WhatsApp**, **Write a reply with AI** | Yes | Yes |
| **Reply** (opens the owner's own conversation) | Yes | No |
| **Delete lead** | Yes | No |
| **Download as CSV** | Yes | No |

The history records who did what, under each person's own name.

## Assign a lead

When a store has a team, each lead has a second list next to its status: pick a person, or **Not assigned**. It saves
straight away. The lead then shows *Assigned to* and the name (or *Assigned to you*), and the history says who
assigned it.

**Assigned to me**, next to the status tabs, shows only the leads assigned to you. It works together with the status
tab you picked.

## Reminders for the team

A lead's [reminder email](/leads/#reminder-emails) goes to the person it is assigned to while they are still in the
team. An unassigned lead, or one whose assignee left the team, reminds the store owner. Each person gets one email
per store a morning, with a link straight to their own leads in that store.

## When someone leaves

When a member leaves the team or is removed, they lose access to the store's leads at once. Leads assigned to them
count as unassigned again, and their reminders go to the owner. When a member deletes their account, their
assignments are removed.
