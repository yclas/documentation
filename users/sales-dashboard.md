Sellers who can see how they're doing sell more and stay longer. The sales dashboard gives every seller on your site
one page with their numbers: what they sold, what they were paid, and how many people looked at their listings and
their profile.

## Switch it on

The dashboard is off until you switch it on:

1. Go to **Settings › General**.
2. In **Business sellers**, switch on **Sales dashboard for every seller**.
3. Click **Save changes** in the bar at the bottom.
{: .steps}

In the Nova theme, sellers then find **Sales dashboard** in the menu of their account pages (next to **My Listings**).
In other themes there's no link: tell sellers they can open it at `/oc-panel/sellerdashboard` on your site.

If the switch says *Set by Yclas support for your site*, ask us through **Support** to change it.

## What sellers see

Sellers choose the period at the top: **7 days**, **30 days** (the default), **90 days** or **365 days**.

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Sales** | **Orders**, **Items sold**, **Revenue**, **Refunded**, the **Commission** you took, **Paid out to you** and, when there is some, **Waiting to be paid**. One row per currency. |
| **Views** | **Listing views**, **Contacts**, **Profile views** and **Contacts per 100 views**, with a chart of listing and profile views per day. |
| **Top listings** | Their 10 most viewed listings in the period, with views, contacts and sales. |

**Download orders (CSV)** saves the orders of the period as a spreadsheet file: order number, date paid, listing,
quantity, amount, currency, whether it was paid or refunded, and how it was paid.

Sales count everything paid through your site for the seller's listings: [Buy Now](/pay-directly-from-ad/), accepted
[offers](/make-an-offer/) and [quotes](/request-a-quote/), [auctions](/auctions/) and the [shopping cart](/shopping-cart/). Commission and
payouts come from sales paid by card with [Stripe Connect](/stripe/#sell-for-your-members-with-stripe-connect); with
other payment methods they stay at zero.

## Good to know

- Profile views are only counted while the dashboard is on, so that figure starts on the day you switch it on.
  Listing views and contacts go back further.
- Each seller sees only their own numbers.
- [Business sellers](/business-sellers/) also have their **Business dashboard**, with bulk import and the stock feed.
  The sales dashboard is for every seller, private or business.
