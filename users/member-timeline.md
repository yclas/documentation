The member timeline is your view of one member: when they joined, what they posted, paid, reported and reviewed, in
date order, with a summary at the top. You can add private notes and tags. Tags also work as newsletter groups.

The timeline is part of Leads & CRM, on the **Professional** and **Marketplace** plans. Older plans keep it too. Only
administrators can open it, not moderators.
{: .note}

## Open a member's timeline

1. In the admin panel, go to **Users**.
2. Click **Timeline** next to the member, or open the member and click **Timeline →** in the shortcuts on the right.
{: .steps}

If your plan doesn't include Leads & CRM, the page says so instead. Tags and notes you added earlier are kept and come
back when you upgrade.

## The summary

Counts about the member:

- **Listings** per status, and **Paid orders** with the total paid.
- **Messages**: how many they sent and received, and the date of the last one. Only counts: conversations stay
  private.
- **Leads received** per status, and **Enquiries made** as a buyer (when [Leads](/leads/) have been recorded on your
  site).
- **Favorites**, **Search alerts** and whether they are subscribed to the **Newsletter**.
- **Reviews** given and received, and how many members they **Referred**.

## The timeline

Everything that happened, newest first and grouped by day: sign-up, referral, being added to your Recruit sellers
list, listings posted, published and marked sold, payments and refunds, reports they made or got, disputes, reviews,
and your notes. Many lines have an **Open →** link to the listing, order or report.

## Tags

Under **Tags**, type a tag (for example *dealer* or *vip*) and click **Add tag**. Tags you used before are suggested
as you type. Click **×** on a tag to remove it.

- Tags are saved in lower case, up to 40 characters.
- A member can have up to 20 tags.
- Only admins see tags.

### Send a newsletter to one tag

1. Go to **Email** and, in the **Newsletters** card, click **Write a newsletter**.
2. Under **Members tagged**, pick the tag. The number in brackets is how many members will get it.
3. Fill in the rest as usual and click **Send**.
{: .steps}

Only active members who agreed to receive the newsletter get it. A member who is also in a group you ticked under
**To** gets it once. **Members tagged** appears once at least one tagged member is subscribed. See
[Newsletters](/how-to-send-the-newsletter/).

## Private notes

Under **Private notes**, write a note (up to 2,000 characters) and click **Save note**. Each note shows who wrote it
and when, and appears in the timeline. **Delete** removes it.

Only admins see notes. The member never sees them, and they are not in the data a member can download. If a member
asks for their data under a privacy law, copy the notes for them by hand.
{: .warning}

When a member's account is deleted, their tags and notes are deleted with it.

## Good to know

- The timeline works while your plan includes Leads & CRM, even if you unticked **Leads for every seller**.
- The timeline shows up to 200 events and 50 listings.
