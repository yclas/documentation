The blog gives your marketplace a place for news and articles: new features, buying and selling tips, local
stories, seasonal guides. Posts are listed newest first at `/blog`, each one has its own page, and the blog has an
RSS feed that readers and other sites can follow.

A blog is also one of the easiest ways to bring visitors in from search engines. People search for questions
("how much is a used tractor worth?") long before they search for a listing, and a good post answers them on your
site. See [SEO for your marketplace](/seo-classifieds-website/) for more ideas.

## Switch the blog on

1. In the admin panel, go to **Addons**.
2. Open **Blog** and click **Enable**.
{: .steps}

**Blog** now appears in the sidebar under **Content**, and your site gets a **Blog** link. To switch the blog off
again, open it in **Addons** and click **Disable**. Your posts are kept, but `/blog` sends visitors to the home page
until you switch it back on.

## Write a post

1. Go to **Blog** in the sidebar.
2. Click **+ New post**.
3. Type the post title at the top and write the post in the editor below it.
4. Under **Publish**, switch on **Published** to put it live now, or leave it off to save a draft.
5. Click **Create post**.
{: .steps}

A new post can take a short while to appear because of the [cache](/modify-cache-time/); the confirmation message
has a **Delete cache** button if you are in a hurry.

To edit a post, click it in the **Blog** list, make your changes and click **Save changes**. Published posts have a
**View post** button. To remove one, open it and click **Delete post** (this is permanent; switching it back to a
draft hides it instead).

## Post settings

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | The post heading and the title in the blog list. |
| **Content** | The post. Use headings, lists, links, tables, images and videos from the toolbar. |
| **Published** | When off, the post is a draft: hidden from visitors and members, but you and your team can preview it. |
| **Language** | Only on [multilingual sites](/how-to-activate-multilingual-mode/): which language the post is written in. |
| **Address** | The end of the post's address, `/blog/your-address.html`. Made from the title if you leave it empty. Changing it later breaks existing links. |
| **Meta title** and **Meta description** | Appear once the post is saved, under **Search engines**. What search engines show for the post. Empty means the title and the start of the post. |

The first image in a post is used as its picture when someone shares the post on social networks, so put a good
image near the top.
{: .tip}

## What visitors see

| Address | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| `/blog` | All published posts, newest first, with page numbers when there are many. |
| `/blog?search=word` | What the **Search the blog** box shows: posts whose title or text contains the word (at least three letters). |
| `/blog/post-address.html` | A single post, with links to the previous and next post. |
| `/rss/blog.xml` | The RSS feed of your posts. |

### The Blog link

While you haven't set up your own [menu](/modify-top-menu/), the Nova theme puts **Blog** in the top menu by
itself. Once you build your own menu, add a link to `/blog` there if you want to keep it. The footer link stays
either way.

### Blogs on multilingual sites

On a [multilingual site](/how-to-activate-multilingual-mode/), visitors see only the posts written in the language
they are browsing in. The **Blog** list in the admin panel has a language menu (**All languages** or one language)
so you can check what each audience sees. A post opened in another language falls back to the British English
version if one exists with the same address.

## Comments on posts

Posts have no comment box of their own, but you can add one from Disqus or Facebook. See
[Comments on listings and posts](/how-to-activate-comments-with-disqus/).

## Ideas for your first posts

- How to write a listing that sells, with a checklist.
- How to stay safe when buying and selling (link it from your [scam advice](/5-facts-get-scammed/)).
- A short story about a happy buyer or seller.
- A guide to the most popular category on your site, linking to it.
