A Frequently Asked Questions page saves you from answering the same emails again and again: how to post, how to
pay, how to contact a seller, how to delete an account. Members find the answer themselves, any time of day.

Your FAQ lives at `/faq`. It lists every published question in the order you choose; in the Nova theme each
question opens to show its answer, and a **Still stuck?** box at the end sends members to your contact page. Each
question also has its own page, `/faq/question-address.html`, that you can link to from emails or other pages, and
`/faq?search=word` finds the questions that mention a word.

## Switch the FAQ on

1. In the admin panel, go to **Addons**.
2. Open **Faq** and click **Enable**.
{: .steps}

**FAQ** now appears in the sidebar under **Content**, and your site gets an **FAQ** link. When the FAQ is on, the
contact page also shows a **Read the FAQ first** box above the contact form, which cuts down on messages.

## Add a question

1. Go to **FAQ** in the sidebar.
2. Click **+ New question**.
3. Type the question at the top, then write the answer in the editor below it.
4. Under **Publish**, switch on **Published**.
5. Click **Create question**.
{: .steps}

To edit an answer, click the question in the list, change it and click **Save changes**. To remove it, open it and
click **Delete question**; to hide it for a while, switch **Published** off instead.

### Question settings

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | The question, written the way a member would ask it. |
| **Content** | The answer. You can use links, lists, images and videos. |
| **Published** | Drafts are not shown on your site. |
| **Language** | Which language the question is written in. |
| **Address** | The end of the question's own address, `/faq/your-address.html`. Made from the question if you leave it empty. |
| **Meta title** and **Meta description** | Appear after the first save, under **Search engines**: what Google shows for the question's page. |

## Put the questions in order

In the **FAQ** list, drag the questions up and down. The new order is saved as soon as you drop a question, and the
FAQ page shows them in that order (in Nova, the first question is shown open). Put the questions people ask most at the top.

## FAQ in more than one language

Each question belongs to one language, and the **FAQ** list shows one language at a time (switch it with the
language menu at the top). When you open a language that has no questions yet, click **Copy from en_UK** to copy
all the British English questions across, then translate them.

On a [multilingual site](/how-to-activate-multilingual-mode/), visitors see only the questions in the language they
are browsing in. On a site with a single language, keep all questions in that language, or visitors will see
questions from every language mixed together.
{: .note}

## What makes a good FAQ

- Start from your inbox: every question you have answered twice belongs in the FAQ.
- One question per entry, with a short answer first and detail after.
- Link to the page where the member can act, such as the post-a-listing page or their profile.
- Cover the money questions: what is free, what costs money, how refunds work.
- Review it after every change to your prices, rules or settings.

## Comments under the FAQ

You can let members discuss the answers with Disqus comments under the FAQ. See
