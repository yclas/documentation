After someone posts a listing, your site shows a thank-you page. By default it says *Thanks for submitting your
listing* (in the Nova theme) with a few buttons. You can replace that heading and add your own text: what happens
next, how long review takes, tips for selling faster, or an offer to feature the listing.

## Set your own thank-you page

1. Go to **Pages** and create the page members should see, for example *Thanks for your listing!*, with your text.
   Switch on **Published** and click **Create page**. See [Pages](/how_to_add_pages/).
2. Go to **Listings › Settings**.
3. Under **Posting**, choose that page in **Thank You Page**.
4. Click **Save changes** in the bar at the bottom of the screen.
{: .steps}

Post a test listing to see it. To go back to the standard page, set **Thank You Page** back to **Deactivated**.

The page you choose is not listed in your site's footer with your other pages, since it only makes sense after
posting.
{: .note}

## What the thank-you page shows

| Part | When |
| --- | --- |
| Your page's title and text | When you've chosen a **Thank You Page**. Otherwise the standard heading. |
| **Go to Your Listing** | When listings go live straight away (moderation set to **Post directly**). |
| **Go Featured!** with the price | When members can pay to feature a listing and this one isn't featured. See [Featured listings](/how-to-create-featured-plan/). |
| **Share on Facebook** | When **Show Sharing Buttons** is on in **Listings › Settings**. |
| **My Listings** | Always: a link to the member's own listings. |

If posting costs money, members go to the payment page first instead of the thank-you page.

## What to write

Match the text to how listings go live on your site (see [Moderation](/how-ads-moderation-works/)):

- **Listings go live straight away:** congratulate them, and suggest sharing the listing or adding more photos.
- **You review listings first:** say when they can expect it to go live, for example "within 24 hours", and that
  they'll get an email.
- **Members confirm by email:** remind them to click the link in the email you've just sent, and to check their
  spam folder.

A short list of selling tips or a link to your [FAQ](/create-frequent-asked-questions-faq/) also works well.
Keep it short: the member has just finished a form.
