Public comments let visitors ask questions and discuss a listing, a blog post or an FAQ answer in the open, so the
next visitor sees the answer too. Yclas doesn't have its own comment system; it shows comments from one of two free
services:

- **Disqus**, which works on listings, blog posts and the FAQ. Visitors comment with a Disqus or social media account,
  or as guests if you allow it, and you moderate everything in your Disqus dashboard.
- **Facebook comments**, which works on listings and blog posts. Visitors comment with their Facebook account.

You can use either or both. If both are set up, both comment boxes are shown.

For private questions between buyers and sellers, use [messaging](/how-to-use-messaging-system/) instead. For
star ratings, see [Reviews and ratings](/review-system-works/).

## Add Disqus comments

1. Create a free account at disqus.com and add your site there. Choose a **shortname** (Disqus also calls it your
   website name or site ID), for example *mymarketplace*.
2. In your admin panel, go to **Integrations** and open **Disqus**.
3. Enter the shortname in the places you want comments: **Disqus for advertisements**, **Disqus for blog** and
   **Disqus for FAQ**. You can use the same shortname in all three.
4. Click **Save**.
{: .steps}

Leave a field empty to keep comments off in that place. Yclas adds the Disqus code for you; you don't need to copy
anything from Disqus.

Moderation, spam filtering, guest comments and comment notifications are all set in your Disqus dashboard, not in
Yclas.
{: .note}

## Add Facebook comments

1. Create an app at developers.facebook.com and copy its **App ID**.
2. In your admin panel, go to **Integrations** and open **Facebook**.
3. Paste the App ID into **Facebook comments**.
4. Click **Save**.
{: .steps}

Facebook comments then show under every published listing and blog post, in the language your visitor is browsing
in. Moderate them from your Facebook app's comment moderation tools.

## Where comments appear

| Where | Disqus | Facebook |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Listings | With **Disqus for advertisements** | With **Facebook comments** |
| Blog posts | With **Disqus for blog** | With **Facebook comments** |
| FAQ page and each question | With **Disqus for FAQ** | No |

Comments only show on published listings and posts. The [blog](/how-to-create-a-blog/) and
[FAQ](/create-frequent-asked-questions-faq/) must be switched on in **Addons** for their comments to appear.

## Let sellers turn comments off

You can let sellers choose whether their own listing accepts comments. Create a listing custom field with the
reserved name *commentsdisabled*; when a seller ticks it, their listing shows no comments. See
[Special-purpose fields](/special-custom-fields/).

## Tips

- Comment services load extra code from Disqus or Facebook on every page that shows comments, which slows the page a
  little. Switch them on only where you'll actually answer comments.
- Decide who answers: questions under listings are usually for the seller, who needs to be signed in to Disqus or
  Facebook to reply.
- If your site asks for [cookie consent](/cookie-consent/), remember that both services set their own cookies.
