Your members know the people most likely to join your marketplace. With referrals, every member gets their own invite
link. When a friend signs up through it and then pays for something or publishes a listing (you choose which), the
member who invited them gets a reward: credit in their wallet, or a one-time coupon.

## Switch on referrals

Referrals are off until you switch them on.

1. In the admin panel, go to **Referrals** (in the sidebar, below **Subscribers**).
2. Tick **Turn on referrals**.
3. Choose the reward settings (below).
4. Click **Save**.
{: .steps}

Only administrators see the **Referrals** page. If the switch is greyed out with *Set by Yclas support for this site*,
ask us through **Support** to change it.

## Choose the reward

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Reward when the friend** | **makes their first payment** (the default) or **publishes their first listing**. |
| **Coupon value** or **Wallet credit** | How much the reward is worth. 5 by default. |
| **The coupon takes off** | **an amount** in your currency, or **a percentage**. Not shown when the reward is wallet credit. |
| **Coupon valid for (days)** | How long the coupon can be used, from 1 to 365 days. 30 by default. |
| **Rewards per user per month, at most** | A cap per member, 10 by default. Friends beyond it are recorded but not rewarded. |

What the reward is depends on how your site takes payments; the page tells you which applies:

- With the [eWallet](/ewallet/) on, the reward is **credit in the inviter's wallet**.
- With [coupons on sales](/coupons-on-sales/) on, it is a **coupon for the shopping cart**, which comes off your
  site's fee on their next purchase.
- Otherwise it is a **coupon for your own paid extras**: a featured listing, a bump to the top, posting in a paid
  category or a membership plan.

Reward coupons are made automatically, with codes starting with `REF`, and appear in your list of
[coupons](/how-to-use-coupon-system/).

If your site doesn't charge for anything a coupon could pay for, the page warns you: the reward would be worth nothing.
Switch on paid extras in [Make money](/make-money/), or the eWallet, first.
{: .warning}

## What members see

Every member gets an **Invite friends** page with their link and a **Copy link** button. In the Nova theme it is in
the account menu; in other themes, send members to `/oc-panel/referral` on your site.

The page says what the reward is, and lists the friends who joined with the status of each. When a reward is earned,
the inviter gets an email (*You earned a reward for inviting a friend*), with the coupon code if there is one, and a
button back to their invite page.

The invite link takes the friend to your sign-up page and remembers who invited them for 30 days, so they can sign up
later.

## What counts

For the **first payment** trigger, the friend's payment counts when it is something they bought on your site: a
featured listing, a bump, a posting fee, a boost, a plan or an item bought from a seller. It doesn't count when:

- it was paid with the wallet;
- it is worth less than the reward (for an amount reward);
- the friend bought their own listing, or bought from the member who invited them.

A bank transfer or cash sale counts once you mark it as paid.

## Protection against abuse

A sign-up isn't counted (**Not counted (same person)**) when it looks like the member inviting themselves: the same
browser that opened their own invite page, the same internet address they last signed in from, the same email, or a
second friend of the same member signing up from the same address.

If the payment that earned a reward is refunded or cancelled, or the listing that earned it is deleted, the reward is
taken back while it hasn't been used: the wallet credit is removed, or the coupon is switched off.

## Follow your referrals

Under the settings, the **Referrals** page counts referrals by status and lists them, with who invited whom, when
they joined, the status and the reward:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Signed up, no reward yet** | The friend joined but hasn't paid or published yet. |
| **Rewarded** | The inviter got their reward. |
| **Not counted (same person)** | The sign-up looked like the inviter themselves. |
| **Not rewarded (monthly limit)** | The inviter had already reached the monthly cap. |
| **Reward taken back (refunded, cancelled or deleted)** | What earned the reward was undone. |
