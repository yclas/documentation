Every marketplace attracts scammers sooner or later. One bad experience can make a member leave for good and tell
their friends why, so trust is worth protecting from day one. You can't stop every scam, but you can make your site a
hard target and teach your members the warning signs.

## The scams to know about

| Scam | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Fake payment or overpayment** | A "buyer" sends a fake payment confirmation, or pays too much and asks for the difference back before their payment bounces. |
| **Advance fee** | A "seller" asks for a deposit or shipping fee for an item that doesn't exist, often priced well below market. |
| **Moving off the site** | The scammer asks to continue on WhatsApp or by email straight away, where your site can't see or stop them. |
| **Fake delivery or payment links** | A message with a link to a fake courier or payment page that steals card details or logins. |
| **Fake rentals** | A flat that isn't the scammer's, offered cheaply to someone who "can't view it, but can pay a deposit". |
| **Account takeover** | A phishing email that pretends to be from your site to get a member's password. |

## Settings that make scams harder

| What | Why it helps | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Moderation | Fake listings are caught before anyone sees them. Turn it on at least for new sellers' first listings. | [Moderation](/how-ads-moderation-works/) |
| Email verification | Members must confirm their email, so throwaway accounts are harder. | **Settings › General › Sign-ups › User Must Verify Email** |
| Blocked email domains | Stop sign-ups from disposable email services. | [Sign-up and login settings](/registration-and-login/) |
| reCAPTCHA | Stops bots from creating accounts and posting in bulk. | [reCAPTCHA](/set-recaptcha-website/) |
| Banned words | Block or replace words often used by scammers (for example "Western Union", "deposit only"). | [Blacklist and banned words](/activate-blacklist-works/) |
| Report button | Members flag suspicious listings for you to review. | [Reported listings](/flag-ad-inappropriate/) |
| On-site messaging | Conversations stay on your site, where you can see reports. | [Messaging](/how-to-use-messaging-system/) |
| Require login to contact | Only signed-in members can contact sellers. | **Listings › Settings › Listing details › Require Login to Contact** |
| Verified members | A badge for sellers you've checked. | [Verified members](/verified-user/) |
| Reviews | Members see a seller's track record. | [Reviews and ratings](/review-system-works/) |

## Publish a safety page

Create a page with safety tips and link it from your menu and footer. Members read it more than you'd think,
especially before their first deal. Go to **Pages**, create a page called "Stay safe" and adapt this text:

<div class="note" markdown="1">
**Stay safe when buying and selling**

- Meet in person, in a public place, and check the item before you pay.
- Never pay a deposit for something you haven't seen.
- Be wary of prices that are much lower than elsewhere.
- Keep your conversation on our site until you've met. Be careful if someone wants to move to WhatsApp or email
  straight away.
- Never send money back to a buyer who "paid too much", and wait until a payment has really arrived in your account.
- Don't click payment or delivery links sent in messages. We will never ask for your password or card details by
  email or message.
- Report anything suspicious with the report button on the listing.
</div>

See [Pages](/how_to_add_pages/) and [Menu](/modify-top-menu/).

## When a member reports a scam

1. Deactivate the listing straight away from **Listings › All listings**.
2. Look at the seller's other listings and messages, and mark the account as spam if it's fraudulent. See [Manage
   users](/manage-users/).
3. Add their email address or domain to the blocked list so they can't simply sign up again.
4. Reply to the member who reported it, and thank them.
{: .steps}

If money was lost, tell the member to contact their bank and the police. Keep a record of the listing and messages
in case the police ask for it.

## Protect yourself, too

Scammers also target marketplace owners:

- **Emails pretending to be Yclas** that ask for your password or payment details. Yclas support never asks for your
  password. If in doubt, sign in at yclas.com yourself instead of clicking a link.
- **"Developers" offering to fix or upgrade your site** who ask for your admin login or payment up front. Hosted Yclas
  sites are maintained by the Yclas team; you don't need anyone to install updates. For anything else, ask [Yclas
  support](/use-yclas-support-system/) first.
- **Fake buyers for your marketplace** asking you to pay a "valuation" or "transfer" fee before they buy it.
