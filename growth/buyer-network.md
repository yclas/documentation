A new or small marketplace often has a buyer searching for something it doesn't have yet. The buyer network links
Yclas sites together: when your site is in it, your published listings can be found from other Yclas sites, and every
result links straight back to the listing on your site. Buyers you would never have reached come to you.

It works both ways. When a search on your own site finds fewer than 3 listings, your site can show a few matching
listings from other sites in the network under **Also on nearby Yclas sites**, so the visitor still finds something
instead of an empty page.

The buyer network is being introduced gradually. For now Yclas support switches it on for a site; there is no switch
you can turn on yourself yet, and it is off for every site until then.
{: .note}

## Join the network

1. Click **Support** in your admin panel and ask us to add your site to the buyer network.
2. Once we've switched it on, go to **Settings › General**. A **Buyer network** section shows **Share my listings
   with the Yclas network**, switched on, with *Set by Yclas support for your site*.
3. Within a few minutes your site starts sending its published listings to the network. Large sites are sent a few
   hundred listings at a time, so it can take longer before all of them are in.
{: .steps}

The **Buyer network** section only appears on sites where Yclas support has switched the network on.

## What is shared and what isn't

Only listings that anyone can already see on your site are shared: published, and not past their expiry date or
event date. For each one the network gets:

| Shared | Not shared |
| --- | --- |
| Title and the first 500 characters of the description, as plain text | The seller's name, email address or phone number |
| Price, as your site shows it | Messages, orders, offers and bookings |
| Category and location names | Your members' accounts and details |
| Your site's country and the listing's language | Drafts, pending, sold, deactivated and expired listings |
| The first photo | Anything at all while your site is out of the network |
| The link to the listing on your site, and its publication date | |

Your site keeps the network up to date by itself:

- a new or edited listing is sent within a few minutes;
- a listing that is sold, deactivated, deleted or expires is taken out;
- every few hours your site checks the whole network copy against its own listings and fixes anything that differs,
  including listings added by a [bulk import](/how-to-import-ads/).

Every link and photo must point at your own site, so buyers always land on your listing page.

## Where buyers see your listings

- **On other Yclas sites in the network.** When a visitor's search there finds fewer than 3 listings, a box headed
  **Also on nearby Yclas sites** shows up to 6 listings from other sites, in the same language and matching the
  category, location or words searched. Each shows its photo, title, price, category, location and site name.
- **On yclas.com.** Shared listings are held in one central search on yclas.com, which also answers the searches from
  other sites. It is kept out of Google, so it never competes with your own listing pages in search results.

Clicking a result opens the listing on your site in a new tab. Buyers then contact the seller, buy or book on your
site, exactly like any other visitor. Nothing is handled on the other site or on yclas.com.

## Results from other sites on your search

While your site is in the network, your own search shows **Also on nearby Yclas sites** when it finds fewer than 3
listings, with the note *These listings are on other Yclas sites that opted in to share them. Each link opens that
site.* Your own listings always come first, and the box never shows listings from your own site.

The box shows on the search page of **Nova**, **Default** and some other built-in themes. Themes whose search page
doesn't have it, such as **Mercury**, still share their listings but don't show results from other sites.
{: .note}

## Stop sharing

Ask us through **Support** to take your site out of the network. When it's switched off:

1. Your site tells the network to forget its listings, and they disappear from other sites and from yclas.com.
2. Nothing more is sent, and **Also on nearby Yclas sites** no longer shows on your search.
{: .steps}

If your site stops being active, for example when its plan ends, its listings are taken out of the network
automatically. They are sent again once the site is active and in the network again.

## Good to know

- Search engines and bots never trigger the network box, and your search page never waits long for it: if the
  network is slow to answer, the page shows without the box.
- Results are matched by language, so a Spanish site sees Spanish listings from other sites, whatever the country.
- Listings on the network are a copy. Editing or deleting a listing always happens on your own site, and the network
  follows.
