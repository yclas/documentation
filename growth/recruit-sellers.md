Buyers come where there is something to buy, and most marketplaces got their first sellers by asking them one by
one. **Recruit sellers** is your list for that: the shops, dealers and people you'd like to see selling on your site,
the message you send them, and where each one got to, from first contact to their first listing.

Nothing is sent from your site. The WhatsApp, email and call buttons open your own apps with the message filled in,
so every invitation comes from you, one person at a time.

## Where to find it

In the admin panel, go to **Recruit sellers** in the sidebar (below **Referrals**). Only administrators see it. It's
on for every site; to switch it off, untick **Recruit sellers is on** at the bottom of the page. You can also reach it
from **Share your site** (*Inviting sellers one by one? Recruit sellers*).

## Add people to your list

Only you see this list, and nothing on it is ever published. There are three ways to fill it:

| Way | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Add one** | Type a **Name or business**, and if you have them a **Person to ask for**, a phone (with country code, for WhatsApp), an email and a website. |
| **Paste a list** | One per line: the name, then a phone, email, website or address in any order. Up to 200 lines at a time; you can paste columns from a spreadsheet too. |
| **Find businesses near you** | Type a **Kind of business** in English (*car dealers*, *plumbers*), a town or city **Near**, **Within (km)** (1 to 50) and **At most** (up to 200), then click **Find**. Businesses from open map data ([Overture Maps](/import-businesses-open-data/)) are added to your list with their phone, website and address when the data has them. They are never published, and each one uses one of your plan's monthly business records. |

Someone already on your list (the same name with the same phone, email or address) is left out, so pasting
again doesn't make duplicates. A list can hold up to 5,000 people.

## Follow each person through the pipeline

The chips at the top filter the list: **Everyone**, **Follow up today**, then one for each stage with how many people
are in it.

| Stage | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **To contact** | On your list, not contacted yet. |
| **Contacted** | You messaged or called them. |
| **Replied** | They answered. |
| **Joined** | They signed up through their personal link. |
| **First listing** | Their first listing went live. |
| **Not interested** | They said no. The message buttons go away, so you don't contact them again. |

Change a person's stage with the menu on their row. **Joined** and **First listing** are set for you (see
[Personal links](#personal-links-and-the-welcome-page)).

Using the **WhatsApp**, **Email**, **Call** or **Copy message** button moves someone from **To contact** to **Contacted**. Three days after you
contact someone, or move them to **Contacted** or **Replied**, they show up in **Follow up today**.
{: .tip}

## Send your message

Each row has buttons for that person: **WhatsApp** (when there's a phone number), **Email**, **Call**, **Copy
message**, **Copy their link** and **See their page**. The message is filled in for them, ready for you to read and
send from your own app.

Open **Your message** to change the text and the **Email subject**, then click **Save message**. These placeholders
are replaced for each person:

| Placeholder | Becomes |
| --- | --- |
| `{name}` | The person to ask for, or the business name. |
| `{site}` | Your site's name. |
| `{link}` | Their personal link. |
| `{views}` | How many times your listings were viewed in the last 30 days. |
| `{listings}` | How many listings are live on your site. |

Message one person at a time, say who you are, and stop when they say no. On WhatsApp, only message people who gave
you their number or show it for their business. Some countries need a person's consent before you email them, even
a business; when your site's language is German, Spanish or UK English, the page reminds you of the rule there.
{: .important}

### Write it with AI

On the Starter, Professional and Marketplace plans, each row also has **Write it with
AI**. It drafts a personal message for that person in your site's language, using only what your list says about
them (their name, kind of business and your notes), with their link and your site's numbers filled in. Choose a
**Friendly**, **Formal** or **Short** tone under **Your message**.

The draft appears in a box under the row: change anything you like, and the WhatsApp, email and copy buttons send
your edited text. A site gets 200 drafts a month, and up to 40 a day; past that, write the message yourself.

## Personal links and the welcome page

Every person on your list has their own link. When they open it, they see a welcome page with their name, how many
times your listings were viewed in the last 30 days and how many are live, and a short **How it works**. The button
takes them to their first listing: **Post from a photo** where [listings from a photo](/listing-from-a-photo/) are
on (AI fills in the listing, and they check it and post it themselves), otherwise **Post your first listing**. If your
site asks people to sign up before posting, the button takes them to the sign-up page first; if it doesn't, there's
also **Create an account first**. While nobody new can post (only administrators post, or your plan's listing limit
is reached), the page says new listings are paused.

- When they open the link, it's noted on their timeline.
- When they sign up within 60 days of opening it, they move to **Joined**, with a link to **their account**.
- When their first listing goes live, they move to **First listing**.

**See their page** shows you the welcome page as they'll see it, without recording anything. If you switch Recruit
sellers off, personal links go to your home page.
{: .note}

## Each person's history

Open **Edit and history** on a row to change their details, add **Notes** (the AI draft uses them), see their
timeline (contacted by WhatsApp, moved to a stage, opened their link, signed up, first listing) or **Remove** them.

To keep a copy of the whole list, click **Download as CSV** at the top of the page. The file has every person's
details, stage, times contacted, follow-up date, personal link and notes.

## Your Monday email

While someone on your list needs a follow-up, or someone joined or posted their first listing in the last week, you
get an email on Mondays with who to follow up and who joined, and a button to open your list. It goes to your site's
notification address. To stop it, untick **Email me on Mondays** at the bottom of the page.

## What buyers are looking for

Once your visitors search, the page shows what they searched for in the last 30 days:

- **Found nothing**: searches with no results. These tell you which sellers you're missing.
- **Searched most**: the words searched most often.

Searches with no results are only counted while Recruit sellers is on. For demand by category, see the **What buyers
are waiting for** card in [saved searches](/saved-searches/#see-what-buyers-are-waiting-for).
