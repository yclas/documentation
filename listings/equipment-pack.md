The equipment pack turns an ordinary classifieds site into a marketplace for machinery: diggers and tractors,
forklifts, lab and medical equipment, catering and salon equipment. Equipment listings get a **Key specs** block,
similar equipment, a spec sheet to download and the seller's own documents. Buyers can search by hours and year, see
a finance estimate and ask for a price. Dealers get stock pages, sellers can offer machines for hire, and every make
and model gets a landing page that search engines can find.

The pack works on every theme. Nova shows the most; older themes show the main parts (see
[Older themes](#older-themes)).

## Switch the pack on

The pack is **off on existing sites**. It is on for new sites created as an **Equipment and machinery** marketplace
in the sign-up wizard. There are two ways to switch it on:

- **With the equipment fields.** Go to **Listings › Custom Fields** and find **Equipment and machinery**. Choose the
  **Kind of equipment** and click **Add equipment fields**. This adds the standard fields for that kind and switches
  the pack on.
- **With the fields you already have.** Go to **Settings › General**, open **Listing types**, switch on
  **Equipment pack** (*Key specs, similar equipment, documents, finance, hire, dealer pages and landing pages on
  listings with a make, model or hour reading*) and click **Save changes**. Use the same switch to turn it off.

If the switch is greyed out with *Set by Yclas support for your site.*, ask us through
[Support](/use-yclas-support-system/).
{: .note}

The pack only changes **equipment listings**: listings with a make, a model or an hour reading filled in. Vehicle
listings are left to the [vehicles pack](/vehicles-pack/). The pack finds your fields by their names, in English,
Spanish, German, French, Dutch, Italian and Polish, so fields you created yourself work too. For example *Make*,
*Brand*, *Marca* or *Hersteller* counts as the make, and *Operating hours*, *Horas de uso* or *Betriebsstunden* as the
hours. Opening or working hours are never taken for an hour meter.

### The equipment fields

**Add equipment fields** gives every kind **Make**, **Model**, **Year**, **Condition** (new, used, refurbished, for parts
or repair) and **Serial number**, plus the fields that kind of machine needs:

| Kind of equipment | Its own fields |
| --- | --- |
| **Construction and earthmoving** | **Hours**, **Power (hp)**, **Operating weight (kg)**, **Machine type** (excavator, wheel loader, bulldozer and more) |
| **Farm machinery** | **Hours**, **Power (hp)**, **Machine type** (tractor, combine harvester, baler and more), **Drive** |
| **Forklifts and material handling** | **Hours**, **Machine type**, **Lift capacity (kg)**, **Lift height (mm)**, **Power source** |
| **Industrial, lab and medical** | **Voltage**, **Last calibration** |
| **Catering, salon and shop equipment** | **Voltage**, **Dimensions** |
| **Other equipment** | **Hours**, **Power (hp)**, **Operating weight (kg)** |

It also adds a **Price type** list (see [Price on request, or best offer and VAT](#price-on-request-or-best-offer-and-vat)).
Fields you already have, such as your own make or hours field, are kept and not added twice. None of the fields is
required, and they apply to every category: open each one and limit it to your machinery categories. You can click the
button again for another kind.

## What the pack adds

| Feature | What visitors see |
| --- | --- |
| **Key specs** | A block on the listing: make, model, year, hours and the other machinery fields the seller filled in. |
| **Similar equipment** | Other listings of the same make and model, else the same machine type. |
| **Spec sheet and flyer** | A PDF spec sheet to download and a printable A4 flyer. |
| **Documents** | The seller's brochure, manual, inspection report or service history, as PDFs. |
| **Serial checked** and **Inspection report** | Trust badges for expensive machines. |
| **Finance** | *From X / month* with a finance calculator, and optional finance and transport links. |
| **Price types** | *Or best offer*, *Price on request*, *excl. VAT* or *incl. VAT*, and a currency per listing. |
| **Hours and year search** | Search by make, model, machine type, year, hours, price and distance. |
| **Dealer pages** | A dealer's stock with filters, and a **Dealer** badge on their listings. |
| **Equipment hire** | A **For hire** badge, a price per day and a delivery note. |
| **Landing pages** | A page per make, model and location, such as `/equipment/caterpillar/320`. |
| **Listings from a photo** | The AI recognises the machine and fills in the make, model and machine type. |

### Older themes

On themes other than Nova, the **Spec sheet**, **Print flyer** and **Documents** links show above the related
listings, with the trust badges, the *From X / month* estimate and the partner links. The related listings then show
similar equipment first. Older themes already list every custom field, so they have no separate **Key specs** block.

The finance calculator, the **Equipment search** section for your pages and listings from a photo are Nova only. The
price types and the **For hire** badge show on Nova and on themes that use the standard listing pages; the **Dealer**
badge shows on the listing pages of Nova and the older built-in themes. With **Related listings** set to 0 in
**Listings › Settings**, the spec sheet, flyer and documents links still show, on their own.

## Equipment settings

Once the pack is on, **Listings › Settings** has an **Equipment** section. Click **Save** when you're done.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Listing documents** | Sellers can attach PDF documents to their listings. On until you switch it off. |
| **Finance interest rate (%/year)** and **Finance term (months)** | With a term set, equipment listings show *From X / month* and a finance calculator starting from these values. Leave the term empty to hide it. |
| **"Get finance" link** | Optional: a finance partner's page, shown on equipment listings. |
| **"Get a transport quote" link** | Optional: a transport or haulage partner's page, shown on equipment listings. |
| **Listing currencies** | Currency codes sellers can choose for their price, such as `EUR, USD, GBP`. Empty means no currency field. |

## Spec sheet, flyer and documents

Every published equipment listing gets two buttons:

- **Spec sheet**: a PDF with the title, price, main photo, key specs, the other details, the description, the seller
  and the listing's address. On sites in Arabic, Hebrew, Chinese, Japanese and other languages the PDF can't print,
  the button opens the flyer instead.
- **Print flyer**: an A4 page with a big photo, the price, the key specs and a short description, ready to print or
  save as PDF.

Sellers can also add their own **Documents**. On the edit page of their listing, they choose the **Kind** (brochure,
manual, inspection report, service history or other), an optional title and the **PDF file**, and click **Upload**.
A listing can have up to 5 PDFs of up to 10 MB each. Visitors download them from the listing. Only the seller and
you, as administrator, can add or remove them. Switch documents off with **Listing documents** in the equipment
settings.

## Trust badges, finance and paying safely

Expensive machines need trust. The pack adds:

- **Serial checked.** After you've checked a listing's serial number with the seller or the manufacturer, open the
  listing's edit page as administrator and, under **Serial number check**, click **Mark serial as checked** (with an
  optional private note). The listing shows **Serial checked** until the serial number is changed. Only admins see
  this card.
- **Inspection report.** A listing with an inspection report among its documents shows an **Inspection report** badge
  that opens it.
- **Finance.** Once you set a **Finance term**, equipment listings show *From X / month*, worked out from the price
  with no down payment. In Nova, visitors open a **Finance calculator** to change the down payment, interest rate and
  term. It is an estimate only, not an offer of credit.
- **Partner links.** **Get finance** and **Get a transport quote** link to the partners you set.
- **Pay safely.** Where [Stripe escrow](/stripe/) holds payments until the buyer confirms delivery and the listing has
  **Buy now**, it says *Pay safely: your payment is held until you confirm you have received the item.* Stripe escrow
  is on the Marketplace plan.

## Price on request, or best offer and VAT

The **Price type** field lets the seller say how the price is meant: **Fixed price**, **Or best offer**,
**Price on request**, **Excl. VAT** or **Incl. VAT**. The listing and its card then say *or best offer*, *excl. VAT* or
*incl. VAT* after the price. **Price on request** hides the price, along with **Buy now**, the cart and offers, and
puts **Request a quote** first when [quote requests](/request-a-quote/) are on. Search engines aren't given a price for
those listings either.

To let sellers price in different currencies, fill in **Listing currencies** in the equipment settings, then click
**Add equipment fields** again to add the **Currency** field. Prices are not converted: sorting and price search
compare the plain numbers.

## Search by hours and year

Search results can be filtered by the machine's hours and year, whatever your fields are called. If your plan includes
the [AI page builder](/ai-page-builder/), add the **Equipment search** section to your pages. It has a make list taken
from your live listings, a model box that suggests that make's models, the machine type, year from and to, maximum
hours, price, location and *within X km* when your site uses maps.

With [saved searches](/saved-searches/) on, the results offer **Save this search**, and alerts follow the same hours
and year filters.

## Dealers

A **dealer** is a seller with an approved [business account](/business-sellers/) on a site with the pack on.

- **Dealer page.** Their profile (or their [store](/storefronts/), in Nova) says how many machines they have in stock,
  with filters for make, model, machine type, year, maximum hours and maximum price, and a sort by newest, price, year
  or hours.
- **On their listings.** A **Dealer** badge and a *N machines in stock at …* link to their page.
- **Bulk import and stock feeds.** When the dealer maps the columns of a CSV file or feed, your equipment fields are in
  the list, marked *(equipment)*. Headings that machinery exports and dealer systems use, such as *Manufacturer*,
  *YOM*, *Hour meter* or *Serial number*, are matched automatically in the CSV import.
- **Leads.** With **Listing performance for sellers** on in **Settings › General**, the listing's **Performance**
  page sums up its calls, WhatsApp and Telegram clicks, quote requests and messages (Nova).

Business accounts are on the Professional and Marketplace plans.
{: .note}

## Equipment hire

Many equipment sites hire machines out as well as selling them. Hire uses [bookings](/bookings/), which are on the
Professional and Marketplace plans (they also work during the free trial, and sites on an older plan that's no longer
sold keep them). After the trial, on the Starter plan,
listings show no booking calendar, so machines can't be booked for hire.

1. Go to **Listings › Custom Fields** and find **Equipment and machinery**.
2. Tick **Also for hire** and choose the categories machines can be hired in.
3. Click **Add equipment fields**. This switches bookings on for those categories and adds the **Delivery available**
   and **Delivery note** fields.
{: .steps}

Sellers then make a listing bookable as usual. A bookable equipment listing shows a **For hire** badge on the listing
and its card, its price as *from 50 € / day*, with the weekly price when the seller sets a weekly discount, and
*Delivery available* with the seller's delivery note. Delivery is information only: it isn't charged in the booking.

New sites created as an equipment marketplace are asked *Selling, hiring or both?* in the sign-up wizard, and get hire
categories and bookings set up to match.
{: .tip}

## Make and model landing pages

People search for things like "used Caterpillar 320 in Texas". The pack gives your site a page for each:

| Page | Example |
| --- | --- |
| All makes | `/equipment` |
| A make | `/equipment/caterpillar` |
| A model | `/equipment/caterpillar/320` |
| With a location | `/equipment/caterpillar/texas`, `/equipment/caterpillar/320/texas` |

Each page lists the matching live listings, 24 per page. Pages with 3 or more listings go into your
[sitemap](/sitemap-classifieds-website/); pages with fewer are hidden from search engines so they don't look thin. A
page only exists while its make has live listings.

- If you already have a category whose address is `/equipment`, that page stays your category.
- While the vehicles pack is also on, the `/equipment` pages are off, as both would list the same makes.

## Listings from a photo

Where [listings from a photo](/listing-from-a-photo/) are on, the AI recognises the machine from its decals, badges or
maker's plate and fills in your make, model and machine type fields, and the year only when it can read it on the
plate. It never fills in the hours, serial number, condition, weight or price: the seller adds those.
