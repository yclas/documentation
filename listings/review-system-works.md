Reviews build trust. Buyers are more willing to deal with a seller who has a row of good ratings, and sellers have a
reason to behave well. With reviews switched on, members can give each listing one to five stars and a short text,
and every seller gets an average rating across all their listings.

## Switch reviews on

Reviews are an add-on:

1. In the admin panel, click **Addons** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Reviews**.
3. If only buyers who paid through your site should be able to review, tick **Only for paid transactions** (see below).
4. Click **Enable**.
{: .steps}

To change **Only for paid transactions** later, click **Disable**, tick or untick the box, then click **Enable** again.
Afterwards, check that the button reads **Disable**, which means reviews are on.

Once reviews are on, a **Reviews** item appears in the sidebar, and **Rating** becomes one of the sort orders you can
choose in [Publishing options](/how-to-configure-publish-options/).

## How members leave a review

1. On a listing, the member clicks **Leave a Review** (or the star rating, if the listing already has reviews).
2. On the reviews page they click **Add New Review**, choose a **Rating**, write their **Review** and solve the captcha.
3. They click **Post Review**.
{: .steps}

The rules:

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Signed in | Only signed-in members can review. |
| Not your own | Sellers can't review their own listings (*You can not review yourself.*). |
| One per listing | A member can review each listing once (*You already added a review*). |
| Length | The text must be between 5 and 1,000 characters. |
| Paid only (optional) | With **Only for paid transactions** on, only members who bought the listing through your site can review it (*You can only add a review if you bought this product*). |

The review is published straight away, and the seller gets the *ad-review* email with the rating and the text.

## Where ratings show

- On the listing, as stars with the number of reviews, linking to its reviews page.
- On the seller: their average across all their listings, shown with the seller's details on listings (Nova) and on
  their public profile.
- In search, if you or a buyer sort by **Rating**.

## Reviews only from buyers

**Only for paid transactions** makes reviews much harder to fake: a review needs a paid order for that listing. That
only works if members can actually buy through your site, with the **Buy Now** button. Set that up first:
[Let members sell with Buy Now](/pay-directly-from-ad/). Without it, nobody will be able to leave a review.

## Moderate reviews

Click **Reviews** in the sidebar to see every review, with its rating, listing, author, date and status. You can
filter by member, rating and status. Click a review to edit it; set its status to inactive to hide an unfair or
abusive review. Hidden reviews no longer appear in the list of reviews. The star averages of the listing and the
seller are recalculated the next time someone reviews that listing.

Hiding a review doesn't email anyone. If you remove a review because it broke your rules, it's good practice to tell
the author why.
