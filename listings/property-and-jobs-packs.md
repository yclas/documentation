The property and jobs packs add what a property site or a job board needs on top of ordinary listings. The
**Property pack** gives listings floor plans and a full set of property fields. The **Jobs pack** lets candidates
apply to a job with their CV and gives employers a list of everyone who applied.

Both packs are available on every plan and work with the **Nova** theme.
{: .note}

## Switch a pack on

The packs are off on existing sites. A new site created as a property or jobs marketplace in the sign-up wizard starts
with its pack on.

The quickest way to start is the pack's field template, which adds the fields and switches the pack on in one click:

1. Go to **Listings › Custom Fields**.
2. Under **Start from a template**, click **Property pack** or **Jobs pack** and confirm.
3. The new fields apply to every category. Open each one and limit it to the right categories.
{: .steps}

To switch a pack on or off without adding fields, go to **Settings › General**, open **Listing types** and use
**Property pack** or **Jobs pack**, then click **Save changes**. If a switch is greyed out with *Set by Yclas support
for your site.*, ask us through [Support](/use-yclas-support-system/).

The older **Real Estate** and **Jobs** templates are still there and haven't changed. The packs are the newer, fuller
templates.
{: .tip}

## Property pack

### The property fields

The **Property pack** template adds these fields, none of them required:

| Field | Type |
| --- | --- |
| **Type**, **For sale or rent** | Lists to choose from. |
| **Bedrooms**, **Bathrooms** | Numbers. |
| **Size**, **Size unit**, **Plot size** | Numbers, with square metres or feet. |
| **Year built**, **Energy rating**, **Floor** | A number and two lists. |
| **Features** | Tick boxes. |
| **Agency name** | Text, for estate agents. |

### Floor plans

With the pack on, the seller of a listing (or you, as administrator) sees a **Floor plan** box on the listing page
itself: **Add a floor plan image**, then **Upload**. A listing can have up to 6 floor plans, in JPEG, PNG, GIF or
WebP, within your site's [image size limit](/how-to-configure-image-settings/). Visitors see them under **Floor plan**
and click one to open it full size. The seller removes one with the **×** on it.

Floor plans are added from the listing page after posting, not from the posting form. Deleting a listing deletes its
floor plans.

### Mortgage calculator and property search

If your plan includes the [AI page builder](/ai-page-builder/), the pack adds two sections you can put on any page:

- **Property search**: a search with sale or rent and "3+ bedrooms" filters taken from your own fields.
- **Mortgage calculator**: visitors enter a home price, deposit, interest rate and term and see an estimated monthly
  payment.

## Jobs pack

### The job fields

The **Jobs pack** template adds **Contract type**, **Remote or on-site**, **Salary from**, **Salary to**,
**Salary period**, **Company** and **Company description**.

### Apply with a CV

On a job offer, candidates see **Apply for this job**. They add an optional message, attach their CV (PDF or Word
.docx, up to 5 MB) and click **Send application**. Visitors who aren't signed in also give their name and email. Your
captcha applies, as on the contact form.

A listing counts as a job offer when it has one of the job fields filled in (contract type, remote or on-site, salary,
company or job type) and it isn't in your job seekers category. The listing's own seller never sees the form.

The employer gets an email with the candidate's details and a **Download the CV** link. The CV isn't attached to the
email: it's stored privately, and only the employer, signed in, can download it, for 30 days.
{: .important}

### Job applications

Employers find every application in **Job applications**: in the account menu at the top of your site, or in the
admin panel sidebar under **Listings**. Each one shows the candidate, their message, **Reply by email** and, for 30
days, **Download CV**.

After 30 days the CV is deleted and the application stays. Applications are deleted after 12 months, and when their
listing or the candidate's account is deleted.

### Job offers and job seekers

A job board often has two sides: employers posting vacancies and people posting their profile. A jobs site from the
sign-up wizard gets two categories for this, **Job offers** and **Job seekers**, in your site's language. Listings in
the job seekers category never show the apply form.

With the [AI page builder](/ai-page-builder/), the pack also adds a **Job search** section (contract and remote
filters) and a **Post your CV** section that invites job seekers to post their profile.

## Emails

The application email uses the *user-contact* template, the same one as the contact form. Reword it in
[Email templates](/automatic-emails-sent-to-users/).

## Good to know

- The packs only change listings that use their fields. Your other categories look as they did.
- If you switch the jobs pack off, CVs already sent still expire after 30 days as usual.
- For estate agencies or recruiters who post many listings, look at [business seller accounts](/business-sellers/).
- Vehicle marketplaces have their own pack: see [Vehicle data and the vehicles pack](/vehicle-data/).
