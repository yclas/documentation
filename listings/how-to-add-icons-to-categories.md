An icon next to each category makes your home page much easier to scan: buyers spot *Cars* or *Jobs* before they
read the words. Yclas lets you give a category either an uploaded image or an icon from a built-in icon library.

## Upload an image icon

1. Go to **Listings › Categories** and click the category.
2. In **Category icon** on the right, click **Choose an image** and pick a file.
3. Click **Upload**.
{: .steps}

The preview shows the new icon straight away. To replace it, upload another file; to remove it, click
**Delete icon**.

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Shape | Square works best. Themes show icons in square tiles. |
| Format | A format allowed under **Media** (JPEG, PNG, WebP…). PNG with a transparent background looks best. |
| Size | At most 1 MB. Larger pictures are scaled down to your **Image Width** and **Image Height** from **Media**. |

## Pick an icon from the library

If you don't have images, use the **Icon Font** field instead:

1. Open the category and click in **Icon Font**.
2. Choose an icon from the list that opens.
3. Click **Submit**.
{: .steps}

When a category has both, themes use the uploaded image.

## Where icons appear

It depends on your [theme](/how-to-change-theme/):

| Theme | Where icons show |
| --- | --- |
| **Nova** | Top-level categories on the home page, as tiles. On a category page, its subcategories as tiles with their icon and number of listings (first page of results only). Categories without an icon get a generic tag icon. |
| **Mercury** | Top-level categories on the home page. On a category page, its subcategories as tiles with their icon and number of listings, on the first page and only when at least one subcategory has an icon. In lists, a listing without photos shows its category icon instead. |
| Older themes | Most show the uploaded image or the library icon next to the categories on the home page. |

The categories list in the sidebar ([Categories widget](/overview-of-widgets/)) stays text only.

Upload icons for all top-level categories, or none: a home page where half the tiles have pictures looks unfinished.
{: .tip}

## Good to know

- Icons are stored with your other site images in Yclas's cloud storage and served from there.
- If you change a category's **Seoname**, its icon is renamed with it; you don't need to upload it again.
- If a new icon doesn't show, your browser may be showing the old one: see
  [My changes don't show up](/changes-not-showing/).
- Locations can have icons too, from the **Location icon** card on a location's edit page. Only some themes show them.
