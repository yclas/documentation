A vehicle marketplace is a site where dealers and private sellers list cars, motorcycles, boats, RVs, trucks or
powersports for one town, country or niche (classic cars, camper vans, farm trucks). Yclas runs it with the
[vehicles pack](/vehicles-pack/), and the site can earn from the people who list.

This guide covers the set-up around the pack: the launch checklist, dealers, leads and ways to earn. The
[Vehicles pack](/vehicles-pack/) guide explains what listings show, and [Your vehicle fields](/vehicle-fields/) the
fields behind them.

Every tool below is off until you switch it on, unless the guide says otherwise.
{: .note}

## Start a vehicle site

1. In the sign-up wizard, choose **Vehicles** as the kind of listings.
2. Answer **Who will sell on your site?**: **Private sellers only**, **Mostly dealers**, or **Both private sellers
   and dealers**. It's optional; if you skip it, *both* is used.
3. Finish the wizard.
{: .steps}

A new vehicle site starts with:

- the vehicles pack on, the built-in make and model catalogue, and the standard vehicle fields (for the vehicle types
  your site is about, and your country's [field group](/vehicle-fields/#more-vehicle-fields) where there is one);
- **Quiet site** set to automatic (see [Quiet site](#quiet-site)), the fees note under the price label, the
  **Verified dealer badge**, walkaround videos, listing checks and contact click counts on;
- up to 20 photos per listing, as your plan allows;
- test drives off, as a one-click switch on the launch checklist.

With **Private sellers only**, dealer (business) accounts start off. You can change your answer later on the launch
checklist, but changing it never switches dealer accounts on by itself.

On an existing site, switch the pack on first: see [Switch the pack on](/vehicles-pack/#switch-the-pack-on).

## The launch checklist

On a site set up for vehicles, the dashboard shows a **Launch checklist** card with how many tools are on. Click
**Open the checklist** to see every tool for your marketplace, which ones are on, and a **Turn on** or **Turn off**
button for each. You can change your answer to **Who sells on your site?** there too. With private sellers only, the
dealer tools are left out of the list (they stay in **Settings**).

On any site with the pack on, the checklist is also on the **Solutions** page (under **Grow** in the sidebar), in the **Vehicles** group, and on the
**Vehicle pack** page. On an existing site, answering **Who sells on your site?** there adds the card to your
dashboard.

| Tool | What it does | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Seller plans** | Sellers pay a monthly plan to post more listings. | **Create plans** makes Free (3 listings), Pro seller (20) and, with dealers, Dealer (100) plans, switched off and without prices. Set the prices first. Turning plans on asks you to confirm: every seller then needs a plan to post, so keep a free plan if sellers should post without paying. |
| **Boosts and featured listings** | Sellers pay to move a listing to the top or feature it for some days. | Needs a boost package with a price in **Make money**. |
| **Banners** | Businesses buy a banner on your site by the week, and you approve each one. | |
| **Dealer accounts and dealer pages** | Dealers get a business account and their own page with their stock. | Dealers only. |
| **Dealer directory (/dealers)** | A page listing your approved dealers. | Dealers only. Turn on dealer accounts first. |
| **Stock import and feeds** | Sends your vehicles to Google vehicle ads and Facebook's car catalog; dealers can upload their stock from a file or a feed. | Dealers only. |
| **Monthly seller email** | Each seller gets a monthly email with the views, contacts and clicks their listings got. | |
| **Test drives** | Buyers ask for a test drive and the seller accepts it. | Professional plan and up. |
| **Sponsored slots** | A dealer pays to show at the top of a make's page, marked as sponsored. | Dealers only. Needs the make pages. |
| **Fee hint** | Asks sellers to include dealer or documentation fees in the price. | |
| **Still-for-sale reminders** | Asks sellers by email whether older listings are still for sale, so sold cars come down. | |
| **Parts fitment** | Sellers say which vehicles a part fits; buyers find parts for their vehicle. | See [Parts fitment](/parts-fitment/). |

Seller plans, boosts and banners need a way to get paid: connect Stripe, PayPal or another provider in **Make money**
first. If a tool shows *Set by Yclas support for your site.*, write to us to change it.
{: .note}

The checklist also shows **Suggestions** when your site is ready for the next step, for example:

- a seller with many live listings: offer them a dealer account;
- many sellers with live listings and nothing charged yet: start charging sellers;
- many live listings over 90 days old: make listings expire.

Suggestions never switch anything on by themselves.

## Quiet site

While a marketplace is small, "3 listings" and "posted 4 months ago" make it look empty. **Quiet site** hides listing
counts (category and location counts, "N listings", the live numbers on the home page, a dealer's "N vehicles in
stock") and listing dates on your public pages.

Find it in **Listings › Settings › Listing pages**:

- **Off: always show counts and dates**;
- **Automatic: hide them while the site has fewer than 20 listings** (new vehicle sites start here);
- **Always hide counts and dates**.

You still see everything in the panel. The setting shows on vehicle sites, and on any site that already uses it.

## Dealers

A **dealer** is a seller with an approved [business account](/business-sellers/). Switch business accounts on in
**Settings › General › Business sellers**, or with **Dealer accounts and dealer pages** on the launch checklist. See
[Dealers](/vehicles-pack/#dealers) for what buyers see.

### Verified dealer badge

A dealer who is also [verified](/verified-user/) can show one **Verified dealer** badge on their listings and dealer
page, instead of separate **Dealer** and **Verified** badges. Switch on **Verified dealer badge** in
**Listings › Settings › Vehicles**. It's on for new vehicle sites. Nova, the default theme, Atlantic Lite and most
other built-in themes show it.

### Dealer directory

**Dealer directory** in **Settings › General › Business sellers** gives your site a public page at `/dealers`. It
lists your business sellers that have vehicles for sale, with their logo or photo, location, stock, top makes and,
with reviews on, their rating. Buyers filter by location and make, and sort by stock or name; `/dealers/<location>`
lists one location.

- The page exists only while dealer accounts are on, at least one dealer has live vehicles, and no category of yours
  uses the address `dealers`.
- A list with fewer than 3 dealers is hidden from search engines and left out of the sitemap.
- No menu links to it by itself: add a link in your menu or pages.
- Dealer pages you prepared (below) show up only once the dealer has claimed theirs.

### Prepare dealer pages

Bring a dealer in with their page already full: you make the account, import their stock, then send them a link to
take it over. Only administrators can do this, and only on a vehicle site with business accounts on.

1. Go to **Users** › **Business** in the menu and click **Prepare dealer pages**.
2. Under **A new dealer**, enter their **Business name** and, optionally, **Their email**. Tick **Straight to the
   dealer** only if the dealer agreed to get buyer messages by email.
3. Click **Create and import stock**, and import their stock from a CSV file or set up a **Stock feed**, as a dealer
   would (see [Dealer stock import](/dealer-stock-import/)).
4. Back on **Prepare dealer pages**, click **Get claim link**, then **Copy**.
5. Send the link to the dealer yourself: by email, WhatsApp or in person.
{: .steps}

The dealer opens the link, sees *Your page is ready with N listings*, chooses their email and password, ticks that
it's their business and agrees to your terms, and clicks **Claim my page**. From then on the account and its
listings are theirs.

- A claim link works once, for 30 days. For safety it's shown only once: copy it straight away. You can make a
  **New claim link** at any time, and the old one stops working. **Revoke link** cancels it.
- Anyone with the link can take over the account until it is used, so send it only to the dealer.
- Until the dealer claims it, buyer messages and other emails for the account go to you (**You, until they claim
  it**), unless you ticked **Straight to the dealer**. Don't use any sign-in or password reset link in those emails:
  a real sign-in makes the page no longer claimable.
- Google and Apple sign-in never open an unclaimed account; the claim link is the only way in.
- An unclaimed account gets every seller plan perk (import, feed, dealer page), so your plans don't stop you. Once
  claimed, your usual plan rules apply. It shows no **Verified** badge until claimed.
- Under **An account that already exists**, you can import stock for an approved business seller who already has an
  account. They get no claim link: the account is already theirs.

### Dealers import from their own website

With **Dealers can import from their website** on, in **Settings › General › Business sellers**, a dealer can enter
the address of their own dealership website on their business dashboard (**Import from your website**) and get draft
listings of the vehicles on it.

- They must tick *I own this website or have permission to copy its listings.* Who confirmed it, and when, is kept.
- Only that website is read. Up to 25 vehicles and 10 pages per run, 3 runs a day per dealer.
- Vehicles are read from the standard vehicle details most dealer websites include. AI fills gaps only while
  [Listings from a photo](/listing-from-a-photo/) is on, and uses its monthly allowance.
- Drafts are not visible to anyone until the dealer publishes them, and your usual rules (moderation, plans) apply.
  Running it again updates its drafts and leaves the ones the dealer changed alone.

## Leads

With [Leads](/leads/) on (Professional and Marketplace plans), each seller has a Leads inbox with every buyer who
got in touch. On a vehicle site two more kinds of lead come in.

**Trade-in offers.** On a listing that accepts a trade-in, a buyer can add their own vehicle to the contact form
([Offer a trade-in](/vehicles-pack/#offer-a-trade-in)). The lead shows a **Trade-in** badge in the inbox, with the
details under the message.

**Phone and WhatsApp taps.** Many buyers call instead of writing. To count those:

1. Go to **Marketing** › **Leads** in the menu.
2. Switch on **Phone and WhatsApp taps as leads**.
3. Save.
{: .steps}

A visitor tapping a listing's phone or WhatsApp button then shows in the seller's inbox as a lead with the listing,
the channel and the time, once a day per visitor and listing. Visitors stay anonymous, signed in or not: the lead
says *A buyer*. A seller's own taps aren't counted, and the click counts on listings stay as they were.

## Marketplace health

**Marketplace health**, on the **Solutions** page in the **Vehicles** group, shows how your sellers, listings and buyers are doing, updated
about once an hour: sellers with live listings and new sellers, new listings per week, how much depends on your top
sellers, stale stock, buyer contacts per listing, and what buyers searched for and didn't find. With the vehicles pack,
it also shows your top makes by stock and the makes buyers asked for that you have none of: the dealers to invite
first.
