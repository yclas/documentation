Your members are your best moderators. A **Report** button on every listing lets them warn you about scams, spam,
duplicates or offensive content, so you can act before more people see it.

## Turn the Report button on

1. Go to **Listings › Settings**.
2. In **Listing details**, switch on **Show Report this Listing Button**.
3. Click **Save changes** in the bar at the bottom.
{: .steps}

The button appears on every published listing (in Nova next to **Save** and **Share**; older themes call it
**Report this ad**).

## What happens when someone reports a listing

1. The visitor clicks **Report**.
2. Your site's **contact page** opens, with the subject and message already filled in: *Report Ad*, the listing's ID,
   its title and its link.
3. The visitor adds their reason and clicks **Send message**, like any contact form message (with the captcha, if
   you use one).
4. You receive it by email.
{: .steps}

Reports arrive at the **Contact Email** address on the **Email** settings page, in the **Addresses** section. If that is
empty, they go to **Notify Email**. There is no separate list of reports in the admin panel, so make sure that
address is one you read. See [Email settings](/general-email-configuration/).
{: .important}

You can add a short note above the contact form, for example asking people to say why they report a listing. See
[Contact page](/how-to-add-text-contact-page/).
{: .tip}

## Handle a report

1. Open the link in the email, or search for the listing's ID in **Listings › All listings**.
2. Decide what to do:
   - **Deactivate** to take it offline while you check with the seller.
   - **Edit** to fix a wrong category or remove a phone number from the title.
   - **Spam** for spam and scams: the listing goes offline and the seller is flagged as a spammer.
   - **Delete** to remove it for good.
3. If needed, write back to the person who reported it; their name and email address are in the message.
{: .steps}

See [Manage listings](/how-to-manage-advertisements/) for what each action does.

If the same kind of listing keeps getting reported, prevention works better than cleaning up: approve listings first
with [moderation](/how-ads-moderation-works/), block words with [banned words](/activate-blacklist-works/), and see
[Fight spam](/how-to-avoid-spam-in-my-site/).
