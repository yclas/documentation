Deals let a seller put an offer on their listing: *10% off your first visit*, *Free delivery this month*, *Two for one
on Tuesdays*. Visitors see them on the listing and on a **Deals** page that collects every running offer on your site,
which gives them a reason to come back. They work well on a business directory, where shops and restaurants want to
bring customers through the door, but any seller can use them.

Deals are separate from [coupons](/how-to-use-coupon-system/), which take money off a payment on your site. A deal is
an offer the seller honours themselves, in their shop or with their own checkout.
{: .note}

Deals are off until you switch them on, and they show with the **Nova** theme.

## Switch deals on

1. Go to **Settings › General**.
2. In **Access & privacy**, switch on **Deals**.
3. Click **Save changes** in the bar at the bottom.
{: .steps}

If the switch is greyed out with *Set by Yclas support for your site.*, ask us through
[Support](/use-yclas-support-system/) to change it.

## How sellers add a deal

On their own published listing, the seller clicks **Add a deal to your listing** (or **Manage your deals** once they
have one) and fills in:

| Field | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | The offer, up to 120 characters, for example *10% off your first order*. |
| **Terms (optional)** | The small print, for example *One per customer. Not with other offers.* |
| **Code (optional)** | A code customers give the seller, such as `WELCOME10`: letters, numbers, `-` and `_`. |
| **Ends on** | The last day of the offer, at most 90 days ahead. |

They click **Add deal**. A listing can have up to 3 deals running at a time, and the seller can **Remove** one at any
time. A deal disappears by itself after its end date.

## What visitors see

- **On the listing**: a **Deals** section with each offer, its terms and its end date. A deal with a code has a
  **Show code** button: the code appears when the visitor clicks it.
- **On listing cards**: a **Deal** badge, in results and lists, while the listing has a running deal.
- **On the Deals page**: every running deal on your site, at `/deals` on your domain. Add it to your
  [menu](/modify-top-menu/) so visitors find it.

Each click on **Show code** is counted. On the seller's **Deals** page each offer shows *code shown N times*, a simple
measure of interest.

If one of your categories has the web address name `deals`, the Deals page doesn't open, so the category keeps its
address.
{: .tip}
