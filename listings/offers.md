Haggling is part of buying second-hand. With offers in chat, a buyer can offer their own price on a listing, and the
seller can accept, decline or come back with a counter-offer. It all happens inside the conversation between them, so
both sides always see where things stand, and nobody has to agree on a price in free text.

Offers use [messaging](/how-to-use-messaging-system/), so switch messaging on first. They work with the **Nova** and
**default** themes. Themes with their own conversation page don't show offers.
{: .note}

## Switch offers on

Newer sites have offers on already. To check, or to switch them on:

1. Make sure **Messaging** is on in **Addons**.
2. Go to **Settings › General**.
3. In **Messages**, switch on **Offers in chat**.
4. Click **Save changes** in the bar at the bottom.
{: .steps}

## Set the minimum offer and quick replies

Go to **Addons › Messaging**. In the **Offers** section:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Minimum offer** | The lowest offer allowed, as a percentage of the asking price. The default is 50%. Lower offers are refused with a friendly message. 0 accepts any amount. |
| **Quick replies** | Short answers sellers send with one click, such as "Yes, it is still available." One per line, up to 10. Leave it empty to use the examples. |

## How it works for buyers

On a listing with a price, the buyer clicks **Make an offer**, enters **Your offer**, adds an optional message and
clicks **Send offer**. They can also make an offer from an existing conversation about the listing. The offer
appears as a card in the conversation, with the asking price.

An offer is refused if it is:

- below your minimum offer (*Offers on this listing start at …*);
- the asking price or more (the buyer can simply contact the seller or buy it);
- on a listing without a price, or on an [auction](/auctions/).

There can be one offer waiting for an answer per conversation. The buyer can **Withdraw offer** while it waits.

## How it works for sellers

The seller answers from the offer card in the conversation:

- **Accept** the offer;
- **Decline** it;
- **Counter** with their own price (*Counter-offer*). The buyer can then accept or decline the counter-offer.

Each step adds a short line to the conversation and emails the other person. An offer nobody answers expires after
7 days.

## After an offer is accepted

| The listing can be bought online | The listing can't be bought online |
| --- | --- |
| The buyer gets a **Pay now** button for 48 hours and pays the agreed price through your site's checkout, plus the listing's shipping if it has any. | The card says to agree on pickup and payment in the conversation. |

"Bought online" means the listing has the **Buy Now** button: see [Let members sell with Buy Now](/pay-directly-from-ad/).
Paid offers appear in [Orders](/how-to-manage-orders/).

Until it is paid, either side can **Cancel offer** on an accepted offer, and then a new offer can be made.

## Good to know

- Offers are in the listing's own currency.
- If you switch offers off later, an accepted offer that is still waiting for payment stays visible and payable.
- The emails use the normal new-message notification, so there's no separate template to edit.
