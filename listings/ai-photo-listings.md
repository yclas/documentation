Writing a good listing takes time, and many sellers give up halfway. With listings from a photo, a seller takes or
picks a photo on the post page, and AI writes the listing for them: the title, a description, the category, a price
and any of your custom fields it can tell from the picture. The normal post form opens already filled in, with the
photo added. The seller checks it, fixes anything that isn't right, and posts.

Listings from a photo works with the Nova theme, the default for new marketplaces. It's included in every plan and
the free trial, with a monthly allowance that depends on your plan.

## What sellers see

1. The seller clicks **Post an ad**. At the top of the form is a box: **Create a listing from a photo**.
2. Optionally, they type what's for sale in the small box (*Optional: what's for sale?*). This helps when the photo
   shows more than the item, for example a sofa in a furnished room.
3. They click **Choose a photo** and take a picture or pick one from their device. *Writing your listing from the
   photo…* shows while AI works, usually a few seconds.
4. The form opens filled in, with the note *Filled in from your photo*, and the photo is in the photos box as the cover.
5. The seller checks every field, adds more photos if they like, and clicks **Publish ad** as usual.
{: .steps}

If the result isn't right, **Use another photo** starts again with a different picture.

**Nothing is published until the seller posts.** AI only fills in the form. Your usual rules all still apply:
[moderation](/how-ads-moderation-works/), paid categories, posting limits, the captcha and your terms.
{: .important}

## What AI fills in

| Field | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** and **Description** | Written in your site's language. |
| **Category** | Chosen from your own categories, never a new one. |
| **Price** | An estimate in your site's currency. Left empty when the item looks free or the price can't be told. On a marketplace where almost no listings show a price, the price is left empty. |
| Custom fields | Filled in where the photo makes the answer clear, such as a colour, a size or a choice from your list. Fields only admins can edit are never filled in. |

Location, contact details and the remaining photos are left for the seller, as on any listing. If AI can't tell what
the photo shows, the form still opens with the photo added and the message *We couldn't tell what it shows, so please
fill in the details below.*

AI makes mistakes: it may guess the wrong model or price. That's why the form asks sellers to *check every detail*
before they post.
{: .note}

### On vehicle, property and machinery sites

With one of the listing-type packs on, the AI does more:

- **Vehicles**: it recognises the vehicle and fills in make, model, body type, colour and fuel (the year only when it
  is sure; never mileage, VIN or condition). Nova's posting form also gets **Hide number plates**, which counts as
  one listing from a photo per click. See [Vehicles pack](/vehicles-pack/#listings-from-a-photo).
- **Property**: Nova's posting form gets **Check my photos with AI**, which captions rooms, suggests an order and blurs
  faces, house numbers and plates. Each batch of up to 6 photos counts as one. See
  [Property pack](/property-pack/#check-photos-with-ai).
- **Machinery**: it reads the make, model and machine type from decals or the maker's plate. See
  [Equipment pack](/equipment-pack/#listings-from-a-photo).

## Who can use it

Anyone who may post on your site sees the box on the post page. If you only let signed-in members post (for example
with **Require login to post**, [membership plans](/membership-plans/) or escrow payments), visitors sign in first, as they
would to post at all. The box doesn't show when only administrators may post, or when your site has reached the
number of listings your plan allows.

The box is on the page for a new listing only, not on the edit page.

## Switch it on or off

1. In the admin panel sidebar, open **AI features** and click **Listings from a photo**. This opens the **AI** page
   at its card.
2. Switch **Listings from a photo** on or off.
3. Click **Save changes** in the bar at the bottom.
{: .steps}

It's on from the start on newer marketplaces that use Nova. On older marketplaces it starts off: switch it on here
when you're ready. If your site uses another theme, the card says *Needs the Nova theme.*; see
[Choose and change your theme](/how-to-change-theme/). The switch used to be in **Settings › General**; see
[AI features and allowances](/ai-features/) for all the AI switches.

If the switch is greyed out with *Set by Yclas support for your site.*, our team has set it for you.
[Contact support](/use-yclas-support-system/) if you'd like it changed.

## Monthly allowance

| Limit | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Per marketplace | Depends on your plan: **50** a month on Starter, **100** on Professional, **500** on Marketplace, and **200** on older plans and the free trial. Counted per calendar month. |
| Per seller | **10 a day**, so one seller can't use up your site's allowance. |

The card on the [AI page](/ai-features/) shows how many your site has used this month. See
[Compare plans](/compare-plans/#ai-included-every-month) for every plan's AI allowances.

When a limit is reached, sellers see a short message (*Listings from a photo are used up on this site for this month.
Please post this one yourself.*) and simply fill in the form by hand. Posting keeps working as normal. The monthly
allowance starts again on the first day of the next month.

Listings written from pasted text in [Add listings from text](/how-to-import-ads/#no-spreadsheet-add-listings-from-text)
count against the same allowance: one per listing written.
