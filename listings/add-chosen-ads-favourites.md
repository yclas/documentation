Favourites let members save listings they are interested in and come back to them later, like a wishlist. Buyers
rarely decide on the first visit, so an easy way back to a listing brings them back to your marketplace too.

Favourites are always on; there is nothing to set up.

## How members save a listing

Signed-in members click the **heart** on a listing card or on the listing page (where it is labelled **Save**). The
heart fills in to show the listing is saved. Clicking it again removes it.

Visitors who aren't signed in are asked to sign in or create an account first.

## Where members find their favourites

Under **My account › My Favorites**. The page shows the saved listings as cards, most recently saved first; clicking
the heart on a card removes it. Members who haven't saved anything see *No favorites yet* and a **Browse Listings**
button.

Only listings that are currently published are shown. A saved listing that is sold, deactivated or expired disappears
from the page, and comes back if it is published again. Deleted listings are removed from everyone's favourites.
{: .note}

## How you can use favourites

- **See what is popular.** On the listing page, themes like Nova show how often it has been saved: *Saved N times*.
- **Sort by popularity.** **Favorited** is one of the orders in **Sort Listings** on **Listings › Settings**, so you
  can show the most-saved listings first. See [Publishing options](/how-to-configure-publish-options/).
- **Tell sellers.** A listing that is saved often but never contacted may have a price that is too high: worth
  mentioning when sellers ask why they get no messages.

Saving a favourite doesn't notify the seller and doesn't send the member alerts when the listing changes. For alerts
about new listings, members can subscribe to categories: see [Notifications and alerts](/notification-system/).
