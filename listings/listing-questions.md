Buyers often have the same questions: does it come with a charger, is the size true to fit, can I collect on Sunday?
With questions on listings, a visitor asks on the listing page, the seller answers there, and everyone sees the
answer. Sellers answer once instead of in ten private messages, and the next buyer finds the answer straight away.

## Switch questions on

Questions are off until you switch them on:

1. Go to **Settings › General**.
2. In **Access & privacy**, switch on **Questions on listings**.
3. Click **Save changes** in the bar at the bottom.
{: .steps}

If the switch says *Set by Yclas support for your site*, ask us through **Support** to change it.

Questions show with **Nova**, **Default**, **Atlantic**, **Mercury** and most other built-in themes. If your theme
doesn't show them, the switch tells you so.
{: .note}

## How it works

1. On a listing, a signed-in visitor types their question under **Questions and answers** (**Ask the seller a
   question**) and clicks **Ask**. Visitors who aren't signed in see **Sign in to ask a question**.
2. The seller gets an email, *A question about "…"*, with the question and an **Answer** button.
3. On their listing, the seller writes in **Your answer, shown to everyone** and clicks **Answer**. They can change
   it later with **Update answer**.
4. The person who asked gets an email that their question was answered, with a link to the listing.
{: .steps}

Until it's answered, a question is only shown to the person who asked it and to the seller. Answered questions show
to every visitor, with a **Q** and an **A**. They're also given to search engines as questions and answers, which
helps the listing show up for what buyers search for. Questions can only be asked on published listings.

## Keeping it clean

- A question is 5 to 500 characters long.
- Your site's [banned words](/activate-blacklist-works/) apply to questions and answers, and so does Akismet if
  you use it.
- Each member can ask up to 5 questions a day across your site, and nobody can ask on their own listing.
- The seller, your administrators and your moderators can remove any question with **Hide**. A hidden question
  disappears for everyone.

With [email preferences](/automatic-emails-sent-to-users/#let-members-choose-their-emails) on, members can switch
off the question emails (*Stores you follow and questions*).

## Questions or messages?

[Messages](/how-to-use-messaging-system/) stay private between buyer and seller, which suits prices, meeting points
and personal details. Questions are public, which suits facts about the item that every buyer wants to know. Both
can be on at the same time.
