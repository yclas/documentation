Buyers don't always search the way your filters are built. Many type what they want as a sentence: *toyota hilux
under 30,000 in Cordoba* or *two bedroom flat near the beach*. With AI search on, your marketplace reads that sentence
and turns it into your own filters: the right category, the right location, a price range and the few words that
matter. Buyers get results that fit, without opening a single filter.

AI search works with the Nova theme, the default for new marketplaces. It's included in every plan and the free
trial, with a monthly allowance that depends on your plan.

## What buyers see

1. A buyer types a sentence in the search box in your site's header, or in the big search on your home page, and
   presses Enter.
2. After a moment, the results page opens with the filters already chosen. A **Searching for** bar at the top shows
   what was understood, for example the category, the town, *Up to 200* and the words searched for.
3. Each item in the bar has a **×**. Clicking it removes that filter, so buyers can widen the search with one click.
4. **Search as typed** runs the sentence exactly as the buyer wrote it, without AI, if they prefer that.
{: .steps}

Short searches work as they always have. AI only steps in when the search looks like a sentence: three words or
more, or a number in it (a price, a size, a year). A search for *bike* or *iPhone case* runs as typed. AI also stays
out of the way when the buyer has already picked a category or location in the search form.

AI only chooses from your own categories and locations. It never invents a filter, and it works in your site's
language and currency.
{: .note}

## Switch AI search on or off

1. In the admin panel sidebar, open **AI features** and click **AI search**. This opens the **AI** page at the AI
   search card.
2. Switch **AI search** on or off.
3. Click **Save changes** in the bar at the bottom.
{: .steps}

AI search is on from the start on newer marketplaces that use Nova. On older marketplaces it starts off: switch it on
here when you're ready. If your site uses another theme, the card says *Needs the Nova theme.*; see
[Choose and change your theme](/how-to-change-theme/).

If the switch is greyed out with *Set by Yclas support for your site.*, our team has set it for you.
[Contact support](/use-yclas-support-system/) if you'd like it changed.

The switch used to be in **Settings › General › Search**. That section now links to the AI page. See
[AI features and allowances](/ai-features/) for all the AI switches.
{: .note}

## When few listings match

AI search is built to help buyers find something rather than nothing, even on a young marketplace:

- **Empty categories and locations are skipped.** If the search names a category or town that has no listings yet,
  the search uses the category or location above it when that has listings. Otherwise it leaves that filter out and
  searches with the rest.
- **The words are dropped before the filters.** A listing has to contain every word searched for. If nothing matches
  them in the chosen category or location, the site shows everything else that matches and says so: *No listing had
  "…", so these are all that match the rest.*
- **No unrelated results.** If nothing matches and there's no category or location left to go by, buyers see an
  honest empty page with **Search as typed**, rather than every listing on your site.

The more listings you have in the right categories and locations, the better AI search works. See
[Categories](/how-to-add-categories/) and [Locations](/how-to-add-locations/).
{: .tip}

## Monthly allowance

The number of AI searches your site gets each month depends on your plan:

| Plan | AI searches a month |
| --- | --- |
| **Starter** | 500 |
| **Professional** | 1,000 |
| **Marketplace** | 2,000 |
| Older plans and the free trial | 1,000 |

See [Compare plans](/compare-plans/#ai-included-every-month) for every plan's AI allowances.

They're counted per calendar month, and the AI search card on the [AI page](/ai-features/) shows how many you've used,
for example *312 of 2,000 searches this month*. Repeats don't use the allowance: the same search typed again on your
site the same day gives the same answer for free.

If your site uses up its searches, or AI can't answer for any reason, search keeps working as normal: the buyer's
words are searched as typed, as on a site without AI search. Nothing breaks and buyers see no error. The allowance
starts again on the first day of the next month.
