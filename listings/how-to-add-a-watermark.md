A watermark is a short text, usually your site name, stamped on each listing photo. It discourages other sites from
copying your sellers' photos, and when they do, the photos still carry your name.

## Turn the watermark on

1. In the admin panel, click **Media** in the sidebar.
2. Go to the **Watermark** section and switch on **Watermark**.
3. Type the text in **Watermark Text**, for example your site name or domain.
4. Adjust the look with the settings below.
5. Click **Save changes** in the bar at the bottom.
{: .steps}

**Watermark Text** must be filled in. The field shows your site name as a hint, but if you leave it empty no
watermark is added.
{: .important}

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Watermark** | Adds the watermark to new listing photos. |
| **Watermark Text** | The text to stamp on photos. |
| **Watermark Text Size** | The size of the text, in pixels. |
| **Watermark Text Color** | The colour of the text. Pick it or type a hex code such as `#ffffff`. |
| **Watermark Background Color** | The colour of the band behind the text, for example `#000000`. |
| **Watermark Position** | Where the text goes on the photo: **Center**, **Bottom** or **Top**. |

## Things to know

- **Only new photos get the watermark.** Photos uploaded before you switched it on stay as they are, and switching it
  off doesn't remove it from photos that already have it.
- **The text is added before the photo is resized.** A large original photo is scaled down afterwards, and the text
  shrinks with it. If the watermark looks tiny, increase **Watermark Text Size**; test with a typical phone photo.
- **Bottom** uses your **Image Height** (in the **Listing photos** section) to find the bottom edge. If **Image
  Height** is empty (Auto), choose **Center** or **Top**.
- Keep it short and readable: a domain like `example.com` works better than a sentence. A watermark across the middle
  protects best but can put buyers off; **Top** or **Bottom** is a good compromise.

Test before you rely on it: post a test listing with a photo from your phone, check the result, and adjust the size
and position until you are happy. Then delete the test listing.
