Our team answers questions and fixes problems for every hosted Yclas site. You can reach us two ways, on every plan:

- **Chat**, for quick questions: the chat bubble in your Yclas account and in your site's admin panel.
- **Support tickets**, for problems we need to look into: from **Support** in your Yclas account.

We reply within 24 working hours, usually much sooner. Both kinds of conversation are kept together on your
**Support** page.

Before you write, try searching this Help Centre: most "how do I…" questions have a step-by-step answer here.
{: .tip}

## Chat with us

The chat bubble is in the bottom corner of every page of your Yclas account (**My sites**, **Purchases**,
**Support**, **Edit profile**), of your site's admin panel and of yclas.com. Click it, type your question and click
**Send**.

- To add screenshots or documents, click **Add a file**. You can send up to 5 files at a time: JPG, PNG, GIF or WebP
  images, or PDFs, of up to 8 MB each. If several large files are too big to send together, send fewer at a time.
- The chat belongs to your Yclas account, not to the browser: you can start it on your computer and carry on from your
  phone, as long as you're signed in. After you sign out, it isn't shown.
- When you write from a site's admin panel, we see which site you're writing about.
- If you leave before we answer, we email you our reply, with a link back to the conversation.

In your site's admin panel, the chat is shown only to the site's owner: the administrator account the site was
created with, which **Open admin** in **My sites** signs you into. Other administrators and moderators of your site
don't see it, because the conversation belongs to your Yclas account.
{: .note}

If your question needs a longer look, we may continue it in a support ticket, with the chat copied into it. We tell
you in the chat, and the chat's page on your **Support** page says *We continued this conversation in support ticket
#…*, with an **Open the ticket** link.

## Open a ticket

1. Go to your support page, either from **Support** in your site's admin panel sidebar, or at yclas.com by signing
   in and clicking **Support** in the top menu.
2. Click **New ticket**.
3. Under **What is your question about?**, type a few words, for example *change my logo*, and click **Continue**.
4. Choose the **Site** the ticket is about (if you have more than one), check the **Subject** and write your
   **Message**.
5. Optionally, add screenshots or a PDF under **Attachments (optional)**.
6. Read the reminder of what to include, click **I understand**, then click **Send ticket**.
{: .steps}

We email you that the ticket was received, and again whenever we reply.

You can also open a ticket for a particular site from **My sites**: open the **More** menu (···) next to the site
and choose **Get support**.

## What to include

The fastest answers come from tickets that let us see the problem straight away:

- **The URL** of the page where it happens (copy it from your browser's address bar).
- **The steps to reproduce it**: what you clicked, what you expected and what happened instead.
- **A screenshot**, especially of any error message.
- For a problem with one listing, member or order, which one (a link or its title).

Your message needs at least a few sentences (50 characters or more).

### Attach screenshots and files

You can attach up to 3 files to a new ticket and to each reply, 8 MB each: images (PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP) or PDF
documents. In a reply, click the paperclip (**Attach a file**) next to **Send reply**.

Attachments are private: only you and the Yclas team can open them. Photos are saved again when you upload them,
which removes hidden details such as the location where they were taken.

Never send us a password in a ticket unless we ask for it, and if you do, change it once we're done. We can look
into your site without your password.
{: .warning}

## Follow your chats and tickets

Your **Support** page lists your conversations with us. On the **All** tab, your **Chats** come first, then your
**Tickets**. Filter the tickets with **All**, **Open**, **In progress**, **Answered** and **Closed**, or search them
by subject or ticket number.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Open** | Your ticket is waiting for our team. |
| **In progress** | One of our team has read it and is working on it. |
| **Answered** | We've replied and are waiting for you. |
| **Closed** | The ticket is finished. |

Click a ticket to read the whole conversation. Write your answer under **Reply** and click **Send reply**. When
your question is solved, click **Close ticket** at the top of the ticket. A closed ticket can't be answered any more: if you need more help,
click **New ticket** on it and we'll pick it up.

Click a chat to read it the same way. It's the same conversation as in the chat bubble, so a reply you send from the
Support page shows in the bubble too, and the other way round. Replying to a closed chat opens it again.

Tickets that stay answered for a long time without a reply from you may be closed. If you still need help, reply
or open a new ticket.

## Who can open tickets

Tickets are tied to a site, so you need at least one site that is online on a plan or a free trial. The chat is
open to everyone signed in to a Yclas account. If your site has
expired, renew it first (see [Plans and billing](/plans-and-billing/)); for billing questions you can also reply to
any email we've sent you.

## What support covers

We're happy to help with:

- Anything that doesn't work as it should: errors, bugs, emails not arriving, pages not loading.
- How to do something with Yclas, or which setting does what.
- Your plan, payments, receipts and your domain.

Support doesn't cover:

- Custom design or development, such as changes to a theme or new features built just for you. For a quote,
  email info@yclas.com.
- Teaching HTML, CSS, JavaScript or other web technologies.
- Setting up or supporting other companies' services, such as your domain registrar, email provider or payment
  provider (we'll tell you which settings Yclas needs).

Some plans include priority support from our developers. See the [Pricing page](https://yclas.com/pricing.html).
