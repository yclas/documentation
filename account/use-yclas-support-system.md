Our team answers questions and fixes problems for every hosted Yclas site. You ask through a support ticket in your
Yclas account, and we reply within 24 working hours, usually much sooner.

Before you write, try searching this Help Centre: most "how do I…" questions have a step-by-step answer here.
{: .tip}

## Open a ticket

1. Go to your support page, either from **Support** in your site's admin panel sidebar, or at yclas.com by signing
   in and clicking **Support** in the top menu.
2. Click **New ticket**.
3. Under **What is your question about?**, type a few words, for example *change my logo*, and click **Continue**.
4. Choose the **Site** the ticket is about (if you have more than one), check the **Subject** and write your
   **Message**.
5. Read the reminder of what to include, click **I understand**, then click **Send ticket**.
{: .steps}

We email you that the ticket was received, and again whenever we reply.

You can also open a ticket for a particular site from **My sites**: open the **More** menu (···) next to the site
and choose **Get support**.

## What to include

The fastest answers come from tickets that let us see the problem straight away:

- **The URL** of the page where it happens (copy it from your browser's address bar).
- **The steps to reproduce it**: what you clicked, what you expected and what happened instead.
- **A screenshot**, especially of any error message.
- For a problem with one listing, member or order, which one (a link or its title).

Your message needs at least a few sentences (50 characters or more).

To add a screenshot, drag a JPEG or PNG file into the message box, or use the image button in the editor's toolbar.
Images are uploaded to a public image-hosting service, so crop out anything private, such as members' emails or
payment details, before you add them.
{: .note}

Never send us a password in a ticket unless we ask for it, and if you do, change it once we're done. We can look
into your site without your password.
{: .warning}

## Follow and reply to your tickets

Your support page lists every ticket with its status. Filter the list with **All**, **Open**, **In progress**,
**Answered** and **Closed**, or search by subject or ticket number.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Open** | Your ticket is waiting for our team. |
| **In progress** | One of our team has read it and is working on it. |
| **Answered** | We've replied and are waiting for you. |
| **Closed** | The ticket is finished. |

Click a ticket to read the whole conversation. Write your answer under **Reply** and click **Send reply**. When
your question is solved, click **Close ticket**. If you reply to a closed ticket, it opens again.

Tickets that stay answered for a long time without a reply from you may be closed. If you still need help, reply
or open a new ticket.

## Who can open tickets

Tickets are tied to a site, so you need at least one site that is online on a plan or a free trial. If your site has
expired, renew it first (see [Plans and billing](/plans-and-billing/)); for billing questions you can also reply to
any email we've sent you.

## What support covers

We're happy to help with:

- Anything that doesn't work as it should: errors, bugs, emails not arriving, pages not loading.
- How to do something with Yclas, or which setting does what.
- Your plan, payments, receipts and your domain.

Support doesn't cover:

- Custom design or development, such as changes to a theme or new features built just for you. For a quote,
  email info@yclas.com.
- Teaching HTML, CSS, JavaScript or other web technologies.
- Setting up or supporting other companies' services, such as your domain registrar, email provider or payment
  provider (we'll tell you which settings Yclas needs).

Some plans include priority support from our developers. See the [Pricing page](https://yclas.com/pricing.html).
