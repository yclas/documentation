Every Yclas site starts with a free trial. To keep it online after that, you choose a plan and pay for it from your
Yclas account. This guide covers the whole cycle: the trial, choosing and changing plans, paying, renewals, receipts
and cancelling.

Plans and prices are on the [Pricing page](https://yclas.com/pricing.html). Prices are in US dollars.

## The free trial

- Every new site gets **15 days free**. No credit card is needed to start.
- During the trial, the admin panel's sidebar shows how many days are left, with a **Choose a plan** link. You also
  get an email reminder about a week before the trial ends.
- When you choose a plan, everything you set up during the trial stays exactly as it is.
- During the trial your site is hidden from search engines, so only people you share it with find it. Once you
  choose a plan, search engines can list it.
- If the trial ends without a plan, the site is paused. Come back and choose a plan within 30 days and you lose
  nothing.
- Made a trial site you don't need? You can [delete it yourself](/my-sites/#delete-a-trial-site).
- Want to skip the trial? On the [Pricing page](https://yclas.com/pricing.html), click **Or pay now and skip the
  trial** under a plan. You name your site, then go straight to the checkout.

## How the plans differ

There are three plans: **Starter**, **Professional** and **Marketplace**. They differ in how many listings your site
can hold, how many photos each listing can have, how much AI is included every month (AI searches, listings written
from a photo, AI page builder edits and more), a few features for bigger marketplaces and the level of support. Every
paid plan includes your [own domain](/custom-domain/) with SSL.

| Plan | Price | Listings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Starter** | $19 a month for your first 3 months on a new site, then $69 a month | up to 5,000 |
| **Professional** | $99 a month | up to 100,000 |
| **Marketplace** | $149 a month | up to 300,000 |

Every plan includes the REST API. **Professional** adds, among others, copying a design from a website link, custom
code sections, multilingual sites, business seller accounts, memberships, auctions, bookings and rentals (with
vehicle test drives and property viewings), sale prices, returns, disputes and [webhooks](/webhooks/) for Zapier and
Make. **Marketplace** adds escrow and a commission on every sale, paid bookings with security deposits, the
multi-seller shop (shopping cart, storefronts, bundles, live shipping rates and more), the Full API with
[API keys](/api-keys/) that have their own permissions, the [MCP server](/mcp-server/) for AI assistants and your own branded iPhone app.

See [Compare plans, listings and AI allowances](/compare-plans/) for everything each plan includes.

What happens if you reach your listing limit? We email you when you reach 90%, so you can tidy up old listings or
move to a bigger plan. Nothing is deleted.
{: .note}

## Choose a plan

You can start from either place:

- In your site's admin panel, click **Choose a plan** in the sidebar (or in the trial pill at the top on a phone),
  then **Choose this plan** under the plan you want.
- At yclas.com, open **My sites**, click **Settings** next to the site and click **Choose a plan** (or
  **Change plan**).

Then, on the checkout page:

1. Pick your **Billing cycle**. Longer cycles, when offered, show how much you save compared with paying monthly.
2. Check your **Billing details**: your country and, if you're a business in the EU, your VAT number (see "VAT" below).
3. If you have a coupon, enter it under **Coupon** and click **Update and recalculate**.
4. Under **Payment**, enter your card details and click **Pay** followed by the amount. On a phone or tablet with
   Apple Pay or Google Pay set up, you can tap that button instead, above the card form.
{: .steps}

The card form is part of the checkout page, so you never leave Yclas. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see
your card number. If your bank asks for a security check, it appears in the form.

The plan starts straight away, and your site's page in **My sites** shows the new plan and its renewal date. The order
summary beside the form (folded under **Show order summary** on a phone) shows the plan, billing cycle, any coupon,
VAT and the **Total today**.

If nothing is due today, the button says **Save card and start plan**: your card is saved for the plan's renewals and
the plan starts.

Left the checkout without paying? Your order is saved, and about an hour later we email you a reminder with a link
straight back to it.

## Renewals: monthly, every 6 months or yearly

The plans on the Pricing page are billed monthly; the Marketplace plan has no 6-month option. Some older plans renew
every 6 months or yearly, and keep doing so. When a plan has another cycle, you pick it under **Billing cycle** at checkout;
compare the price per month before you choose. When you click **Renew now** (see below), the checkout
shows the other cycle too if your plan has one, so you can switch there. To move to a cycle the checkout doesn't
offer, [open a support ticket](/use-yclas-support-system/).

Plans renew automatically for the same cycle: a yearly plan renews every year, a monthly plan every month. The
payment is taken the day before your plan's end date, from the card you used, at the plan's regular price (a coupon
applies to your first payment only). Your site's settings page on yclas.com shows
**Subscription status: Active — Renews automatically** and the date it renews.

If a payment doesn't go through, your site stays online for 60 days while you sort it out: see
[If a renewal payment fails](#if-a-renewal-payment-fails) below.

**Starter at $19:** the new-site price covers your first 3 monthly payments. After that, the plan renews at the
regular Starter price of $69 a month on its own, with nothing for you to do. About a week before the first $69
payment, we email you the new amount and the date.
{: .note}

To pay an overdue or upcoming renewal yourself, open **My sites › Settings** for the site and click **Renew now**.

Keep your card up to date to avoid interruptions: see "Change your card" below. If the card we charge expires
before your next renewal, we email you 30 days and again 7 days before the renewal, with an **Update my card**
button that takes you straight to it.
{: .tip}

## If a renewal payment fails

Cards get declined for all sorts of reasons: an expired card, a new card from your bank, not enough funds on the
day. When a renewal of a paid plan fails, your site doesn't go offline. It stays online for **60 days** after its
renewal date, with your listings, members and settings just as they are, while we try the card again and remind you.

**What you see**

- **An email straight away**, *Payment failed: update your card to keep … online*, with the amount, the reason your
  bank gave, the date your site stays online until, and an **Update card and renew** button.
- **A banner on every page of your site's admin panel**, from the renewal date: *Your renewal payment didn’t go
  through*, the date the site stays online until, the days left and, when your bank gave one, its reason. In the last
  week it turns red and says *Your site goes offline in … days*. Every administrator of the site sees it, but only the
  site's owner can pay.
- **Reminder emails** about 9, 21 and 40 days after the renewal date, while the payment is still missing.
- **A last reminder**, *Last reminder: … goes offline on …*, the day before the 60 days end, after our last automatic
  try.

**Pay and keep the site online**

1. Click **Update card and pay** in the banner, or **Update card and renew** in the email. It opens the checkout for
   that renewal at yclas.com (sign in first if you're asked to).
2. Enter your card, or a new one, and click **Pay**.
{: .steps}

Paying this way also saves the card for your next renewals. You can also just
[change your card](#change-your-card) and let the next automatic try charge it. A renewal paid late starts its new
period on the day you pay, so you aren't charged for the days it was unpaid, and a monthly plan isn't charged again a
few days later.

**How we try your card again**

We try it about 9 times over the 60 days (on days 2, 5, 9, 14, 21, 30, 40, 50 and 59 after the renewal date), and
how depends on why it was declined:

- **Not enough funds**: we also try quietly on the 1st and the 15th of the month, common paydays, without emailing you.
- **A card that can't work any more** (expired, lost or stolen, or the bank said not to try again): we don't charge
  that card again. We only try once you've saved a different card, and the reminder emails carry on until you do.
  If your bank renews an expired card under the same details, we charge the renewed card.
- **Any other decline**: we simply try again on the next retry day.

**When the 60 days end**

If the renewal still hasn't been paid, the site goes offline. It isn't deleted: your listings, members and settings are
kept, and you can bring the site back by renewing from **My sites** (see the warning under
[Cancel your subscription](#cancel-your-subscription)).

The 60 days are for paid plans that renew automatically. It works differently in two cases:

- **The first payment after a free trial with a saved card fails**: the site goes offline on the day the trial ends, and we try the card
  again once a week for 4 weeks, emailing you each time. Pay from the email or with **Renew now** and the site is back.
- **You cancelled the plan**: nothing is charged, and the site goes offline on its end date. Cancelling while your
  site is in its 60 days takes it offline straight away, because there's no renewal left to pay.
{: .note}

## Change plan

You can move to another plan at any time. At yclas.com, open **My sites › Settings** for the site, click
**Change plan** and choose the new one. You pay for the new plan at checkout, and it takes effect straight away.

The days left on your current plan aren't lost: they're converted at the new plan's price and added on. For example,
if you have 20 days left on a plan that costs half as much as the new one, about 10 days are added to the new plan.
Partial days are rounded up in your favour.

- Introductory (promo) prices are for new sites only, so you can't switch an existing paid site to one.

## Downgrade to a smaller plan or to free

To pay less, move to a smaller plan with **Change plan**, as above. Check the smaller plan's limits on the
[Pricing page](https://yclas.com/pricing.html) first: if it allows fewer photos per listing, your site's photo limit
is lowered to match, and AI features the smaller plan doesn't include stop (see
[Compare plans](/compare-plans/#ai-included-every-month)). Moving from Professional to Starter also turns off
[bookings](/bookings/) (with test drives and viewings) and [sale prices](/sale-prices/): bookings already made stay
readable, and your settings are kept for an upgrade.

There's no free plan to move to: the free trial is only for new sites, and a paid site can't go back to it. If you
want to stop paying altogether, cancel your subscription (below). The site stays online until the end of the period
you've paid for.

## Receipts and invoices

Every payment has a receipt. At yclas.com, open **Purchases** in the top menu to see your payments, then click
**Receipt** on any of them. The receipt shows the date, what you paid for, the amount, any coupon applied and who
it's billed to. Click **Print or save as PDF** to keep a copy.

The address and VAT number on your receipts come from **Edit profile › Billing details** in your Yclas account.
Fill them in before you pay so they appear on the receipt. See [Your Yclas account settings](/yclas-account-settings/).

## VAT

If you're in the European Union we have to add VAT at your country's rate. If you're a VAT-registered business,
add your **EU VAT number** at checkout (or in **Billing details** in your profile). Once the number is checked, no VAT
is charged.

## Change your card

Your plan renews on the card you paid with. To use another card, for example because the old one is about to
expire:

1. At yclas.com, open **Edit profile** (in the menu under your name) and go to **Payment card**.
2. Enter the new card's details in the card form.
3. Click **Save card**. If your bank asks for a security check, it appears in the form.
{: .steps}

You see *Card changed*, and your renewals are charged to the new card from then on. Saving a card charges nothing.
The **Payment card** section appears once you've paid by card.

## Cancel your subscription

1. At yclas.com, open **My sites** and click **Settings** next to the site.
2. In **Subscription**, click **Cancel subscription**.
3. Tell us why you're cancelling: pick a reason and, if you like, add a few words under
   **Anything else we should know?**. It helps us improve Yclas.
4. Click **Cancel subscription** to confirm.
{: .steps}

Nothing renews after that. The site stays online until the end of the period you've paid for, then goes offline. We
email you a confirmation.

Changed your mind? On the same page, click **Activate subscription** and the plan renews as before.

An expired site isn't deleted straight away: **My sites** shows *The site is offline. Your listings and settings are
kept until…* with the date. After that date the site is deleted with all its listings, members and photos. If you
might come back, renew before then; if you want a copy of your data, see
[Back up, export or close your site](/export-site/).
{: .warning}

Cancelling stops the payments, but it doesn't delete your site or your Yclas account straight away. To close your
account or have a site deleted, see [Back up, export or close your site](/export-site/).

## Refunds and the money-back guarantee

Every plan comes with a **30-day money-back guarantee**. If you're not happy, ask for a full refund within 30 days of
paying, and you get the whole amount back. It also covers a renewal you didn't want: cancel the subscription first
(above) so it doesn't renew again, then ask for the refund. Custom design and development work isn't covered.

To ask for a refund, [open a support ticket](/use-yclas-support-system/) or reply to your receipt email. Say which site
and which payment it's about (the date and amount on the receipt). The refund goes back to the card you paid with;
depending on your bank, it can take a few days to show on your statement.

## Payment questions

For anything else about a payment, such as a charge you don't recognise, a payment that failed or a receipt you
need changed, reply to your receipt email or [open a support ticket](/use-yclas-support-system/).
