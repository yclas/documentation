Your marketplace carries your name, not ours. On a hosted Yclas site, the only place our name appears is a small
"Powered by Yclas" line at the bottom of the emails your site sends during the free trial. Choosing any paid plan
removes it.

## Where Yclas branding appears

| Place | Free trial | Paid plans |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your site's footer | Your site's name (for example *© 2026 Your Site*) | Your site's name |
| Emails your site sends to members | Your site's name, plus a "Powered by Yclas" line | Your site's name only |
| The admin panel | Yclas look and links to our guides and support | The same |

The admin panel is only visible to you and your team, so its look isn't something your members see.

## Remove "Powered by Yclas" from your emails

Choose a paid plan. The line disappears from every email your site sends from that moment on: sign-up
confirmations, new message alerts, receipts and the rest. There's nothing else to switch off.

See [Plans and billing](/plans-and-billing/).
