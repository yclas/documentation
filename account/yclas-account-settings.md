Your Yclas account is the account you sign in with at yclas.com. It owns your sites, pays for their plans and opens
support tickets. Keep it secure: whoever can sign in to it can open the admin panel of every site you run.

This is a different account from your administrator account on your marketplace. To change the name, picture or
password members see on your site, see [Your profile and the member area](/how-to-edit-your-profile/).
{: .note}

## Open your account settings

Sign in at [yclas.com](https://yclas.com/panel/site) and choose **Edit profile** in the menu under your name, at the
top right. The page has these sections: **Profile**, **Billing details**, **Payment card** (once you've paid by card), **Password**,
**Two-step login** and **Profile picture**.

## Profile

| Field | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | How we address you in emails and support replies. |
| **Email** | The address you sign in with, and where we send receipts, renewal reminders and support replies. |
| **Language** | The language your Yclas account is shown in. |
| **Email signature**, **Description** | Optional. |
| **Receive emails (support, updates, renewals).** | Whether we email you about support, updates and renewals. Keep it ticked so you don't miss renewal reminders. |

Click **Save profile**.

### Change your email address

1. Type the new address in **Email**.
2. If you're asked for it, type your password in **Current password (only to change your email)**.
3. Click **Save profile**.
4. Open the email we send to the new address and click **Confirm my new email address**. The link works for 24 hours.
{: .steps}

Your address only changes once you click that link. We also tell your current address that a change was asked for, so
you'd notice if it wasn't you.

## Confirm your email

To keep your account safe, three things need a confirmed email address: changing your account's email, saving a
payment card outside a checkout, and [connecting your own domain](/custom-domain/). Signing up, your free trial and
paying for a plan don't need it.

If you've paid for a plan, your email already counts as confirmed and you never see this step. Otherwise it's
confirmed the first time you open a link we email you, such as the one in your welcome email, a password reset or the
link below.

If yours isn't confirmed yet, the **Email** field is read-only, with a link to **confirm your email address**, and the
other two actions take you to a **Confirm your email** page first:

1. Click **Send me the link**.
2. Open the email we send you and click the link in it.
{: .steps}

The link signs you in and brings you straight back to what you were doing. You only need to do this once. Can't find
the email? Check your spam folder; you can ask for a new link up to 3 times an hour.

Use an email address you'll keep. Payment problems, trial reminders and password resets all go there, and if you
can't receive them your site could go offline without you noticing.
{: .warning}

## Billing details

The **Address**, **City**, **Postal code**, **Country** and **VAT number** you enter here are shown on your
receipts. Fill them in before you pay so they appear on the receipt. Click **Save billing details**.

If you're a VAT-registered business in the European Union, add your **VAT number** here (or at checkout) and no VAT
is charged on your plan. See [Plans and billing](/plans-and-billing/).

## Payment card

Once you've paid by card, this section shows the card form for the card your plan renews on. To replace it, enter
the new card's details and click **Save card**. Saving a card charges nothing; if your bank asks for a security check,
it appears in the form. You see *Card changed*, and the new card is used from your next renewal. Payments are
processed by Stripe; we never see your card number.

If the card runs out before your next renewal, we email you 30 and 7 days before the renewal date, with an
**Update my card** button that opens this section.

## Password

Type your **Current password**, then the new one in **New password** and **Repeat password**, and click
**Change password**. If you've just signed in from a link we emailed you, the current password isn't asked for.

Forgotten your password? Use the forgotten-password link on the yclas.com login page. If you can't get in at all,
see [Locked out of the admin panel](/accidentally-changed-admin-privilege-can-fix/#i-cant-sign-in-to-yclascom-either).

## Two-step login

Two-step login asks for a six-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone each time you sign in, so your
password alone isn't enough to get in.

1. Install Google Authenticator (or another authenticator app) on your phone.
2. In **Two-step login**, click **Turn on**.
3. Scan the QR code with the app.
4. Type the code the app shows and confirm.
{: .steps}

From now on you'll be asked for a code after your password. To switch it off, click **Turn off** in the same section.

Two-step login on yclas.com protects your Yclas account only. Your administrator account on your marketplace has its
own setting; see [Two-step login with an authenticator app](/2-step-authentication/).
{: .note}

## Profile picture

Click **Upload a new picture** to add or replace it, or **Delete photo** to remove it.
