Yclas has three plans: **Starter**, **Professional** and **Marketplace**. They differ in how many listings your site
can hold, how many photos each listing can have, how much AI is included every month and a few features for bigger
marketplaces. Every plan includes hosting, updates, backups, your [own domain](/custom-domain/) with SSL, no Yclas
branding and $0 commission on your sales.

The [Pricing page](https://yclas.com/pricing.html) always has the current prices and a full **Compare plans** table
(the **Compare plans** link under the plan cards jumps to it). Prices are in US dollars, billed monthly.

## The plans at a glance

| | Starter | Professional | Marketplace |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Price** | $19 a month for your first 3 months on a new site, then $69 a month | $99 a month | $149 a month |
| **Listings** | up to 5,000 | up to 100,000 | up to 300,000 |
| **Photos per listing** | 5 | 20 | 30 |
| **Support** | Email | Priority email, from our developers | Priority email + onboarding call |
| **Made for** | A new marketplace finding its first sellers | A busy site with dealers or agents | A marketplace that earns from every sale |

Need more than 300,000 listings, your own servers or custom development? The Pricing page's
**Need something different?** section has a custom plan, from $299 a month, sized with you through our contact page.

### The Starter price for new sites

The $19 Starter price is for new sites: you choose it for a site on its free trial. After 3 monthly payments at $19,
the site moves to the regular Starter price of $69 a month on its own. You don't have to do anything, and nothing on
your site changes.

About a week before the first $69 payment, we email you with the new amount and the date it is charged, so it's never
a surprise. To stop the plan instead, [cancel the subscription](/plans-and-billing/#cancel-your-subscription) before
that date.

An existing paid site can't switch to the $19 price. See [Plans and billing](/plans-and-billing/#change-plan).
{: .note}

## AI included every month

Every plan comes with a monthly allowance for each AI feature. The allowances start again at the beginning of each
calendar month, and what you don't use doesn't carry over.

| AI feature | Starter | Professional | Marketplace |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **AI design edits** (the AI page builder) | 30 in your first month, then 15 a month | 75 | 150 |
| **Listings written from a photo** | 50 | 100 | 500 |
| **AI searches** | 500 | 1,000 | 2,000 |
| **Listings checked by AI moderation** | 250 | 500 | 1,000 |
| **AI reply drafts for sellers** | 200 | 300 | 1,000 |
| **AI blog drafts** | 5 | 10 | 20 |
| **Listing translations** | Not included | 750 | 1,500 |

An AI design edit is one request to the page builder, such as "add a section with our newest cars" or "make the
header darker".

These allowances come with the three plans on the Pricing page. If your site is on an older plan that's no longer
sold, ask us through [Support](/use-yclas-support-system/) which AI features it includes, or move to one of the three.
{: .note}

### Switch the AI features on

Everything AI can do on your site is on one page in your admin panel: open **AI features › Overview** in the
sidebar. Each feature has its own switch and says where it appears for your visitors and sellers. See
[AI features and allowances](/ai-features/).

- **[AI page builder](/ai-page-builder/)**: on all three plans it's ready to use as soon as the plan starts, from
  **AI page builder** in the sidebar.
- **AI search** and **Listings from a photo**: [AI search](/ai-search/) and [listings from a photo](/listing-from-a-photo/)
  work in the Nova theme.
- **Suggested replies**, **AI pre-screening** and **AI-assisted blog drafting**: included from Starter up. They're
  off until you switch them on, on the [AI features](/ai-features/) page.
- **Translate listings automatically**: included from Professional up, for [multilingual sites](/how-to-activate-multilingual-mode/).
  Also off until you switch it on.

A feature your plan doesn't include shows the smallest plan that does, such as *Included in the Professional plan
and up*, with a **See plans** link.

### See how much you've used

On the **AI features** page, each feature that's on shows this month's use against your plan, for example
*312 of 2,000 searches this month*, and the page builder shows how much of this month's AI you've used and when it
resets. A feature that's off shows what your plan includes, for example *Your plan includes 2,000 searches a month*.

When an allowance runs out, that AI feature stops until the next month starts. The rest of your site keeps working as
usual. If you regularly need more, move to a bigger plan: its allowances apply as soon as the plan changes.
{: .tip}

### During the free trial

On the free trial you can try AI search and listings from a photo. The AI page builder and the other AI features come
with a plan. If you choose a plan before the trial ends, its AI allowances start straight away.

If you move to a plan that doesn't include a feature you use, for example from Professional to Starter with
translations on, that feature stops when the plan changes.

## Choose or change your plan

Click **Choose a plan** in your site's admin panel, or open **My sites** at yclas.com and click **Settings** next to
the site. Changing plan takes effect straight away, and the days left on your current plan carry over. See
[Plans and billing](/plans-and-billing/) for checkout, renewals, receipts and cancelling.
