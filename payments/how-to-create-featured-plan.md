Featured listings are the easiest extra to sell. Sellers pay to have a listing highlighted with a **Featured** badge
for a number of days, and you decide where featured listings get extra exposure: the home page, the top of search
results, a widget. **Bring to top** is the cheaper sibling: a one-off payment that moves a listing back to first
place, as if it had just been posted.

Both need at least one [payment method](/setup-payment-gateways/) connected, unless you make them free.

## Sell featured listings

1. Go to **Settings › Payments**.
2. In **Featured listings**, switch on **Sell featured listings**.
3. Click **+ Add a plan**, enter the number of **Days** and the **Price**, and click **Save**. Add as many plans as
   you like, for example 7 days, 30 days and 90 days.
4. Click **Save changes** in the bar at the bottom of the page.
{: .steps}

The plans are listed from shortest to longest. To change one, click **Edit**; to remove it, click **Delete**.

Members always start on your shortest plan and can switch to another at checkout, so make the shortest one your
entry price. A plan priced at 0 features the listing for free, without a checkout.
{: .tip}

### How sellers feature a listing

Sellers can feature a published listing from:

- **My Listings** in their account: the **Featured** button on each listing.
- The page they see right after posting a listing.
- Their listing page, where most themes show the owner a promotion box with **Go Featured!**.

They pick a plan at checkout, pay, and the listing is featured at once. When the plan runs out, the listing goes back
to normal and the seller gets an email (the *ad-expired* template in [Email templates](/automatic-emails-sent-to-users/))
inviting them to feature it again.

### Feature a listing yourself

1. Go to **Listings › All listings** and find the listing.
2. Open its menu and click **Featured**. You go to the checkout page for that listing's owner.
3. Choose the plan and click **Mark as paid** to feature it without charging anyone.
{: .steps}

To stop featuring a listing early, open the same menu and click **Remove Featured**. You can also filter
**All listings** to show only featured listings.

## Where featured listings show up

Featured listings always carry a **Featured** badge in lists. To give them more exposure:

| Where | How to set it up |
| --- | --- |
| Home page | **Listings › Settings › Listing pages › Listings on the Home Page**: choose **Featured Listings**, or **Features Listings Randomly** to rotate them. |
| Top of search results and categories | **Listings › Settings › Listing pages › Sort Listings**: choose **Featured**. Featured listings come first, then the newest. |
| A box on any page | Add the **Featured Ads** widget in **Design › Widgets**. See [Widgets](/overview-of-widgets/). |
| A slider on listing pages | Some themes have a slider option in **Design › Theme Options**. See [Theme options](/theme-options/). |

Visitors can also choose **Featured** in the sort menu on listing pages.

## Bring to top

Bring to top moves a listing back to the first position, by resetting its publish date to now. It doesn't add a badge
and doesn't change when the listing expires.

1. Go to **Settings › Payments**.
2. In **Bring to top**, switch on **Bring To Top Listing**.
3. Enter the **To Top Price**.
4. Click **Save changes**.
{: .steps}

Sellers then find **Go Top!** in the menu of each published listing in **My Listings**, and in the promotion box on
their listing page. After paying, the listing is at the top straight away.

With a price of 0, sellers can bump their listings for free as often as they like. That can flood the top of your
lists, so keep a small price if you want to discourage it.
{: .warning}

Bring to top is only useful when listings are sorted by date. If **Sort Listings** is set to **Featured** or
**Price**, a bumped listing won't move to first place.
{: .note}

## Tips for pricing

- Offer two or three featured plans with a discount on the longer ones.
- Give new sellers a free first feature with a [coupon](/how-to-use-coupon-system/) for the featured product.
- Combine with [membership plans](/membership-plans/) or [pay to post](/pay-to-post/) only if your members are used to
  paying; otherwise start with featured listings alone.
