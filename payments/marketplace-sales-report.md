When members buy from each other on your site, the money belongs to the sellers, so the **Orders** page and
**Analytics** leave those sales out of your revenue. The **Marketplace sales** page shows them on their own: how much
your members sold, how much of it was refunded, and what you earned on it.

## Open the report

Go to **Analytics** in the admin panel. Once your site has had its first sale between members, a link under the page
title says **Sales between your users and your commission**. Click it to open **Marketplace sales**.

The report has nothing to show until a buyer has paid online for a listing or a booking. Before that, the link isn't
there; to set up selling, see [Commission and service fees](/commission-and-service-fees/).
{: .note}

## Choose the period

At the top, pick **7 days**, **30 days** (the default), **3 months** or **12 months**, or type your own dates in the
two date boxes.

## How much your sellers sold and what you earned

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Gross sales** | Everything buyers paid for listings in the period, with the number of orders and paid bookings. |
| **Refunded** | What was refunded, with the number of refunds and the **net sales** that remain. |
| **You earned** | Your commission and the buyers' service fees on those sales. |
| **Take rate** | What you earned, as a percentage of net sales. |
| **Sellers and buyers** | How many members sold, and how many bought, at least once. |
| **Sales and earnings per day** | A chart of sales and your earnings for each day. |
| **Top sellers** | The 10 sellers who sold the most, with their orders, gross sales and what you earned from them. |

Sales count every way a buyer pays a seller on your site: [Buy Now](/pay-directly-from-ad/), accepted
[offers](/make-an-offer/) and [quotes](/request-a-quote/), [auctions](/auctions/), the
[shopping cart](/shopping-cart/) and [paid bookings](/paid-bookings/). Booking requests that take no money aren't
counted. If you sell in several currencies, each currency gets its own set of figures; they are never added
together.

**You earned** only includes sales paid by card through [Stripe Connect](/stripe/#sell-for-your-members-with-stripe-connect),
where your commission is taken automatically. Sales paid to the seller another way (PayPal to the seller,
Escrow.com, the eWallet) count as sales but earn you nothing here.
{: .important}

## Download the sales

**Download CSV** saves every sale and paid booking of the period as a spreadsheet file, one row each: type (sale or
booking), number, date, listing, seller, buyer, amount, amount refunded, what you earned, currency, status and how it
was paid.

## Good to know

- Your own income (listing fees, promotions, membership plans and your commission) stays on the
  [Orders](/how-to-manage-orders/) page and in [Analytics](/useful-statistics-about-your-advertisements/).
- Sellers see their own figures on their [sales dashboard](/sales-dashboard/).
- If you run your marketplace in the EU, the [DAC7 seller report](/dac7-seller-report/) is linked at the bottom of
  this page (administrators only).
