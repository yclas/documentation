Boosts are the main paid extra on big classifieds sites. Instead of choosing between a featured period and a move
back to the top, sellers pick one ready-made package that gives them both, spread over several days. Alongside
boosts you can sell small add-ons (an *Urgent* label, a highlighted card) and a few premium places on your home page.

All of these are switched on in [Make money](/make-money/) and need a [payment method](/setup-payment-gateways/).

## Boost packages

You sell up to three packages. Each one combines:

- **Featured days**: the listing is featured, with its badge and first place in results. Days are added to a
  featured period that is still running, never lost.
- **Moves to top**: the listing goes back to first place among the newest. The first move happens as soon as the
  seller pays; the others follow automatically, a set number of days apart.

Each move to the top also restarts the listing's time until it expires, so a listing doesn't expire while its boost
still has moves left. Its publication date stays the same, so saved-search alerts and digests don't send it out
again after every move.

The suggested packages are:

| Package | Price | What sellers get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Quick** | 3 | Back to the top now. |
| **Fast** | 9 | 7 days featured, and back to the top 3 times, every 2 days. |
| **Max** | 19 | 30 days featured, and back to the top 10 times, every 3 days. |

Prices are in your site's currency. Rename the packages, change what they include, or remove the ones you don't
want.

### Switch on boosts

1. Go to **Make money** and continue to step 2, **What sellers pay for**.
2. Switch on **Boosts**.
3. For each package, set the **Name**, **Price**, **Featured days**, **Moves to top** and **Every (days)**. Click
   **Remove** to drop a package, or **+ Add a package** to bring one back.
4. Finish the steps and click **Save and switch on**.
{: .steps}

Every package needs a price above 0 and something to give: featured days, moves to the top or both. A package without
them isn't saved, and boosts stay off if no package is left.
{: .note}

What a seller buys is fixed when they order: changing a package later doesn't change boosts already bought.

### Where sellers find boosts

Sellers see a **Boost** button on their own live listings, which opens a page with your packages and how many views
the listing has had so far. Where the button appears depends on your theme:

| Theme | Where sellers are offered boosts |
| --- | --- |
| Nova | Right after posting, on their listing page and in **My Listings**, which also shows each listing's views. |
| Other themes | In **My Listings** and on the page where they edit a listing. |

While boosts are on, sellers see one clear choice: in Nova, the **Boost** offer on their listing replaces the
separate **Go Featured!** and **Go Top!** offers; in other themes, **Boost** replaces the **Featured** button. Your
featured plans and bring to top stay on sale wherever else your theme offers them.

Five days after posting, sellers also get one email with how many people saw their listing and a link to boost it.
It isn't sent to sellers who unsubscribed from emails, or for listings that are already featured, boosted or moved
to the top. To change its wording, create an email template named *ad-boost* in
[Email templates](/automatic-emails-sent-to-users/); otherwise a built-in text is used.

To see what your sellers see, open **Make money** after saving and click **See what sellers see**. The preview
uses one of your live listings and never creates an order.
{: .tip}

### When a listing goes offline during a boost

If a boosted listing expires or goes back into review, its remaining moves to the top wait until it is live again,
for up to 90 days after the boost was bought. If the listing is sold or deleted, the boost ends.

## Urgent and Highlight

Two cheap add-ons for one listing, easy first purchases for private sellers:

- **Urgent** puts a red *Urgent* label on the listing, in results and on its page.
- **Highlight** gives the listing's card a coloured background in results.

In **Make money**, step 2, switch on **Urgent and Highlight** and set the **Urgent price**, the **Highlight price**
and how many **Days** they last (suggested: 1 and 2, for 7 days). Buying the same add-on again adds its days after
the period that is running.

The labels and the coloured cards are shown by the Nova theme. Sellers buy them from the **Promote** page of their
listing (see below).
{: .note}

## Home page spotlight

The spotlight is a row called *In the spotlight* at the top of your Nova home page, with a few paid places shown in
random order. Because there are only a few places, they are worth more than a featured listing: price them higher.

In **Make money**, step 2, switch on **Home page spotlight** and set the **Price**, the number of **Days** and the
number of **Places** (1 to 12; suggested: 15 for 7 days, 4 places).

When every place is taken, sellers see the date the next one frees up. If a payment comes in while the spotlight is
full, that listing's place starts as soon as one frees up.

## Sponsored in search results

Sponsored places are a few paid spots at the top of a category, above its results, with a *Sponsored* label. A
listing sponsored in a top category shows on that category's pages and on those of its subcategories. They suit a
directory well: a plumber pays to appear first when visitors browse *Plumbers*.

In **Make money**, step 2, switch on **Sponsored in search results** and set the **Price**, the number of **Days** and
the **Places per category** (1 to 3; suggested: 10 for 7 days, 2 places).

- Places are counted per top category: with 2 places, each top category has its own 2.
- The sponsored listings of a category show in a different order on each visit, on the first page of results only.
- When every place of a category is taken, sellers can't buy one: they see the date the next one frees up. If a
  payment comes in while the category is full, that listing's place starts as soon as one frees up.

Sponsored places show above the results on every built-in theme. A custom theme made for your site, with its own
results page, may not show them, and then they aren't offered. Sellers buy them from the **Promote** page of their
listing.
{: .note}

## The Promote page

In the Nova theme, each listing has a **Promote** page where its seller finds what they can buy or use for it:
your boost packages, Urgent, Highlight, the spotlight and sponsored places, a [paid renewal](/make-money/#paid-renewal) when the
listing is about to expire, and any [promotions included in their plan](/membership-plans/#promotions-included-in-a-plan).
Sellers open it with **Promote** on their listing page or in the menu of **My Listings**, shown once you sell
Urgent, Highlight or the spotlight, or your plans include promotions.

## Promotions while posting

With **Offer promotions while posting** on, sellers can promote a listing before it is even published:

- **In the post form**, a **Make it stand out** step before the submit button offers your boost packages (with
  *No boost* selected by default) and Urgent, Highlight and Spotlight if you sell them. It is part of the post form
  in Nova, the default theme and Atlantic Lite.
- **On the payment page for pay to post**, sellers can add a boost to the same payment (in most themes).
- **On the thanks page** after posting (Nova), sellers who didn't pick a promotion are offered boosts.

If posting is paid, the promotions are added to the posting fee, so the seller pays once. If posting is free, the
seller goes straight to the payment page for the promotions, with the message *Your listing is saved. One last step:
pay for the promotion you chose below.*

The switch is in **Make money**, step 2, and is on unless you turn it off. It appears once you sell boosts,
featured listings, bring to top, Urgent and Highlight or the spotlight. With it off, sellers can only promote a
listing once it is live.

## Promotions bought before a listing is live

A promotion can be paid for while the listing is still waiting: in review, waiting for its posting fee or for the
seller to confirm their email. This applies to boosts, Urgent, Highlight and the spotlight, and also to featured
listings and bring to top.

Such a promotion is kept on hold and starts when the listing goes live, usually within 15 minutes of your approval.
Featured days and moves to the top are not used up while nobody can see the listing.

### Automatic refunds

If a listing never goes live, its seller gets their money back. A held promotion is refunded automatically when its
listing:

- is deleted;
- is rejected or taken offline (marked as spam, unavailable or sold);
- still isn't live 30 days after the payment.

| Paid with | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Stripe | Stripe refunds the money straight away. When the promotion was part of a bigger payment (for example with the posting fee), only the promotion's share is refunded, with the same coupon or VAT. |
| PayPal or another method | The promotion's order is marked as refunded and you get an email asking you to send the money back in your PayPal (or other) account. |

The seller always gets an email saying the promotion wasn't used and was refunded. You can change it in
[Email templates](/automatic-emails-sent-to-users/) (*promotion-refunded*) once the first one has been sent.

To refund a promotion yourself, for example after a complaint, use **Refund order** or **Mark as refunded** in
[Orders](/how-to-manage-orders/#refunds-and-cancellations). The boost's remaining moves stop, and its featured days,
Urgent, Highlight or spotlight place are taken back.

## In your orders

Each purchase is an [order](/how-to-manage-orders/) under its product: **Boost**, **Urgent or Highlight**,
**Home page spotlight** or **Sponsored in search results**. A promotion bought together with the posting fee is part of that payment; it also appears as
its own order with the payment method *included* and an amount of 0, so you can see what was bought. Your
[earnings on the Make money page](/make-money/#your-earnings) count it under its product.
