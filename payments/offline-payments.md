Not every member wants to pay by card. With **Alternative Payment** you can offer any other way to pay, such as a
bank transfer, cash at your office or a local mobile money service. Members see your instructions at checkout, pay
you outside the site, and you mark the order as paid when the money arrives.

It works for everything members pay *you* for: featured listings, bring to top, pay to post, membership plans and
eWallet top-ups. It isn't offered for [Buy Now](/pay-directly-from-ad/) purchases, where the buyer pays the seller.

## Set it up

1. Go to **Pages** and create a page with your payment instructions, for example *Pay by bank transfer*. Include
   your bank details and ask members to use their order number as the payment reference. See
   [Pages](/how_to_add_pages/).
2. Go to **Settings › Payments**.
3. In **Payment methods**, choose that page in **Alternative Payment**.
4. Click **Save changes**.
{: .steps}

At checkout, members now see a button named after your page. Clicking it opens your instructions in a pop-up. The
button appears next to any other payment methods you have, so members can still pay by card if they prefer.

To turn it off, set **Alternative Payment** back to **Deactivated**.

Keep the page title short and clear, because it is the button label: *Bank transfer* works better than
*How to pay us by bank transfer*.
{: .tip}

## What to put on the instructions page

- How to pay: bank name, account holder, IBAN or account number, and SWIFT/BIC for international payments.
- The reference to use: the order number, shown on the checkout page and in the member's **My Payments** list.
- How long it takes you to confirm, for example *within one working day*.
- How to contact you if they have paid but nothing happened.

## Confirm a payment

When the money arrives:

1. Go to **Orders** and open the **Unpaid** tab, or search for the order number or the member's email.
2. Open the order's menu (the three dots) and click **Mark as paid**, then confirm.
{: .steps}

The order turns to **Paid** with the payment method *Cash*, and whatever the member bought is applied at once: the
listing is featured or published, or the plan starts. You can also click **Mark as paid** on the checkout page itself
while signed in as an administrator or moderator.

Unpaid orders stay in **Orders** until you mark them as paid. For orders about a listing (featured, bring to top, pay
to post), Yclas emails the member a reminder two days later with a link back to the checkout (the *new-order* email in
[Email templates](/automatic-emails-sent-to-users/)).
{: .note}
