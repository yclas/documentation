All the AI on your marketplace is managed from one page in the admin panel. It lists each feature with its switch,
says where visitors and sellers will see it, and shows how much of this month's allowance you've used.

## Open the AI page

In the admin panel sidebar, open **AI features** (near the top, in the **AI** section) and click **Overview**. The
sidebar shows how many AI features are on, for example *3 on*. The links under it (**AI search**, **Listings from a
photo**, **AI pre-screening**) jump straight to that feature's card.

To switch a feature on or off, use its switch and click **Save changes** in the bar at the bottom.

## The features

### For your visitors and sellers

| Feature | What it does | Where it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **AI search** | A search typed as a sentence becomes the right category, location and price filters. See [AI search](/ai-search/). | The search box on every page. |
| **Listings from a photo** | Sellers take a photo and AI fills in the title, description, category and price for them to check. See [Listings from a photo](/listing-from-a-photo/). | The post a listing page. |
| **Suggested replies** | Sellers get a **Suggest a reply** button in a conversation about their listing. AI drafts an answer from the listing and the last messages; the seller edits it and sends it. Nothing is ever sent automatically. | Messages. |
| **Translate listings automatically** | Listing titles and descriptions are translated into your site's other languages. Visitors can always show the original. | [Multilingual sites](/how-to-activate-multilingual-mode/), on the listing page in Nova and the default theme. |
| **AI review summary** | A short *What people say* summary of the pros and cons on listings with 5 or more reviews. Switched on in **Add-ons › Reviews**, on paid plans. See [AI review summary](/review-extras/#ai-review-summary). | The listing and its reviews page. |
| **Ask AI about a listing** | Visitors ask a question about a listing and AI answers from the listing and its reviews. Switched on in **Add-ons › Reviews**, on paid plans. See [Ask AI about a listing](/review-extras/#ask-ai-about-a-listing). | The listing page. |

### For you

| Feature | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **AI pre-screening** | Before a listing posted directly goes live, AI checks it against your rules and for scams, spam and the wrong category. A listing it flags waits in **Moderation** with the reason, instead of publishing. Nothing is ever deleted automatically. |
| **AI-assisted blog drafting** | Adds a **Write a draft with AI** button to new [blog](/how-to-create-a-blog/) posts. |
| **AI assistants** | Run your marketplace from an AI app that supports MCP, such as Claude or ChatGPT: ask for this week's numbers, find and publish listings, see new users and orders. Set it up from **AI features › AI assistants**; see [AI assistants (MCP server)](/mcp-server/). Marketplace plan. |

The [AI page builder](/ai-page-builder/) has its own card at the top of the page, with **Open the page builder**
when your plan includes it, and on the free trial.

A few AI helpers live on their own pages instead, on the Starter, Professional and Marketplace plans:

- **Write it with AI** on [Recruit sellers](/recruit-sellers/#write-it-with-ai) drafts your message to a possible
  seller (200 drafts a month, up to 40 a day).
- **AI drafts of your replies** in **Add-ons › Reviews** gives sellers a **Draft with AI** button when they answer a
  review (300 a month, up to 30 a day per seller). See [Review extras](/review-extras/#ai-drafts-of-your-replies).
- **Write with AI** for [category and city pages](/how-to-add-locations/#your-own-text-for-category-in-location-pages)
  drafts each page's title, description and intro (200 a month).

## Which plan includes what

AI search and listings from a photo work on every plan, including the free trial, with the **Nova** theme. The free
trial can also try the AI page builder, with 5 AI design edits. The others come with the plans:

| Feature | Starter | Professional | Marketplace |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AI page builder | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Suggested replies, AI pre-screening, AI-assisted blog drafting | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Translate listings automatically | No | Yes | Yes |
| AI assistants ([MCP server](/mcp-server/)) | No | No | Yes |

A feature your plan doesn't include shows the smallest plan that does, for example *Included in the Professional
plan and up*, with a **See plans** link. The features a plan includes are off until you switch them on.

On an older plan that's no longer sold, the AI page shows which features your site has. Ask us through
[Support](/use-yclas-support-system/) if you'd like to move to one of the current plans.

## Monthly allowances

Each feature has a monthly allowance that depends on your plan. It starts again on the first day of each month, and
what you don't use doesn't carry over.

| Each month | Starter | Professional | Marketplace | Other plans and the free trial |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AI design edits (page builder) | 30 in your first month, then 15 | 75 | 150 | Free trial: 5 for the whole trial |
| AI searches | 500 | 1,000 | 2,000 | 1,000 |
| Listings from a photo (and listings written from pasted text) | 50 | 100 | 500 | 200 |
| Listings checked by AI pre-screening | 250 | 500 | 1,000 | – |
| Suggested replies | 200 | 300 | 1,000 | – |
| AI blog drafts | 5 | 10 | 20 | – |
| Listing translations | – | 750 | 1,500 | – |

The cards show your own numbers. A feature that's on shows this month's use, for example *312 of 2,000 searches this
month*, with a bar. A feature that's off shows what your plan includes, for example *Your plan includes 500 listings
from a photo a month*. The page builder card shows how much of this month's AI you've used and when it resets; on the
free trial it shows the trial's AI edits, for example *2 of 5 AI edits in your free trial used*.

When an allowance runs out, that feature pauses until the 1st of next month and the rest of your site carries on as
usual: searches run as typed, sellers fill in the post form themselves, and so on. Your admin panel says so where
you'd use it, for example *AI moderation paused until [the 1st of next month]. Upgrade for more.* on the feature's card here, in
the moderation queue, on the Translations page and in the blog editor. **Upgrade for more.** links to the plans (the
Marketplace plan, the biggest, has no link). On the free trial it says *paused: your free trial's AI is used up*, with
**Choose a plan**. See [Compare plans](/compare-plans/#ai-included-every-month).

## Feature by feature

### Suggested replies

Suggested replies need messages between users. Until [Messaging](/how-to-use-messaging-system/) is on, the switch is
greyed out with a link: *Turn on messages between users first*. Each seller can ask for up to 30 suggestions a day.

### Translate listings automatically

Translation needs a second language. Until your site is
[multilingual](/how-to-activate-multilingual-mode/), the switch is greyed out with *Turn on a second language first*.
Listings are translated when they are posted or edited; the seller's own text always stays the original.

### AI pre-screening

When it's on, the card has three more settings:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Strictness** | **Lenient** sends a listing to Moderation only for clear problems, **Standard** is the default, and **Strict** for anything that could be one. |
| **Your own rules, in plain language** | One idea per line, for example *no weapons* or *only farm animals*. The AI follows these as well as your banned words and its own checks. |
| **AI hints in the moderation queue** | For sites that approve every listing by hand anyway: AI checks each one and shows its reason next to the listings it would flag. Off unless you switch it on. |

Pre-screening checks listings that would go live straight away, and checks a live listing again when its seller
changes the title, description or price. If the AI can't check a listing in time, the listing waits in Moderation for
you rather than going live unchecked. You decide on every held listing in [Moderation](/how-ads-moderation-works/).

With pre-screening on, messages between members also show a gentle warning next to a message that asks to pay
outside your site, contains a link or asks for a code. It's a note to the reader, never a block.

## Switches you can't change

- *Set by Yclas support for your site.* means our team has set that feature for you. Ask us through
  [Support](/use-yclas-support-system/) to change it.
- *Needs the Nova theme.* appears on AI search and listings from a photo when your site uses another theme. See
  [Choose and change your theme](/how-to-change-theme/).
- *Available after the next update of your site.* means the feature isn't ready on your site yet. Check again later.

The same switches for suggested replies, listing translation and blog drafting are also on the pages where those
features are used (**Settings › General**, **Settings › Translations** and **Settings › SEO**). They all change the
same setting.
