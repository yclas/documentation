**Settings › General** holds the settings that shape your whole marketplace: what it is called, how new listings
go live, who can see it and sign up, how dates and money look, and how search works. This page goes through the
sections in the order the panel shows them. Some sections only appear with the Nova theme, or once a feature is on.

## Change a setting

1. In the admin panel, go to **Settings › General**.
2. Use the list of sections on the left to jump to the part you need, or scroll.
3. Change the setting.
4. Click **Save changes** in the bar that appears at the bottom of the page.
{: .steps}

Every field on the page is saved together, so you can change several settings and save once. If you try to leave
with unsaved changes, the panel warns you first.

A switch that says *Set by Yclas support for your site* is locked. Ask us through **Support** to change it.
{: .note}

## Your site

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Site name** | The name of your marketplace. It appears in the header when you have no logo, in page titles and browser tabs, and in the emails your site sends. It can't be empty. |
| **Site description** | One or two sentences about your marketplace, used as the description of your home page in Google results and when people share your link. A counter shows the length: keep it under 160 characters, because Google cuts off longer descriptions. |
| **Start page** | The page visitors see when they open your address: the **Home page** (default), **All listings**, or **Users** (the list of members). |
| **Contact page text** | Shows one of your [pages](/how_to_add_pages/) above the form on your contact page, for example your opening hours or address. Choose **Deactivated** to show the form on its own. |

The name your emails come from is a separate setting, **Sender name** in [Email settings](/general-email-configuration/).
New sites start with the same name in both. If you rename your marketplace here, change the sender name too.
{: .tip}

Write the site description for people, not for search engines: say what members can buy or sell and where. A
description stuffed with keywords looks like spam in Google's results.
{: .tip}

## Directory

**Site type** chooses what your marketplace is: **Classifieds** (the default), **Directory (businesses)** or **Both**.
Directory and Both add business listings (opening hours, services, social links), events, claims and quote requests
on top of your classifieds. See [Run a business directory](/directory-mode/).

Once you choose Directory or Both and save, the section also shows where everything that comes with it is managed:

| Row | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Business details** | The fields a business fills in on its listing. **Custom fields** opens them. |
| **Claims** | People asking to take over a business listing, with how many are waiting. **Claims** opens the list. |
| **Quote requests** | Requests visitors send from a business listing, and whether businesses pay for them. **Leads** opens the list; **Pricing in Make money** sets the price. |
| **Business seller accounts** | Whether [business accounts](/business-sellers/) are on. The link jumps to the **Business sellers** section. |

## Listing types

With the Nova theme, **Listing types** gathers the kinds of listing your site offers besides a fixed-price ad:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Auctions** | Sellers can list an item for bidding. Once on, a link opens the bidding rules. See [Auctions](/auctions/). |
| **Vehicles pack** | Key specs, similar vehicles, compare, a finance calculator and a vehicle search on listings with a make or model. Once on, a link opens the finance calculator defaults. |
| **Property pack** | Floor plans on listings, a mortgage calculator and a property search section. See [Property and jobs packs](/property-and-jobs-packs/). |
| **Jobs pack** | Apply with a CV on job offers, the applications list, and job search and *Post your CV* sections. Once on, a link opens **Job applications**. See [Property and jobs packs](/property-and-jobs-packs/). |

The packs are off unless your site was created for that kind of marketplace. Newer sites have auctions on.

## How new listings go live

The moderation mode decides what happens after a member clicks to publish a listing. Pick one of six options:

| Option | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Post directly** | Listings go live as soon as they are posted. |
| **Moderation** | You approve each listing before it goes live. |
| **Payment** | Listings go live once the poster pays. |
| **Email confirmation** | The poster confirms by email, then the listing goes live. |
| **Email confirmation and moderation** | The poster confirms by email, then you approve. |
| **Payment and moderation** | The poster pays, then you approve. |

The payment modes charge the price you set on each category. [Moderation](/how-ads-moderation-works/) explains the
approval queue, and [Categories](/how-to-add-categories/) shows how to set a price to post.

| **Private site** | Only signed-in members can see the site. When it is on, **Private site page** lets you show one of your pages to signed-out visitors instead of your site. See [Private site](/private-site/). |

| **Report this listing** | Visitors report a listing with a reason, and you decide in **Reports**. See [Reported listings](/flag-ad-inappropriate/). |
| **Terms pop-up** | Visitors must accept the page you choose (usually your terms of use) before they can use the site. See [Terms of use and age alert](/activate-access-terms-alert/). |

## Sign-ups

These settings control who can create an account. [Sign-up and login settings](/registration-and-login/) explains
each one in detail.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Verify email addresses** | New members have to confirm their email address after registering. |
| **Allowed email domains** | Only addresses on these domains can sign up, for example `yourcompany.com` for a staff marketplace. Separate domains with commas. Leave it empty to allow everyone. |
| **Blocked email domains** | Addresses on these domains can't sign up. Separate domains with commas. |
| **Block email subdomains** | Blocks addresses on subdomains, such as `name@mail.example.com`, which spammers often use. |
| **Blocked email addresses** | Individual email addresses whose messages your contact forms drop, one per line. |

To ask new members whether they're private or a business, see **Business sellers** below.

## Regional

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Country** | The country the phone number field starts with, so members don't have to pick their dialling code. Choose **None** to leave it blank. |
| **Time zone** | The time zone of your marketplace. See below. |
| **Date format** | How dates are written across your site. See below. |
| **Price format** and **Decimal places** | How prices are written: currency symbol, its position, separators and decimals. See [Currency and price format](/how-to-currency-format/). |
| **Distance units** | **Metric** or **Imperial**. Distances, for example "5 km from you" when [auto-locate](/auto-locate-visitors/) is on, use these units. |

### Time zone

Every date and time your site shows or records follows this setting: when a listing was published or expires, order
and payment dates, reviews, forum replies and blog posts. If replies on your forum seem to come
from the future, or listings expire at odd hours, the time zone is wrong.

Pick the city that shares your time zone, for example **Europe/Madrid** for Spain or **America/New_York** for the
east coast of the United States. Daylight saving time is handled for you.

### Date format

The date format uses letter codes. The field shows how today's date looks with the format you saved, so you can check
it before and after you change it.

| Code | Means | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `d` | Day with a leading zero | 07 |
| `j` | Day without a leading zero | 7 |
| `m` | Month as a number with a leading zero | 10 |
| `n` | Month as a number without a leading zero | 10 |
| `M` | Short month name | Oct |
| `F` | Full month name | October |
| `y` | Two-digit year | 26 |
| `Y` | Four-digit year | 2026 |
| `H:i` | Hours and minutes, 24-hour clock | 14:30 |

Some common formats:

| Format | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| `d-m-y` (the default) | 07-10-26 |
| `d/m/Y` | 07/10/2026 |
| `m/d/Y` | 10/07/2026 |
| `j F Y` | 7 October 2026 |
| `d-m-Y H:i` | 07-10-2026 14:30 |

Any other character, such as `-`, `/`, `.` or a space, is shown as it is.

Month names written with `M` or `F` are always in English, whatever the language of your site. If your site isn't
in English, use a numeric format such as `d/m/Y`.
{: .note}

## Languages

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Multilingual** | Lets visitors switch your site between several languages, and files each listing under the language it was posted in. |
| **Languages** | The language codes visitors can choose from, separated by commas, for example `en_UK,es_ES`. It appears when **Multilingual** is on. |

Read [Multilingual sites](/how-to-activate-multilingual-mode/) before you switch this on: it changes which listings
each visitor sees. To change the main language of your site or edit its wording, see
[Language and translations](/how-to-change-language/).

## Sharing

With the Nova theme, **Share buttons** makes the Share button on a listing offer WhatsApp, Facebook, Telegram, X and
copy link, with a picture of the listing. When it is off, your site shows the older ShareThis buttons.

## Search

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Search descriptions** | Search also looks for the words in listing descriptions, not only in titles. Members find more, but results are less precise. |
| **Pick several categories and locations** | Visitors can pick several categories and locations at once in the search form. |
| **Sort locations A to Z** | Lists locations in alphabetical order everywhere, ignoring the order you set on the [Locations](/how-to-add-locations/) page. |
| **Saved searches** | Buyers save a search and get an email when a new listing matches. See [Saved searches and alerts](/saved-searches/). |

AI search and listings from a photo are switched on the **AI** page, not here. See
[AI features and allowances](/ai-features/).

## Trust & safety, messages and notifications

- **Trust & safety**: verification requests, two-sided reviews and earned badges, plus **Double-blind reviews after
  a deal** when reviews are on. See [Reviews and ratings](/review-system-works/) and [Verified members](/verified-user/).
- **Messages** (when messaging is on): **Offers in chat**. See [Make an offer](/make-an-offer/).
- **Notifications**: **Web push notifications** for new messages, search alerts and members' listings.

## Mobile apps

With the Nova theme, **Mobile apps** has the **iOS and Android apps** switch for your own branded apps, included in
the Marketplace plan. Once the apps are on and saved, it also has the **Apple Team ID** and **Android signing
certificate fingerprints** your apps need, and a link to set up push notifications to the app.

## Advanced

Only change these if you know what they do.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **API key** | The key apps and scripts use to work with your site through the [REST API](/api-documentation/). Treat it like a password. |
| **Code in the page head and footer** | Moved to **Design › Custom code**. See [Add code to the head and footer](/html-in-head-element/). |
| **Ads.txt** | The contents of your `ads.txt` file, which ad networks such as Google AdSense ask for. The link under the field opens the live file. See [Banners and ad slots](/how-to-add-banner/). |
| **Robots.txt** | The contents of your `robots.txt` file, which tells search engines what to crawl. The link under the field opens the live file. See [Search engine visibility](/allowdisallow-bots-crawlers/). |

## When a feature's page says it is off

Some admin pages belong to a feature you switch on elsewhere. If you open one while its feature is off, the panel
takes you to the switch, with a note saying what to turn on:

| Page | Takes you to |
| --- | --- |
| **Claims**, **Leads** | **Settings › General › Directory**: set the site type to Directory or Both. |
| **Auctions** settings | **Settings › General › Listing types**: switch on **Auctions**. |
| **Job applications** | **Settings › General › Listing types**: switch on **Jobs pack**. |
| **Bookings** | **Add-ons › Bookings**. |
| **Commission on sales** | The Stripe page in **Add-ons**: switch on **Enable Stripe Connect**. |

Members and visitors who open these addresses see a "Page not found" page instead.
