The admin panel is where you run your marketplace: listings, members, payments, pages, design and settings. It
lives at `/oc-panel` on your site's address (for example `yourname.yclas.com/oc-panel`), and opens when you sign in
as an administrator.

This guide shows what's where, so you can find any setting in a few seconds. The panel can also show you around
itself: see [The guided tour](#the-guided-tour).

## The guided tour

The first time you open the dashboard of a new site (in its first 45 days, once you're past
[Set up your site](#set-up-your-site)), a one-minute tour offers to show you around. Click **Show me
around** to start it, or **Maybe later**. Each step lights up one part of the panel: your setup guide, the AI page
builder, the sections of the sidebar, the search box, **Visit Your Website**, support and your account menu. Use
**Next** and **Back** to move through it, or **Skip tour** to stop.

On plans that include the [AI page builder](/ai-page-builder/), its step has a button to open it, the free trial
included. On older plans without the builder, the step has **See plans** instead.

To take the tour again at any time, open your account menu (your picture, top right) and click **Take the tour**, or
type *tour* in the search palette.

### Tips on your first visit

The first time you open a few key pages (**All listings**, **Categories**, **Make money**, **Theme Options**,
**Pages** and **Settings › General**), a short tip explains what the page is for. Click **Got it** to close it, or
**No more tips** to stop them all.

The tour and the tips start by themselves for administrators during a site's first 45 days. On an older site, the
tips appear once you've taken the tour. Whether you've seen them
is remembered in your browser, so they may show again on another computer or after you clear your browser data.
{: .note}

## The layout

The panel has three parts:

- **The sidebar**, the dark column on the left, with every section of the panel. On a phone it's hidden behind the
  menu button in the top-left corner.
- **The top bar**, with the name of the page you're on, your site's status, **Visit Your Website** and your account
  menu.
- **The page itself**, in the middle.

At the top of the sidebar is a card with your site's name and address. Clicking it opens **My sites** in your Yclas
account, where you manage your plan and domain. Below it is the search box (**Search or jump to…**).

## The sidebar

The links are grouped under **AI**, **Manage**, **Content** and **Configure**, after **Home**, **Analytics** and
**Support** at the top (with **Set up your site** below **Home** on a new site). Some links only appear once you switch the feature on, and moderators only see the sections their role
allows.

| Link | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Your dashboard: setup guide, key numbers, activity and recent listings. |
| **Set up your site** | The guided setup for a new site, with how many launch steps are done (for example *3/6*). See [Set up your site](#set-up-your-site). |
| **Analytics** | Listing views, contacts, new listings, new users, paid orders and sales over time. See [Analytics](/useful-statistics-about-your-advertisements/). |
| **Support** ↗ | Opens your support tickets on yclas.com. The chat bubble in the bottom corner reaches our team too. See [Get help from Yclas support](/use-yclas-support-system/#chat-with-us). |
| **AI page builder**, **AI features** | The AI tools for your site, with how many are on. See [AI page builder](/ai-page-builder/) and [AI features and allowances](/ai-features/). |
| **Listings** | **All listings**, **Moderation** (when moderation is on, with a count of listings waiting), **Claims** and **Leads** (on a directory site), **Categories**, **Locations**, **Custom Fields**, **Job applications** (with the jobs pack) and listing **Settings**. |
| **Users** | Your members, their roles and status. See [Manage users](/manage-users/). |
| **Referrals** | Switch on invite links and rewards for members who bring friends, and follow each referral. See [Referrals](/referrals/). |
| **Recruit sellers** | Your list of people and businesses to invite as sellers, the message you send them and who joined. See [Recruit sellers](/recruit-sellers/). |
| **Subscribers** | People subscribed to your emails and newsletter. |
| **Subscriptions** | Members' paid memberships (only when [membership plans](/membership-plans/) are on). |
| **Orders** | Everything members have paid for. See [Orders and transactions](/how-to-manage-orders/). |
| **Deposit claims** | Sellers' claims on a booking's security deposit, for you to decide (only when [security deposits](/paid-bookings/#security-deposits) are on). |
| **Make money** | A guided setup for charging your sellers: boosts, featured listings, pay to post and seller plans, and how you get paid. See [Make money](/make-money/). |
| **Banners** | Banners advertisers bought, to approve, and the live ones (only when [selling banners](/how-to-add-banner/#sell-banners-to-advertisers) is on). |
| **Transaction** | eWallet movements (only when the [eWallet](/ewallet/) is on). |
| **Reviews**, **Forum** | Shown when [reviews](/review-system-works/) or the [forum](/add-forums-section/) are switched on. |
| **Reviews to check** | Reviews held as suspicious (only when [fake-review protection](/review-extras/#fake-review-protection) is on). |
| **Business**, **Reports** | Shown when [business seller accounts](/business-sellers/) or [listing reports](/flag-ad-inappropriate/) are switched on. |
| **Account types** | Your own account types and their settings (administrators only, when [your own account types](/account-types/#your-own-account-types) are on). |
| **Pages** | About, Terms, Privacy and your other pages. See [Pages](/how_to_add_pages/). |
| **Blog**, **FAQ** | Shown when the [blog](/how-to-create-a-blog/) or [FAQ](/create-frequent-asked-questions-faq/) is on. |
| **Settings** | **General**, **Payments**, **SEO**, **Coupons**, **Translations** and **User Custom Fields**. |
| **Media** | Photo sizes, watermark and image settings. |
| **Email** | How your site sends email, and the email templates. |
| **Design** | **Themes**, **Theme Options**, **Widgets**, **Menu** and **Custom code**. |
| **Add-ons** | One searchable list of the optional features you can switch on and the services you can connect, such as payment providers, Google and social networks. See [Add-ons](/addons/) and [Integrations](/integrations-overview/). |
| **Tools** | Move your site to Yclas, Export everything, sitemap, cache and scheduled jobs. See [The Tools page](/tools-overview/). |
| **Domain & SSL** ↗ | Opens the page in your Yclas account where you connect your own domain. See [Connect your own domain](/custom-domain/). |

Links marked ↗ open yclas.com in a new tab.

### Pages for features that are off

Some pages belong to a feature you switch on somewhere else, such as **Claims**, **Job applications** or the
**Auctions** settings. If you open one while its feature is off, for example from a bookmark, the panel takes you to
the switch with a note saying what to turn on. See
[General settings](/change-site-name-site-description/#when-a-features-page-says-it-is-off) for the full list.

## The top bar

From left to right:

- **The page title**, after your site's name. On pages with a large heading of their own, it appears once you scroll
  down.
- **Trial countdown** — during the free trial, a pill such as *9 days left · Choose a plan* (on a phone; on a larger
  screen the countdown sits at the bottom of the sidebar).
- **Search the guides** — searches this Help Centre without leaving the panel.
- **Site live** or **Maintenance mode** — whether visitors can see your site. See
  [Maintenance mode](/how-to-activate-maintenance-mode/).
- **Visit Your Website** — opens your site in a new tab.
- **Your picture** — opens the account menu: **Edit profile**, **Support**, **Documentation**, **Take the tour**, **Appearance**
  (**Light**, **Dark** or **Auto**, which follows your device), **Manage Websites** (your sites on yclas.com) and
  **Logout**.

## The home dashboard

**Home** gives you a picture of your marketplace at a glance.

### The setup guide

For the first 45 days of a new site, the top of the dashboard shows a setup guide: a ring with how ready your site
is, and the launch steps from [Set up your site](#set-up-your-site), ticked as you do them. The first step is
**Choose how your site works**; the others follow your answers, for example **Create your categories**, **Add your
logo**, **Post your first 5 listings** (the counter shows how many you have; example listings created at sign-up
don't count) and **Share your site with your first members**.

The main button opens the next step, and **Continue setup** opens the guided setup. A step your plan doesn't include
shows a lock, and its button takes you to the plans. Once every step is done, the guide goes away and **Share your
site** sits at the top of the dashboard. To hide the guide earlier, click the **×** in its corner (**Hide the setup
guide**); it stays hidden in that browser.

If your site doesn't have the guided setup, the guide shows three steps instead: **Create your categories**, **Add
your logo** and **Post your first 5 listings**, with **Import from CSV**, **Share your site** and **Recruit sellers**
next to the listing step.
{: .note}

### Set up your site

**Set up your site** walks you through your launch one card at a time. It's on for every site during its first 45
days, and the first time you open the admin panel it opens by itself. After that, open it from **Set up your site**
in the sidebar or **Continue setup** on the dashboard.

1. **Two questions.** *What do people list on your site?*: **Things people sell or rent** (classifieds),
   **Businesses and professionals** (a directory) or **Both**. Then *How does a deal happen?*: **They contact the
   seller**, **They pay on your site** (Marketplace plan), **They book a time** (Professional plan and up) or, on a
   site with businesses, **They ask for a quote**. Your answers set up the site to match, and you can change them
   any time.
2. **Your plan.** A list of the launch steps for your kind of site, with about how many minutes each takes and the
   ones already done. Click **Let’s go**.
3. **One card per step.** Many steps are done right in the card: tap suggested categories or type your own, upload
   your logo (and use its colour for your buttons and links if you like), switch on business accounts, bookings or
   quote requests, or copy your link and share it on WhatsApp, Facebook or by email. The listings card offers the
   quickest ways to add your first ones, such as posting one yourself, from a photo, importing businesses from Google,
   pasting a list or uploading a spreadsheet. **More options** opens the full page for that step.
4. **Skip for now** moves on and leaves the step for later. When a card sends you to another page, a **Back to setup**
   button stays in the corner: click it when you're done, and the step is ticked.
5. **The finish card.** *Your site is ready to launch*, or how many steps are left for later, with add-ons that suit
   your kind of site (reviews, listings from a photo, payments, bookings…). From there, **Visit your site**, **Go to
   your dashboard** or **Go through the steps left**.
{: .steps}

Depending on your answers, the steps can include connecting Stripe and turning on the cart (they pay on your site),
turning on bookings, letting businesses sign up as a business, or sending quote requests to businesses. Steps your
plan doesn't include stay in the list, marked with the plan they need.

**Save and exit** (top right) takes you back to the dashboard; your answers and finished steps are kept. **All steps**
shows the whole setup on one page, in three parts: *How your site works*, *Before you launch* and *When you're ready
to grow*.
{: .tip}

### Start earning

Until your site charges for anything, the dashboard also shows a **Start earning from your marketplace** card. Its
**Make money** button opens the guided setup; see [Make money](/make-money/). The card goes away once you charge for
something, or once you tell the setup you don't want to charge yet.

### Key numbers

Four cards compare the selected period with the one before:

| Card | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Listing views** | Views of your listings, with the number of contacts to sellers underneath. If view counting is switched off, this card shows **Paid orders** instead. |
| **New users** | Members who signed up and are active. |
| **New listings** | Listings published, and how many are waiting for review. |
| **Revenue** | What members paid you (featured listings, memberships, posting fees…), and the number of paid orders. |

Choose **7 days**, **30 days** or **90 days** at the top right. Click a card to open the page behind it.

Below the cards you'll find a chart of the main number, an **Activity** feed (new listings, sign-ups and payments)
and your **Recent listings**.

### Needs your attention

When something is waiting for your team, such as listings to review, reports, claims, banners to approve or
security deposit claims to decide, the dashboard shows a **Needs your attention** card with a count for each and a
link to the page that deals with it. See [Needs your attention](/admin-support-tools/#needs-your-attention) for every
line.

## Search and jump anywhere (Ctrl K / ⌘ K)

Press **Ctrl K** (Windows, Linux) or **⌘ K** (Mac), or click **Search or jump to…** in the sidebar, to open the
command palette. Start typing and press **Enter** to go:

- **Actions**: **Post a new listing**, **Visit Your Website**, **Edit profile** and **Read the guides**.
- **Go to**: every page in the sidebar, including sub-pages such as *Listings › Categories* or *Settings › Payments*.

The search is forgiving: typing a few letters in order is enough, so `lcat` finds *Listings › Categories*. Use the
arrow keys to move through the results and **Esc** to close.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcuts work anywhere in the panel, except while you're typing in a field.

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| **Ctrl K** / **⌘ K** | Open or close the search palette. |
| **Esc** | Close the palette or the mobile menu. |
| **G** then **H** | Go to Home. |
| **G** then **L** | Go to All listings. |
| **G** then **U** | Go to Users. |
| **G** then **O** | Go to Orders. |
| **G** then **S** | Go to Settings › General. |
| **G** then **P** | Go to Pages. |
| **N** | Post a new listing (on the Home page). |
| **Ctrl S** / **⌘ S** | Save a settings page that has unsaved changes. |

Press **G**, let go, then press the second key within a second or so.

Inside a text editor, **Ctrl K** keeps its usual job of inserting a link, so the palette won't open from there.
{: .note}

## Saving settings

On settings pages, a bar appears at the bottom as soon as you change something: *Unsaved changes*,
with **Discard** and **Save changes**. If you try to leave the page before saving, your browser asks you to confirm.

## The admin panel and the member area

Your marketplace has two back offices:

- **The admin panel** (this guide) is for you and your team: administrators, moderators and translators.
- **The member area** is what every member, including you, uses for their own things: **My Listings**,
  **My Favorites**, **Messages**, payments and their profile. It uses your site's design rather than the admin
  look. See [Your profile](/how-to-edit-your-profile/).

When a member who isn't on your team opens `/oc-panel`, they're taken to their own **My Listings** page instead.
