The admin panel is where you run your marketplace: listings, members, payments, pages, design and settings. It
lives at `/oc-panel` on your site's address (for example `yourname.yclas.com/oc-panel`), and opens when you sign in
as an administrator.

This guide shows what's where, so you can find any setting in a few seconds.

## The layout

The panel has three parts:

- **The sidebar** on the left, with every section of the panel. On a phone it's hidden behind the menu button in the
  top-left corner.
- **The top bar**, with the name of the page you're on, your site's status, **Visit Your Website** and your account
  menu.
- **The page itself**, in the middle.

At the top of the sidebar is a card with your site's name and address. Clicking it opens **My sites** in your Yclas
account, where you manage your plan and domain. Below it is the search box (**Search or jump to…**).

## The sidebar

Some links only appear once you switch the feature on, and moderators only see the sections their role allows.

| Link | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Your dashboard: setup guide, key numbers, activity and recent listings. |
| **Analytics** | Listing views, contacts, new listings, new users, paid orders and sales over time. See [Analytics](/useful-statistics-about-your-advertisements/). |
| **Support** ↗ | Opens your support tickets on yclas.com. See [Get help from Yclas support](/use-yclas-support-system/). |
| **Listings** | **All listings**, **Moderation** (when moderation is on, with a count of listings waiting), **Categories**, **Locations**, **Custom Fields** and listing **Settings**. |
| **Users** | Your members, their roles and status. See [Manage users](/manage-users/). |
| **Subscribers** | People subscribed to your emails and newsletter. |
| **Subscriptions** | Members' paid memberships (only when [membership plans](/membership-plans/) are on). |
| **Orders** | Everything members have paid for. See [Orders and transactions](/how-to-manage-orders/). |
| **Transaction** | eWallet movements (only when the [eWallet](/ewallet/) is on). |
| **Reviews**, **Forum** | Shown when [reviews](/review-system-works/) or the [forum](/add-forums-section/) are switched on. |
| **Pages** | About, Terms, Privacy and your other pages. See [Pages](/how_to_add_pages/). |
| **Blog**, **FAQ** | Shown when the [blog](/how-to-create-a-blog/) or [FAQ](/create-frequent-asked-questions-faq/) is on. |
| **Settings** | **General**, **Payments**, **Coupons**, **Translations** and **User Custom Fields**. |
| **Media** | Photo sizes, watermark and image settings. |
| **Email** | How your site sends email, and the email templates. |
| **Design** | **Themes**, **Theme Options**, **Widgets**, **Menu** and **Custom CSS** (plus **Page builder** on sites where it's switched on). |
| **Addons** | Optional features you can switch on. See [Add-ons](/addons/). |
| **Integrations** | Connections to other services, such as Google, social networks and payment providers. |
| **Tools** | Imports, sitemap, cache and scheduled jobs. See [The Tools page](/tools-overview/). |
| **Domain & SSL** ↗ | Opens the page in your Yclas account where you connect your own domain. See [Connect your own domain](/custom-domain/). |

Links marked ↗ open yclas.com in a new tab.

## The top bar

From left to right:

- **The page title**, after your site's name.
- **Trial countdown** — during the free trial, a pill such as *9 days left · Choose a plan* (on a phone; on a larger
  screen the countdown sits at the bottom of the sidebar).
- **Search the guides** — searches this Help Centre without leaving the panel.
- **Site live** or **Maintenance mode** — whether visitors can see your site. See
  [Maintenance mode](/how-to-activate-maintenance-mode/).
- **Visit Your Website** — opens your site in a new tab.
- **Your picture** — opens the account menu: **Edit profile**, **Support**, **Documentation**, **Manage Websites**
  (your sites on yclas.com) and **Logout**.

## The home dashboard

**Home** gives you a picture of your marketplace at a glance.

### The setup guide

For the first 45 days of a new site, the top of the dashboard shows a setup guide with the three steps that matter
most:

1. **Create your categories** — replace the starter category with the ones your members will browse.
2. **Add your logo** — it shows in the header and in your emails.
3. **Post your first 5 listings** — the counter shows how many you have (example listings created at sign-up don't
   count).
{: .steps}

A ring shows how ready your site is, and the button always takes you to the next step. Next to the listing step
there's also **Import from CSV** if you already have listings in a spreadsheet; see
[Import listings from CSV](/how-to-import-ads/).

The guide disappears once all three steps are done. To hide it earlier, click the **×** in its corner (**Hide the
setup guide**); it stays hidden in that browser.

### Key numbers

Four cards compare the selected period with the one before:

| Card | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Listing views** | Views of your listings, with the number of contacts to sellers underneath. If view counting is switched off, this card shows **Paid orders** instead. |
| **New users** | Members who signed up and are active. |
| **New listings** | Listings published, and how many are waiting for review. |
| **Revenue** | What members paid you (featured listings, memberships, posting fees…), and the number of paid orders. |

Choose **7 days**, **30 days** or **90 days** at the top right. Click a card to open the page behind it.

Below the cards you'll find a chart of the main number, an **Activity** feed (new listings, sign-ups and payments)
and your **Recent listings**.

## Search and jump anywhere (Ctrl K / ⌘ K)

Press **Ctrl K** (Windows, Linux) or **⌘ K** (Mac), or click **Search or jump to…** in the sidebar, to open the
command palette. Start typing and press **Enter** to go:

- **Actions**: **Post a new listing**, **Visit Your Website**, **Edit profile** and **Read the guides**.
- **Go to**: every page in the sidebar, including sub-pages such as *Listings › Categories* or *Settings › Payments*.

The search is forgiving: typing a few letters in order is enough, so `lcat` finds *Listings › Categories*. Use the
arrow keys to move through the results and **Esc** to close.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcuts work anywhere in the panel, except while you're typing in a field.

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| **Ctrl K** / **⌘ K** | Open or close the search palette. |
| **Esc** | Close the palette or the mobile menu. |
| **G** then **H** | Go to Home. |
| **G** then **L** | Go to All listings. |
| **G** then **U** | Go to Users. |
| **G** then **O** | Go to Orders. |
| **G** then **S** | Go to Settings › General. |
| **G** then **P** | Go to Pages. |
| **N** | Post a new listing (on the Home page). |
| **Ctrl S** / **⌘ S** | Save a settings page that has unsaved changes. |

Press **G**, let go, then press the second key within a second or so.

Inside a text editor, **Ctrl K** keeps its usual job of inserting a link, so the palette won't open from there.
{: .note}

## Saving settings

On the redesigned settings pages, a bar appears at the bottom as soon as you change something: *Unsaved changes*,
with **Discard** and **Save changes**. If you try to leave the page before saving, your browser asks you to confirm.

## The admin panel and the member area

Your marketplace has two back offices:

- **The admin panel** (this guide) is for you and your team: administrators, moderators and translators.
- **The member area** is what every member, including you, uses for their own things: **My Listings**,
  **My Favorites**, **Messages**, payments and their profile. It uses your site's design rather than the admin
  look. See [Your profile](/how-to-edit-your-profile/).

When a member who isn't on your team opens `/oc-panel`, they're taken to their own **My Listings** page instead.
