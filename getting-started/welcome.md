Yclas lets you run your own classifieds site or marketplace without writing code or managing a server. You choose
a name, and a minute later you have a working site where people can sign up, post listings, contact each other and,
if you want, pay you for extras such as featured listings or memberships.

People use Yclas for local buy-and-sell boards, car and property listings, job boards, rental sites, business
directories and niche communities such as vintage guitars or farm machinery.

## How Yclas works

Yclas is a hosted service. We run the servers, keep the software up to date, back up your data and look after
security. You manage everything else from your browser:

- **Your marketplace** is the public site your members visit. It starts at an address like
  `yourname.yclas.com`, and you can [connect your own domain](/custom-domain/) whenever you're ready.
- **The admin panel** is where you run the site: listings, categories, users, payments, design and settings. Open
  it from any page of your site by signing in as the administrator. See [A tour of the admin panel](/admin-panel-tour/).
- **Your Yclas account** at [yclas.com](https://yclas.com/panel/site) is where you manage the business side: your
  plan and payments, your domain, support tickets and any other sites you run.

Every site starts with a 15-day free trial, no credit card needed. After that you pick a plan to keep it online.
See [Plans and billing](/plans-and-billing/).
