Creating a marketplace on Yclas takes about a minute. You answer a few short questions, and Yclas builds a site that
already fits your niche: the right language, currency and time zone, starter categories and, in most cases, an About
page, a homepage headline and a few example listings to show how things will look.

Every new site comes with a **15-day free trial**. You don't need a credit card to start, and nothing is charged
unless you choose a plan.

## Create your site

1. Go to [yclas.com](https://yclas.com/) and click the button to create your website (or open
   [yclas.com/create-website.html](https://yclas.com/create-website.html) directly).
2. **What do you want to build?** Choose **Classifieds** (people post listings and contact each other directly),
   **Marketplace** (buyers pay online and you earn a commission on each sale) or **Not sure yet**. You can change
   your mind later.
3. **What will people list?** Pick the closest fit, such as **Vehicles**, **Property**, **Jobs**, **Services**,
   **Items for sale**, **Things for rent**, **Business directory** or **Something else**. Yclas sets up categories
   to match.
4. **What's your niche?** Describe it in a few words, for example "vintage guitars and amps" or "used farm machinery
   in Kenya". The more specific you are, the better your starting site. This becomes your site's description.
5. **Where, and in which language?** Check the **Country** and **Site language** Yclas has filled in for you. The
   country sets your currency and time zone; the language is used for your site and its emails.
6. **Name your marketplace.** Type a name. Underneath you'll see the address it will get, such as
   `fretboardexchange.yclas.com`, and whether it's available. Use at least 5 letters or numbers.
7. **Save your marketplace.** Enter your email address, or click **Continue with Google**. If you already have a
   Yclas account, log in and Yclas finishes creating the site for you.
{: .steps}

Yclas then builds your site and opens the **Your marketplace is ready** page.

If the step-by-step sign-up doesn't load (it needs JavaScript), use the **Use the simple form instead** link. It
asks only for a name and your email; you can set the language, currency and categories yourself afterwards.
{: .note}

## What Yclas sets up for you

While the "ready" page is showing, Yclas works through your answers. Depending on your niche and language, it can:

| What | Where to change it later |
| --- | --- |
| Site name and description | **Settings › General** — see [General settings](/change-site-name-site-description/) |
| Site language, currency and time zone | **Settings › General** — see [Language and translations](/how-to-change-language/) and [Currency and price format](/how-to-currency-format/) |
| Email templates in your language | **Email** — see [Email templates](/automatic-emails-sent-to-users/) |
| Categories, with subcategories and icons | **Listings › Categories** — see [Categories](/how-to-add-categories/) |
| Extra listing fields for your niche (for cars: make, year, mileage…) | **Listings › Custom Fields** — see [Custom fields](/how-to-create-custom-fields/) |
| A homepage headline, photo and accent colour | **Design** — see [Themes](/how-to-change-theme/) |
| An About page | **Pages** — see [Pages](/how_to_add_pages/) |
| Example listings | **Listings › All listings** — see [Manage listings](/how-to-manage-advertisements/) |

On the ready page you can also **Pick a photo for your homepage** from a small selection, then click
**Use this photo**.

The example listings end with a sentence saying they are examples. They make your site look lived-in while you set
it up, but delete or replace them before you invite real members.
{: .tip}

## Your first login

When the setup is done, click **Open my admin** (or **Go to my admin**). You're signed straight into the admin
panel of your new site.

We also email you your login details. Keep that email: it has the address of your site and how to sign in. If you
don't see it, check your spam folder.

From now on you can reach your site's admin in two ways:

- Go to your site's address and sign in with your email address and password.
- Sign in at [yclas.com](https://yclas.com/panel/site), open **My sites** and click **Open admin** next to the site.

## What to do next

Your site is live as soon as it's created, at its `.yclas.com` address. The admin panel's home page shows a short
setup guide with the three things that make the biggest difference: your categories, your logo and your first five
listings. See [A tour of the admin panel](/admin-panel-tour/).

If you'd rather keep the site hidden while you work on it, switch on
[maintenance mode](/how-to-activate-maintenance-mode/).

## During and after the trial

The admin panel's sidebar shows how many days of your trial are left, with a **Choose a plan** link. When you're
ready, pick a plan to keep the site online. Everything you set up during the trial stays as it is.

If the trial ends without a plan, the site is paused. You can come back and choose a plan within 30 days without
losing anything. See [Plans and billing](/plans-and-billing/).

## Creating another site

You can run several marketplaces from one Yclas account. Sign in at yclas.com, open **My sites** and click
**New website**. See [My sites](/my-sites/).
