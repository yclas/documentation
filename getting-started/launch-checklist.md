A marketplace only gets one first impression. Before you tell people about your site, run through this list. Most
steps take a few minutes, and together they make the difference between a site that looks like a test and one that
people trust with their listings.

You can work through the list with your site hidden: switch on [maintenance mode](/how-to-activate-maintenance-mode/)
and only you and your team can see it. Switch it off when you've ticked everything.
{: .tip}

## 1. Name, description and region

In **Settings › General**, check your site name, its description (search engines show it under your name), your
time zone, date format and currency. If you created the site with the sign-up questions, most of this is already
filled in.

See [General settings](/change-site-name-site-description/) and [Currency and price format](/how-to-currency-format/).

## 2. Categories (and locations)

Categories are how members browse and how they file their listings. Replace the starter categories with your own:
a handful of clear top-level categories works better than dozens of empty ones. If your listings depend on where
things are, add locations too.

See [Categories](/how-to-add-categories/) and [Locations](/how-to-add-locations/).

## 3. Listing form

Decide what a listing needs: which built-in fields to show (price, phone, address, website…), any extra fields for
your niche (make and mileage for cars, bedrooms for property), how many photos, and how long listings stay up.

See [Listing page and form fields](/how-to-manage-advertisement-fields/), [Custom fields](/how-to-create-custom-fields/)
and [Publishing options](/how-to-configure-publish-options/).

## 4. Who can post, and moderation

Choose whether new listings go live straight away or wait for your approval, and whether people must register before
posting. On a new site, approving listings yourself is a good way to keep spam out.

See [Moderation](/how-ads-moderation-works/) and [Fight spam](/how-to-avoid-spam-in-my-site/).

## 5. Look and feel

Pick a theme, add your logo and favicon, set your colours and check the home page on a phone as well as a computer.

See [Themes](/how-to-change-theme/), [Theme options](/theme-options/) and [Logo and favicon](/add-logo-classifieds-website/).

## 6. Pages: About, Terms and Privacy

Members look for who runs a site and what the rules are. Write a short About page, your terms of use and a privacy
policy, and add a contact page so people can reach you.

See [Pages](/how_to_add_pages/), [Contact page](/how-to-add-text-contact-page/) and
[Cookie consent and privacy](/cookie-consent/).

## 7. Emails

Your site emails members when they sign up, post, get a message or pay. Check the sender name and address in
**Email**, send yourself a test (sign up with a second email address) and read the main templates in your language.

See [Email settings](/general-email-configuration/), [Email templates](/automatic-emails-sent-to-users/) and
[Make sure your emails arrive](/emails-go-to-spam/).

## 8. Payments (if you charge)

If you'll charge for featured listings, posting in certain categories or memberships, connect a payment gateway and
make one test payment.

See [Take payments on your site](/setup-payment-gateways/), [Featured listings and promotions](/how-to-create-featured-plan/)
and [Membership plans](/membership-plans/).

## 9. Your first listings

An empty marketplace puts people off; marketplaces that reach five listings are far more likely to take off. Delete
the example listings created at sign-up, then post at least five real ones yourself, or import them from a
spreadsheet. Post one as an ordinary member too, so you see the form your members will use.

See [How members post a listing](/how-members-post-listings/) and [Import listings from CSV](/how-to-import-ads/).

## 10. Your address and search engines

Connect your own domain if you have one (it's worth doing before you promote the site, so links and search results
use the final address). Then generate your sitemap and add your site to Google Search Console.

See [Connect your own domain](/custom-domain/), [Sitemap](/sitemap-classifieds-website/) and
[SEO for your marketplace](/seo-classifieds-website/).

## Launch day

1. Switch off maintenance mode in **Settings › General**.
2. Open your site in a private browser window, as a visitor would see it.
3. Sign up as a new member, post a listing and send a message to make sure every step works.
4. Tell people: see [Promote your marketplace](/promote-classifieds-website-free/).
