Everyone who signs up on your marketplace, including you, has a profile and a personal member area. This is where
members manage their own listings, favourites, messages and payments, and change their details and password.

As the owner you have two separate accounts, each with its own profile:

- **Your account on your marketplace** (this guide): the administrator account you use to sign in to your site and
  its admin panel. Members see its name and picture next to anything you post.
- **Your Yclas account** on yclas.com: used for your plan, billing and support tickets. See
  [Your Yclas account settings](/yclas-account-settings/).
{: .note}

## Open your profile

- From the admin panel: click your picture in the top-right corner and choose **Edit profile**, or press
  **Ctrl K** (**⌘ K** on a Mac) and choose **Edit profile**.
- From your site: sign in, open the account menu in the header and choose **Edit profile**.

Your profile opens in your site's own design, not in the admin panel, because it's the same page every member uses.

The labels below are the ones used by the Nova theme, the default for new sites. Other themes show the same options
with a slightly different layout.
{: .note}

## Change your details

The **Edit Profile** section has:

| Field | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Shown on your listings and your public profile. |
| **Email** | The address you sign in with and where your site's emails go. |
| **Phone** (or **Mobile phone number**) | Your phone number. It's called *Mobile phone number* when [two-step login by SMS](/2-step-sms-authentication/) is on, because the codes are sent there. |
| **Location** | Shown when your site uses [locations](/how-to-add-locations/). |
| **Address** | Your address. When maps are on, **Locate me** fills it in from your device and a map is shown on your public profile. |
| **Description** | A few words about you, shown on your public profile. |
| Extra fields | Any [user custom fields](/users-custom-fields/) the site has, such as a company name or **Whatsapp Number**. |
| **Receive digest emails** | How often to get a summary of new listings: **Never**, **Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly** (only when digests are on). |
| **Subscribed to emails** | Whether you receive the site's newsletters. |

Click **Update** to save.

## Change your profile picture

In **Profile pictures**, click **Select** to choose an image, then **Upload**. Square images work best (400 × 400
pixels). If you upload several, use **Make Primary** on the one to show and **Delete** to remove the others.

## Change your password

In **Change password**, type the new password in **New password** and again in **Repeat password**, then click
**Update**.

Forgotten your password? Use the "forgot password" link on your site's login page and we'll email you a link to
choose a new one.
{: .tip}

## Other sections

Depending on what your site has switched on, the profile page also has:

| Section | Shown when | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **2 Step Authentication** | [Two-step login](/2-step-authentication/) is on | Ask for a code from an authenticator app at each login. We recommend it for administrators. |
| **Stripe Connect** | [Stripe Connect](/stripe/) is on | Connect a Stripe account to get paid for sales. |
| **Escrow Pay** | [Escrow payments](/escrow-pay/) are on | Connect an Escrow.com account. |
| **Instagram Connect** | The Instagram integration is on | Link an Instagram account. |
| **Subscription** | [Membership plans](/membership-plans/) are on | The member's current plan. |

## The member area

The menu in the member area (and in the account menu in the site's header) gives every member:

| Link | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **My Listings** | Their listings, with **Edit**, mark as sold, deactivate or activate, promote and **Stats**. See [How members post a listing](/how-members-post-listings/). |
| **My Favorites** | Listings they've saved. See [Favourites](/add-chosen-ads-favourites/). |
| **Messages** | Conversations with other members (when messaging is on). See [Messaging between members](/how-to-use-messaging-system/). |
| **My Payments** | Everything they've paid for on your site (shown once they've made a payment). |
| **My Sales** | What they've sold through checkout (when PayPal for sellers or Stripe Connect is on). |
| **Transactions** | Their eWallet balance and movements (when the [eWallet](/ewallet/) is on). |
| **Subscriptions** | Email alerts they've set up for new listings in a category or location. |
| **Edit profile** | The page described above. |
| **Public profile** | How other people see them: name, picture, description and their listings. |
| **Logout** | Signs out. |

The account menu also has a **Panel** link. For you and your team it opens the admin panel; for ordinary members it
opens **My Listings**.

Change your email address carefully: it's also the address you sign in with. If you're the only administrator and
lose access to that mailbox, you can't reset your password yourself.
{: .warning}
