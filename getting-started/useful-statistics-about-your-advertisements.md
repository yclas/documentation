The **Analytics** page shows how your marketplace is growing: how often listings are viewed, how many people contact
sellers, how many listings and members you're gaining and what you're earning. Every number is compared with the
period just before, so you can see at a glance whether things are going up or down.

Open it from **Analytics** in the admin panel sidebar.

## What you see

Each card shows a total for the period, a small chart by day and the change against the previous period of the same
length (for example *Increased by 12%*).

| Card | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Listing views** | How many times listings were opened. Search engine bots are not counted. |
| **Contacts** | Messages sent to sellers through the contact form on a listing. |
| **Listings** | Listings published in the period that are still published today (not sold, deactivated or waiting for review). |
| **Users** | Members who signed up and whose account is active. |
| **Paid Orders** | Orders paid to you: featured listings, bumps to the top, posting fees, memberships and so on. |
| **Sales** | The money from those paid orders. |

**Paid Orders** and **Sales** count what members pay *you*. When members sell to each other through checkout or
escrow, those sales aren't included here; you'll find them under [Orders](/how-to-manage-orders/).
{: .note}

## Choose the period

At the top right, pick **7 days**, **30 days**, **3 months**, **6 months**, **12 months** or **All time** (since your
site was created). For any other range, type or pick the **From** and **To** dates; the page updates as soon as you
change one.

The line under the title says which dates you're looking at, for example *1 Sep 2026 to 30 Sep 2026, compared with
the 30 days before*.

## See one number in detail

Click **Details** on a card to open that number on its own, with a larger chart and the same period controls. Below
the chart, a table compares the last month, 3 months, 6 months and year with the periods before them, so you can
spot longer trends.

## Turn view counting on

Listing views and contacts are only recorded while view counting is switched on. If it's off, the page tells you:
*Listing views are only counted while view counting is on.*

1. Go to **Listings › Settings**.
2. Switch on **Count Visits Listings**.
3. Click **Save changes**.
{: .steps}

Counting starts from that moment; views from before aren't recovered. The same setting also shows the number of
views on each listing. See [Listing page and form fields](/how-to-manage-advertisement-fields/).

## Stats for a single listing

Members, including you, can see the numbers for each of their own listings. In the member area, open
**My Listings**, open the menu (···) next to a listing and choose **Stats**. It shows that listing's views and
contacts over time. As an administrator you can open the stats of any member's listing.

This option is only there while view counting is on.

## The Home dashboard

The admin panel's **Home** page shows a shorter version of the same numbers (listing views, new users, new listings
and revenue) for the last 7, 30 or 90 days. Click any card there to go to the page behind it. See
[A tour of the admin panel](/admin-panel-tour/).

## Want more detail?

Analytics counts what happens to listings. For full website analytics (where visitors come from, which pages they
read, which devices they use), connect Google Analytics.

See [Google Analytics](/google-analytics/) and [Tracking codes and analytics pixels](/how-to-add-tracking-codes/).
