<div class="summary" markdown="1">
- Every solution on the **Solutions** page has a launch checklist: vehicles, real estate, equipment, jobs, business
  directory, rentals and bookings, community, and marketplace selling.
- Each checklist asks one question about how your site works, then lists the tools that are ready but switched off,
  each with a **Turn on** button.
- Tips under the list come from your site's own numbers, such as requests waiting for an answer.
- Open them from the **Launch checklist** card on your dashboard, or from **Solutions** in the sidebar.
</div>

A launch checklist gathers, on one page, the tools made for your kind of site, so you don't have to look for them in
**Settings** and **Add-ons**. Nothing changes until you click a button, and you can turn any tool off again from the
same page.

## Open a checklist

There are three ways in:

- **Your dashboard.** When a solution is on, the dashboard shows its **Launch checklist** card with how many of its
  tools are on. Click **Open the checklist**.
- **Solutions**, in the sidebar under **Grow**. Each solution that's on has a **Launch checklist** card. See
  [Solutions](/admin-panel-tour/#solutions).
- **Tabs.** When more than one solution is on, for example a community that also takes bookings, the checklist page
  has a tab for each one.

The checklists for vehicles and real estate work the same way; see
[Run a vehicle marketplace](/run-a-vehicle-marketplace/#the-launch-checklist) for the vehicles one.

## How a checklist works

Each checklist has three parts:

1. **One question** about how your site works, such as *Who posts on your site?*. Your answer decides which tools
   are listed: tools that don't fit your answer are left out, and they stay in **Settings**. You can change the
   answer at any time.
2. **The tools**, each with what it does, whether it's on, and a **Turn on** or **Turn off** button. Turning a tool on
   here is the same as turning it on from its own settings page.
3. **Tips** worked out from your site's numbers, each with a link to the page that helps.
{: .steps}

Some tools say why they can't be turned on yet:

- **Your plan** — the tool shows the plan that includes it and **See plans**.
- **Another tool first** — for example, *Turn on reviews first*.
- **A way to get paid** — tools that charge people, such as featured listings or paid bookings, need a payment
  method. See [Make money](/make-money/).
- **Set by Yclas support for your site** — our team set this one for you. [Contact support](/use-yclas-support-system/)
  to change it.

## Jobs

**Who posts on your site?** *Employers post jobs, and job seekers post their profile*, or *Only employers post jobs*.
With employers only, the job seekers' tools are left out.

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Employer accounts and pages** | Employers get a business account and their own page with all their open jobs. |
| **Job alerts** | Job seekers save a search and get an email when a matching job is posted. |
| **Featured jobs** | Employers pay to move a job to the top or feature it for some days. |
| **Charge per job** | Employers pay to post a job, at the price you set for each category. |
| **Leads inbox** | Every employer gets an inbox of the questions their jobs get, with follow-ups. |
| **Monthly employer email** | Each employer gets a monthly email with the views, applications and clicks their jobs got. |
| **Job board on other websites** | Employers and partners show your jobs on their own website. |
| **Recruit employers** | Your list of employers to bring in, with the message to send them. |

Tips include job offers with no application in two weeks, an employer with many live jobs who could have an employer
account, and a category still open to job seekers when you said only employers post.

## Business directory

**Who adds the businesses?** *Owners add their business, and you add others for them to claim*, *Business owners add
their own listing* or *You add the businesses, and owners claim them*. When owners add their own listing, the claim
tools are left out.

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Reviews** | Customers rate and review businesses, and the ratings show on each listing. |
| **Review checks** | Holds suspicious reviews, such as several from one device, for you to check before they show. |
| **Business accounts and pages** | Businesses get a business account and their own page with all their listings and locations. |
| **Claim before contact** | Listings nobody has claimed yet show no message button, so owners have a reason to claim theirs. |
| **Quote requests to nearby businesses** | A visitor describes a job once, and the nearest matching businesses get the request. |
| **Charge businesses for leads** | Businesses pay for each quote request, or for a pack of them. |
| **Several locations** | A business lists its other branches on its page, each with its own address and map. |
| **Monthly business email** | Each business gets a monthly email with the views, calls and messages its listing got. |
| **Recruit businesses** | Your list of businesses to bring in, with the message to send them. |

Tips include claims waiting for your approval, quote requests unanswered for three days, and listings without
opening hours. See [Directory mode](/directory-mode/).

## Rentals and bookings

**What do people rent on your site?** *A mix of things*, *Places to stay, by the night*, *Vehicles, equipment and
other things, by the day*, *Spaces and venues, by the hour* or *Services and appointments, by time slot*. For services
and appointments, security deposits are left out.

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Categories that take bookings** | Sellers in these categories can show a calendar and take booking requests on their listings. |
| **Instant booking, buffers and time zones** | Sellers can let people book without waiting for a reply, leave time between bookings and set each listing's time zone. |
| **Paid bookings** | People pay online when they book, the seller gets paid, and you can take a service fee. Needs [Marketplace selling](#marketplace-selling) set up first. |
| **Deposit online** | People pay part of the price online to confirm, and the rest to the seller. |
| **Security deposits** | Sellers hold a refundable deposit against damage, and you decide any claim. |
| **Reviews** | People rate and review what they rented. |
| **Monthly seller email** | Each seller gets a monthly email with the views, requests and clicks their listings got. |
| **Embed on your website** | Show your listings and their calendars on another website, such as your own. |

Tips include booking requests waiting over two days for the seller's answer, and accepted bookings that could have
been paid online. See [Paid bookings](/paid-bookings/).

## Community

**Who can post on your site?** *Members who are signed in*, *Only members you approve* or *Anyone, without an
account*. Your answer only picks which posting settings are on the list; nothing changes until you switch them, and a
tip tells you when your settings don't match your answer.

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Sign in to post** | Only people with an account can post, so you always know who posted what. |
| **Approve new members** | New accounts wait for you to approve them before they can sign in and post. |
| **Membership dues** | Members pay a yearly or monthly fee to belong, with a membership card and renewal reminders. |
| **Donations** | Anyone can give to your community from a donate button, with amounts you suggest. |
| **Event replies** | Members say they're coming to an event, with a limit on places and a reminder the day before. |
| **Event tickets** | Events sell tickets online, and each ticket has a code to check at the door. Needs event replies. |
| **Forum** | Members start topics and reply to each other. |
| **Referrals** | Members invite friends with their own link, and you choose the reward. |
| **Announcements** | Post news on your site and send it to members once. |
| **Embed on your website** | Show your events or listings on another website, such as your club's own. |

Dues, donations and tickets are also on **Make money**, under **More ways to earn**. They're not available on a site
that pays in credits ([eWallet](/ewallet/)).

## Marketplace selling

This solution is for a marketplace where buyers pay on your site and you take a commission. It's on once sellers can
be paid, and it's set up on **Commission on sales**.

**How do buyers pay sellers?** *Sellers connect their own Stripe account, and you take a commission*, or *You collect
every payment and pay sellers out*. The other way is left out of the list; it stays on **Commission on sales**.

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Buy now with Stripe Connect** | Buyers pay on your site, the money goes to the seller's own Stripe account and your commission comes to you. Needs your Stripe keys. |
| **Hold payment until delivery** | The money waits until the buyer confirms they got the item, or a dispute is settled. |
| **You collect and pay sellers out** | Buyers pay you, each seller's share goes to their balance, and they ask to withdraw it. See [Transactions and withdrawals](/transactions-and-withdrawals/). |
| **Your commission** | The share of each sale you keep, as a percentage and an optional fixed amount. |
| **Shopping cart** | Buyers put items from several sellers in one cart and pay once. |
| **Shipping profiles** | Sellers set their shipping prices once, by country and weight, and buyers see the cost before paying. Needs the shopping cart. |
| **Seller storefronts** | Each seller gets their own store page with their logo, banner and listings, and a stores directory. |
| **Returns** | Buyers ask to return an item within the days you set, and the seller accepts or refuses. |

The checklist also lists offers and reserved listings when they fit your site. The **Marketplace selling** group on
**Solutions** links to **Commission on sales**, **Orders** and, when you collect payments, **Withdrawals**.

## Equipment

**Do people buy or hire equipment on your site?** *Both for sale and for hire*, *For sale* or *For hire*. Tools for
the one you don't use are left out.

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Equipment fields** | Make, model, year, hours and serial number on the listing form, for the kinds of equipment you pick. |
| **Hire bookings** | People ask to hire a machine for some days on a calendar, and the seller accepts or declines. Pick the categories that are for hire. |
| **Dealer accounts and dealer pages** | Dealers get a business account and their own page with their stock. |
| **Listing documents** | Sellers attach service records, inspection reports and manuals as PDF files. |
| **Finance estimate** | Listings show "from … a month", worked out from the term and rate you set. |

Hire bookings and the finance estimate need a choice from you (the categories, or a term and a rate), so their rows
link to the settings and show whether they're set up.
